Hero Academy Director’s Guide

💥 MONDAY — Power of Reading

Theme: “Every hero needs the power to understand messages, decode clues, and comprehend information. Today you unlock the Power of Reading.”

Hero Assembly (10 min)

“Heroes! Welcome to Hero Academy. I’m Director [Name], and this week you’ll train to unlock 5 incredible powers. These powers will help you defeat the ultimate villain: the STAAR test. But here’s the secret — you already HAVE these powers inside you. This week, we activate them. Today’s power: READING. The ability to understand ANY text thrown at you. Let’s train!”

  • Hand out Hero ID Cards
  • Explain the Power-Up Ticket system (earn tickets for effort, use for small rewards)
  • Show the “Villain Board” — a poster listing the “villains” (skill areas) students will battle this week

Training Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Power Scan” — 5 reading questions on whiteboards. Quick warm-up.
  2. (20 min) “Hero Training: X-Ray Vision” — Teacher models reading strategies. “X-Ray Vision lets you see THROUGH the text to find hidden meaning. Watch me…” Demonstrate close reading, inference, finding evidence.
  3. (15 min) “Sidekick Practice” — Partners work through a passage. One reads, one finds evidence. “Every hero needs a sidekick!”

Power Recharge (5 min)

  • “Hero Pose” — students strike their best superhero pose for 30 seconds. Research shows power poses boost confidence! Play epic music.

Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)

“Villain: The Confusing Passage” — Reading Packet - STAAR-format reading questions - Frame: “This villain tries to confuse you with tricky texts. Use your Power of Reading to defeat it!” - Early finishers: “Bonus Villain” challenge

Differentiation: - Struggling: “Training Wheels” version — shorter text, guided questions - Advanced: “Elite Hero” — complex passages, analytical response - ELL: Visual vocabulary, bilingual glossary

Hero Debrief (10 min)

  • Review tricky questions: “The villain almost got us there, but here’s how to beat it…”
  • Award Power-Up Tickets (1-2 per student who completed the battle)
  • Hero Tip #1: Post on wall — “Always go back to the text for evidence!”

💥 TUESDAY — Power of Numbers

Theme: “Numbers are the language of the universe. Today you unlock the Power of Numbers — the ability to solve any mathematical challenge.”

Hero Assembly (10 min)

“Heroes, yesterday you unlocked the Power of Reading. Today: the Power of NUMBERS. Think about it — every superhero uses math. Calculating distances, timing their moves, measuring strength. Without numbers, heroes would crash into buildings. Today’s training: mathematical precision.”

Training Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Speed Force” — Timed computation. “How fast can you calculate?”
  2. (20 min) “Hero Training: Problem-Solving Vision” — Teacher models word problems step-by-step. “Every word problem is a villain in disguise. First, figure out what it’s REALLY asking…”
  3. (15 min) “Dynamic Duo” — Partner math: one solves, one checks. Switch.

Power Recharge (5 min)

  • “Super Speed” — 30 seconds of running in place, 30 seconds of silence. Reset.

Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)

“Villain: The Trick Question” — Math Packet - STAAR-format math problems - “This villain hides extra information in word problems to confuse you!”

Hero Debrief (10 min)

  • Hero Tip #2: “Always show your work — heroes don’t skip steps!”
  • Power-Up Tickets awarded

💥 WEDNESDAY — Power of Knowledge

Theme: “The smartest heroes know science and history. Knowledge is the most versatile superpower.”

Hero Assembly (10 min)

“What makes a hero truly powerful isn’t just strength — it’s KNOWLEDGE. Knowing how the world works. Knowing history so you don’t repeat mistakes. Today’s power: KNOWLEDGE. Science and social studies are your arsenal.”

Training Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Knowledge Scan” — Rapid-fire science/SS questions
  2. (20 min) “Hero Stations” — 4 stations around the room, each a different “power source” (science topic or SS era). Groups rotate, 5 min each.
  3. (15 min) “Villain Lineup” — Match vocabulary terms to definitions in a timed relay. “Identify the villain before it attacks!”

Power Recharge (5 min)

  • “Invisibility” — Everyone closes their eyes for 60 seconds of complete silence. “Heroes need to be able to focus in any situation.”

Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)

“Villain: The Unknown” — Science/SS Packet - Mixed science and social studies questions - Midweek energy: keep it shorter (10-12 questions), leave time for a review game

(15 min) BONUS: “Villain Battle Royale” Review Game - Quick whole-class game (see ACTIVITIES.md — “Hero vs. Villain Showdown”)

Hero Debrief (10 min)

  • Hero Tip #3: “When you don’t know an answer, eliminate what you DO know is wrong!”
  • Power-Up Tickets + midweek treat

💥 THURSDAY — Power of Focus

Theme: “The greatest power a hero has is FOCUS. The ability to concentrate under pressure.”

Hero Assembly (10 min)

“Today is the most important training day. Today you train your FOCUS. Any hero can be strong. Any hero can be fast. But the hero who can stay FOCUSED when things get tough? That’s the one who wins. Today we simulate the real battle. Test conditions. Show me your Power of Focus.”

Training Session 1: Simulation (45 min)

“Ultimate Villain: The STAAR Test” — Mini Practice Test - 20-30 mixed questions, test-like conditions - Silent, independent, timed - “This is the ultimate villain. You’ve trained for this.”

Power Recharge (5 min)

  • Quiet stretch.

Training Session 2: Analysis (40 min)

  1. (15 min) Review answers
  2. (10 min) Self-assessment — students identify their strongest and weakest “powers”
  3. (15 min) Small group “power boosts” — targeted review by need

Hero Debrief (10 min)

  • Hero Tip #4: “Skip the hard questions and come back. A smart hero picks their battles.”
  • Power-Up Tickets

💥 FRIDAY — Power of Confidence

Theme: “Your final power — and the most important one. CONFIDENCE. Believing in yourself.”

Hero Assembly (10 min)

“Heroes. You’ve unlocked Reading, Numbers, Knowledge, and Focus. Today’s power is the one that ties them ALL together: CONFIDENCE. Confidence isn’t about knowing everything — it’s about trusting your training. You’ve TRAINED. You’re READY. Today we celebrate.”

Training Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (15 min) Review top strategies (Hero’s Handbook)
  2. (15 min) “Villain Trap Spotter” — identify common trick questions
  3. (15 min) Fun review game (see ACTIVITIES.md)

Power Recharge (5 min)

  • Hero pose + deep breathing. “Channel your power.”

Training Session 2: Celebration (40 min)

  1. (10 min) “Letter to My Hero Self” — students write a letter to themselves
  2. (10 min) Hero Graduation Ceremony — certificates awarded
  3. (10 min) Students design their own “Hero Emblem” (draw their personal superhero logo representing their best skill)
  4. (10 min) Celebration — music, treats, hero poses for photos

Final Debrief

  • Hero Tip #5: “The power was inside you all along. You ARE a hero. Now go prove it on STAAR day.”
  • All 5 Power-Ups = “STAAR Superhero” status 💥

With great power comes great STAAR scores. 💥

💥 STAAR Power — Themed Activities

10 superhero-themed activities with 3 review games.

🎮 REVIEW GAMES

1. Hero vs. Villain Showdown (Whole Class)

Type: Class game | Time: 20 min | Best for: Any subject

How to Play: - Project a question. Class is the “Hero.” The question is the “Villain.” - Students write answers on whiteboards. If the majority gets it right, the Hero wins. If not, the Villain wins. - Track on the board: Hero | Villain — tally marks for each - Play 10-15 rounds. “Can the heroes defeat more villains than not?” - Dramatic narration: “The villain attacks with… a MULTI-STEP WORD PROBLEM! Heroes, defend yourselves!”

Materials: Questions, whiteboards, markers, scoreboard

2. Power-Up Relay (Team Relay)

Type: Teams of 5-6 | Time: 15 min | Best for: Quick computation, vocabulary

How to Play: - Teams line up. First hero runs to the board, solves a question, runs back - Correct answer = earns a “power-up” (point) for the team - If wrong, next hero can attempt the same question - First team to collect 6 power-ups wins - “Speed AND accuracy — that’s what separates heroes from sidekicks!”

Materials: Question cards at the front, answer key, scoreboard

3. Villain Battle Cards (Partner Game)

Type: Partners | Time: 15-20 min | Best for: Vocabulary, facts, formulas

How to Play: - Create a deck of cards: front = question, back = answer - Also create “Villain Cards” (5-6 scattered in the deck) — these have trick questions or bonus challenges - Partners take turns drawing cards and answering - Correct = keep the card (1 point). Villain cards = must answer correctly for 3 points, wrong = lose 2 points - Most points after the deck is done wins

Materials: Card deck (index cards), answer key on backs

👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

4. Hero ID Card Creation

Type: Individual | Time: 15 min | Best for: Monday warm-up, community building

Instructions: Students complete their Hero ID cards: - Hero Name (they create a superhero alter-ego) - Superpower (their strongest academic skill) - Weakness (the skill they’re working on) - Catchphrase (a motivational phrase they’ll use on test day) - Self-portrait in hero costume

Materials: Hero ID template, colored pencils

5. Hero’s Handbook (Personal Study Guide)

Type: Individual | Time: 20 min | Best for: Synthesis

Instructions: Students create a small booklet (folded paper): - Page 1: “My Superpowers” (skills I’m confident in + strategies) - Page 2: “Villains I’ve Defeated” (skills I’ve mastered this week) - Page 3: “Training Still Needed” (areas to review) - Page 4: “My Game Plan for STAAR Day”

Materials: Paper, pencils

6. Villain Wanted Posters (Concept Review)

Type: Individual | Time: 15-20 min | Best for: Identifying common mistakes

Instructions: Students create “WANTED” posters for their worst test-taking villains: - Villain name: “The Rushing Robber” (not reading carefully) - Illustration - “Crimes:” what this villain makes you do wrong - “How to Defeat:” specific strategy - Post on a “Villain Wall” in the classroom

Materials: Paper, markers

👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

7. Sidekick Swap (Partner Quiz)

Type: Partner | Time: 20 min

Instructions: Each student writes 5 questions. Swap with partner. Answer each other’s questions. Grade together. Discuss any disagreements using evidence. “A good sidekick challenges you to be better!”

Materials: Paper, pencils

8. Hero Training Montage (Timed Partner Drills)

Type: Partner | Time: 15 min

Instructions: Play epic music. Partners take turns doing rapid-fire practice: - 2 min: Partner A quizzes Partner B (flashcards) - 2 min: Switch - 2 min: Both solve problems independently, compare - Repeat for 3 rounds - “Every hero training montage has a soundtrack!”

Materials: Flashcards or question strips, timer, epic music playlist

👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

9. Hero HQ Defense (Station Rotation)

Type: Groups of 4-5 | Time: 30 min | Best for: Mixed review

Stations: - Shield Station — Reading defense (comprehension questions) - Laser Station — Math precision (computation) - Mind Station — Science knowledge - Time Station — Timed challenges (speed round)

Groups rotate every 7 minutes. Record answers on a “Mission Log” sheet.

Materials: Station question sets, mission log sheets, timer

10. Avengers Assemble (Group Problem Solving)

Type: Groups of 4 | Time: 20 min

Instructions: Each group gets one complex, multi-part problem. Each member is assigned a “role”: - The Brain — Reads and interprets the problem - The Calculator — Handles all computation - The Checker — Verifies each step - The Reporter — Presents the solution to the class

All four must contribute. “A hero team is only as strong as its weakest link!”

Materials: Complex problems (1 per group), chart paper, markers

🎟️ Power-Up Tickets

Description: Small reward tickets earned throughout the week. Can be collected and redeemed for small rewards (teacher’s choice: homework pass, first in line, small prize, etc.)

Tip: Be generous. These cost nothing and motivate everything. Award 1-3 per student per day.
