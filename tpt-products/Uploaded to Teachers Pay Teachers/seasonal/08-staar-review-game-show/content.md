# Summer STAAR Review Game Show — Full Content

## Visual Design Notes
- **Color Palette:** Electric blue, neon purple, hot pink, gold, black backgrounds (game show aesthetic)
- **Style:** TV game show — spotlights, stage curtains, neon signs, confetti animations
- **Layout:** Classic Jeopardy grid on main board, individual question slides with dramatic reveals
- **Fonts:** Bold block letters for categories, clean readable font for questions
- **Animations:** Click to reveal answers, sound effect cues noted in teacher guide

---

## Google Slides Structure

### Main Board Slide
- 10 columns (5 Math + 5 RLA categories)
- 5 rows (100-500 points)
- Each cell is a clickable link to the question slide
- Answered questions can be "X'd out" by the teacher

### Question Slide Template
- Top: Category name + point value
- Middle: Question in large, readable text
- Bottom: "Click to reveal answer" button (linked to answer slide)
- Answer slide shows correct answer + TEKS standard

### Navigation
- Every question slide has a "Back to Board" button
- Daily Double slides have special animation
- Final Round slide has wager area

### MATH CATEGORY 1: Number Operations (3.4)

**100:** What is 347 + 258?
*Answer: 605* (TEKS 3.4A)

**200:** Sam has 524 stickers. He gives away 189. How many does he have left?
*Answer: 335* (TEKS 3.4A)

**300:** What is 7 × 6?
*Answer: 42* (TEKS 3.4D)

**400:** There are 8 bags with 9 marbles in each bag. How many marbles are there in all?
*Answer: 72* (TEKS 3.4D)

**500:** Maria has 3 boxes of crayons. Each box has 24 crayons. She gives 15 crayons to her friend. How many crayons does Maria have now?

*Answer: 1/3* (TEKS 3.3H)

### RLA CATEGORY 1: Reading Comprehension (3.7)

**100:** What is the MAIN IDEA of a passage?
*Answer: What the passage is mostly about* (TEKS 3.7A)

**200:** Read: "The dog wagged its tail, jumped up, and licked the boy's face." How does the dog feel?
*Answer: Happy/excited* (TEKS 3.6F)

**300:** What does it mean to SUMMARIZE a passage?
*Answer: To retell the most important parts in your own words* (TEKS 3.7D)

**400:** Read: "Lily practiced piano every day for a month. At the recital, she played her song perfectly." What can you INFER about Lily?
*Answer: Her practice paid off; she is dedicated/hardworking* (TEKS 3.6F)

**500:** What is the difference between a FICTION and NONFICTION text? Give an example of each.
*Answer: Fiction = made up (stories, fairy tales); Nonfiction = true/factual (biographies, articles)* (TEKS 3.8A-B)

### RLA CATEGORY 2: Vocabulary in Context (3.2)

**100:** What does "enormous" mean?
*Answer: Very big/huge* (TEKS 3.2B)

**200:** Read: "The car was ancient — it was rusted and falling apart." What does "ancient" mean here?
*Answer: Very old* (TEKS 3.2B)

**300:** What is a SYNONYM for "happy"?
*Answer: Joyful, glad, cheerful, etc.* (TEKS 3.2A)

**400:** What does the PREFIX "un-" mean in "unhappy"?
*Answer: Not (unhappy = not happy)* (TEKS 3.2C)

**500 (DAILY DOUBLE):** Read: "The scientist was perplexed by the strange results." What does "perplexed" mean? What CONTEXT CLUES helped you?
*Answer: Confused/puzzled. "Strange results" is a clue.* (TEKS 3.2B)

### RLA CATEGORY 3: Author's Purpose & Text Structure (3.9)

**100:** What are the 3 main purposes an author writes? (PIE)
*Answer: Persuade, Inform, Entertain* (TEKS 3.9A)

**200:** An article about how tornadoes form is written to _____.
*Answer: Inform* (TEKS 3.9A)

**300:** A story about a dragon who saves a village is written to _____.
*Answer: Entertain* (TEKS 3.10A)

**400:** What text structure is used when an author describes what happened first, next, and last?
*Answer: Chronological order / sequence* (TEKS 3.9D)

**500:** A letter asking the principal to extend recess is written to _____. Name TWO things the author might include to be convincing.
*Answer: Persuade. Reasons, facts, examples, emotional appeal, etc.* (TEKS 3.9A)

---

### RLA CATEGORY 4: Inference & Text Evidence (3.6F, 3.7C)

**100:** What does it mean to make an INFERENCE?
*Answer: To use clues from the text + what you already know to figure something out* (TEKS 3.6F)

**200:** Read: "Marco grabbed his umbrella and raincoat before heading out." What can you infer about the weather?
*Answer: It's raining or about to rain* (TEKS 3.6F)

**300:** What is TEXT EVIDENCE?
*Answer: Words, phrases, or details directly from the text that support your answer* (TEKS 3.7C)

**400:** Read: "The puppy whimpered and hid under the bed during the fireworks." How does the puppy feel? What is your evidence?
*Answer: Scared. Evidence: whimpered, hid under the bed* (TEKS 3.6F, 3.7C)

**500:** Why is it important to use text evidence when answering questions about a passage?
*Answer: It proves your answer is supported by the text, not just a guess; it makes your answer stronger and more accurate* (TEKS 3.7C)

---

### RLA CATEGORY 5: Grammar & Writing (3.11)

**100:** Is this a COMPLETE sentence? "Running through the park."
*Answer: No — it's a fragment (missing a subject)* (TEKS 3.11D)

**200:** What punctuation goes at the end of a question?
*Answer: Question mark (?)* (TEKS 3.11D)

**300:** Fix this sentence: "me and him went to the store"
*Answer: "He and I went to the store."* (TEKS 3.11D)

**400:** Combine these two sentences using "because": "Sarah was tired. She stayed up late."
*Answer: "Sarah was tired because she stayed up late."* (TEKS 3.11C)

**500:** Write a sentence using a SIMILE (comparison using "like" or "as").
*Answer: Varies — e.g., "The snow was as white as cotton."* (TEKS 3.10D)

## Teacher Game Guide

### Setup (5 min)
1. Open Google Slides in presentation mode
2. Divide class into 2-4 teams
3. Distribute answer recording sheets
4. Explain rules

### Rules
- Teams take turns choosing a category and point value
- All teams can attempt to answer (write on whiteboards or recording sheets)
- Choosing team answers first; if wrong, other teams can "steal"
- Daily Doubles: Only the choosing team can answer; they wager points
- Final Round: All teams wager and answer

### Tips
- Use a 30-second timer for each question
- Allow discussion within teams (builds collaboration)
- Play music during thinking time for energy
- Award bonus points for showing work on word problems
- Can split into 2 days (Math one day, RLA another)
