2. **Bessie Coleman** — First Black woman to earn a pilot's license (from Waxahachie, TX)
3. **Dr. Heman Marion Sweatt** — Civil rights hero who integrated UT Law School
4. **Congresswoman Eddie Bernice Johnson** — First Black person to lead the House Science Committee
5. **Matthew Gaines** — Born enslaved, became a Texas State Senator during Reconstruction
