# ☀️ 3rd Grade Morning Work — ELAR Bell Ringers (4 Weeks)

### Start every day with purpose — 5 minutes that build STAAR-ready skills! ✨

**20 days of print-and-go ELAR warm-ups** with a structured weekly rotation: Monday Grammar, Tuesday Vocabulary, Wednesday Reading, Thursday Writing, Fun Friday! Each day takes 5-10 minutes and spirals key 3rd grade TEKS all the way to STAAR.

---

## 📅 Weekly Structure:

### 🔴 MONDAY — Grammar & Mechanics
- Fix-it sentences (capitalization, punctuation, spelling)
- Parts of speech identification
- Subject-verb agreement
- Sentence combining practice
- **STAAR format:** "Which sentence is written correctly?"

### 🔵 TUESDAY — Vocabulary Builder
- Context clues practice (read the sentence, define the word)
- Prefix/suffix challenges
- Multiple-meaning words
- Synonym/antonym pairs
- **STAAR format:** "What does the word ___ mean in paragraph 2?"

### 🟢 WEDNESDAY — Reading Mini-Passage
- Short passage (150-200 words) + 3 comprehension questions
- Mix of fiction and nonfiction
- Text evidence practice ("Which sentence from the passage supports...")
- Inference, main idea, author's purpose rotation
- **STAAR format:** Multiple choice + constructed response

### 🟣 THURSDAY — Writing Prompt
- Narrative, opinion, and informational writing rotation
- Sentence starter provided for scaffolding
- 4-6 sentence response expected
- Self-editing checklist on each page
- **Builds toward:** STAAR written composition skills

### 🟡 FUN FRIDAY — Word Games & Puzzles
- Word searches with vocabulary words
- Riddles and brain teasers
- Figurative language fun
- "Would You Rather?" writing challenges
- Emoji story interpretation
