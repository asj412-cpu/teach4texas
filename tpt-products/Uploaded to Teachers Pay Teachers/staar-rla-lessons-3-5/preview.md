## Lesson 1: Story Detective 🔍

**Grade:** 3rd
**TEKS:** 3.6F (Make inferences and use evidence), 3.7C (Use text evidence to support response)
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Mystery passages, "Detective Notebook" sheets, highlighters, anchor chart paper, sticky notes

### Learning Objective
*I can make inferences about a story using clues from the text and explain my thinking with evidence.*

### STAAR Connection
Inference questions are the **most common** on STAAR RLA. STAAR 2.0 requires citing specific text evidence in constructed responses.

---

### Warm-Up (8 minutes): "What's the Story?"

Display this paragraph:

> *She pulled on her rubber boots and grabbed the big yellow umbrella from the hook by the door. Through the window, she could see the puddles forming on the sidewalk. "I hope the flowers are okay," she muttered, picking up the watering can and putting it back in the closet.*

**Think-Pair-Share:**
1. What's the weather like? (Raining — boots, umbrella, puddles)
2. Why did she put the watering can away? (Rain will water the flowers)
3. Where is she probably going? (Outside)

**Anchor chart:** **INFERENCE = Text Clue + What I Already Know**

---

### Main Activity (25 minutes): "Mystery Passages"

**Phase 1: Guided Practice (10 min)** — Read aloud Mystery Passage #1:

> *Marcus sat on the bench and watched the other kids run onto the field. He looked down at his ankle, wrapped tightly in a bandage. Coach Rivera walked over and handed him a clipboard and pencil. "I need you to keep track of everyone's time for the sprints," Coach said. Marcus forced a smile. "Sure, Coach."*

**Whole-class detective work:**

| Clue from the Text | What I Already Know | My Inference |
|---|---|---|
| "Watched the other kids run" | If you're watching, you're not playing | Marcus can't participate |
| "Ankle wrapped in bandage" | Bandages mean injury | Marcus hurt his ankle |
| "Forced a smile" | "Forced" means not natural | Marcus is disappointed but trying to be positive |

**Model constructed response:** "Marcus is probably feeling disappointed because he can't practice with his team. The text says he 'forced a smile,' which means it wasn't real. He also 'watched the other kids run' instead of joining them."

**Phase 2: Partner Detective Work (15 min)** — 2 additional mystery passages with Detective Notebooks and STAAR-format constructed response questions.
