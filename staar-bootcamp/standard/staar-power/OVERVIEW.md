# 💥 STAAR Power — Quick-Start Overview

## Welcome, Hero!

You've downloaded **STAAR Power** — a superhero-themed STAAR prep week. Your students are heroes-in-training, you're the Hero Academy Director, and STAAR is the ultimate villain they must defeat.

---

## 📦 What's in This Package

| File | What It Is |
|------|-----------|
| **LESSON-PLANS.md** | 5-day "Hero Training" lesson plans |
| **ACTIVITIES.md** | 10+ themed activities including villain battle review games |
| **DECORATIONS.md** | Classroom decoration guide (Hero HQ setup) + hallway banner |
| **STUDENT-MATERIALS.md** | Hero ID cards, power-up tickets, certificates |
| **PARENT-LETTER.md** | Family communication template |

---

## 🦸 The Theme at a Glance

- **Students are:** Heroes-in-training
- **Teacher is:** Hero Academy Director / Senior Hero
- **Each day is:** A training session to unlock a new "power"
- **Completing work earns:** Power-Up Tickets
- **STAAR test is:** The ultimate villain to defeat
- **Classroom becomes:** Hero Headquarters (Hero HQ)

## 📅 Week Overview

| Day | Training | Focus |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Monday | **Power of Reading** | ELA — unlock the power to understand any text |
| Tuesday | **Power of Numbers** | Math — calculating your way through any challenge |
| Wednesday | **Power of Knowledge** | Science/SS — knowing things others don't |
| Thursday | **Power of Focus** | Mixed practice under test conditions |
| Friday | **Power of Confidence** | Strategies, celebration, hero graduation |

---

## ⏰ Quick Setup

1. Print Hero ID cards and Power-Up tickets
2. Set up your door sign ("HERO HQ") and a few motivational posters
3. Read the 5-day lesson plans
4. Send the parent letter
5. Run the week — it's that simple!

*Standard theme = less decoration, same great content. Focus your energy on the activities and student engagement.*
