# 👻 Escape Room 4: The Haunted Grammar House
## Grades 3-5 (Multi-Level) | Mixed Grammar Review — PREMIUM
### TEKS: 3.11D, 4.11D, 5.11D (Editing Conventions)

---

## Design Overview
- **Color Palette:** Spooky purple (#6C3483), pumpkin orange (#E67E22), ghost white (#F8F9FA), midnight black (#1B1B2F), eerie green (#00FF88)
- **Font:** Creepster (headers), Nunito (body)
- **Characters:** Ghost Guide ("Boo-k Worm" — a friendly ghost with glasses and a book), Grammar Ghosts (one per room — must be "cleared" by fixing grammar)
- **Interactive Mechanic:** Fix grammar errors to "clear" ghosts. Wrong answer → ghost gives a spooky hint. THREE difficulty tracks (3rd, 4th, 5th grade).
- **Structure:** Choose-your-path format. After the intro, students select their grade level track. Each track has 5 rooms.

### 📌 SLIDE 5 — Room 1 Challenge (3rd Grade)
**Text:**
> ## 🟢 Room 1 Challenge: Fix the Capitals!
> 
> **Which sentence has CORRECT capitalization?**
> 
> **1.**
> - A) my family went to dallas, texas in july.

> - C) My Family went to dallas, texas in July.

> - B) Every Monday, Mrs. johnson reads to us.
> - C) Every Monday, Mrs. Johnson reads to us. ✅

> **3.**
> - A) I think that Spot is the best Dog in the world.
> - B) I think that spot is the best dog in the World.
> - C) I think that Spot is the best dog in the world. ✅
> 
> **4.**
> - A) We celebrate thanksgiving in november.

> - C) We Celebrate Thanksgiving In November.

### 📌 SLIDE 5B — Hint
> ## 👻 Capital Carl's Hint:
> "Capitalize proper nouns — specific NAMES of people, places, holidays, months, and days. Don't capitalize common nouns like 'dog' or 'family' unless they start a sentence!"

### 📌 SLIDE 36-37 — Challenge
**Text:**

> 
> **Choose the correctly written complex sentence.**
> 
> **1.**
> - A) When the bell rings we line up at the door.
> - B) When the bell rings, we line up at the door. ✅
> - C) When the bell rings. We line up at the door.
