## Writing Prompts

### Prompt 1: My Favorite Teacher Memory
Write about your favorite memory with your teacher this year. Describe what happened, how you felt, and why it's your favorite. Use specific details!

**Sentence starters:** "One of my favorite memories with my teacher is when..." / "I'll never forget the time..."

### Prompt 2: Why Teachers Matter
Write an opinion piece about why teachers are important. Give at least 3 reasons with examples. Think about how your teacher has helped you grow.

**Sentence starters:** "I believe teachers are important because..." / "Without teachers, the world would..."

### Prompt 3: A Day as My Teacher
Imagine you get to be the teacher for one day. Write a narrative about your day. What would you teach? How would you handle the class? What would you do differently? What would you keep the same?

### Prompt 4: Thank You Letter (Formal)
Write a formal thank-you letter to your teacher following proper letter format: date, greeting, body (3 paragraphs), closing, signature.

**Template:**
```
[Date]

Dear _____________,

[Paragraph 1: Why are you writing? Express gratitude.]

[Paragraph 2: Specific examples of things you appreciate.]

[Paragraph 3: How has this teacher impacted you?]

Sincerely,
[Your name]
```

### Prompt 5: If I Could Give My Teacher Any Gift
If you could give your teacher ANY gift in the world (money is no object!), what would you give them and why? It can be something you buy, something you make, or even something that doesn't exist yet!

## Collaborative Class Book: "Why We Love Our Teacher"

**Each student gets one page to contribute to a class book:**

**Page Template:**
- **My name:** _____________
