## 🚀 HALLWAY TRANSFORMATION: Space Station Corridor

### The Vision
Students walk down the hallway and feel like they're inside a space station. Black walls, twinkling lights, planet labels on classroom doors, a mission control banner overhead.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Black Out the Walls
- Cover bulletin boards and wall space with **black butcher paper** or **black plastic tablecloths** (dollar store)
- Doesn't need to be floor-to-ceiling — waist-height to above door frames is plenty
- Tape securely with painter's tape (won't damage walls)
- **Cost:** ~$8-12 for butcher paper roll, or ~$6 for 3-4 plastic tablecloths

#### 2. Stars & Galaxies
- Cut small stars from **white and silver cardstock** or use **metallic star stickers**
- Scatter across the black background — random clusters look more realistic
- Optional: Add a few **glow-in-the-dark stars** (dollar store packs)
- For galaxies: crumple white tissue paper into loose spirals and tape flat against the black
- **Cost:** ~$3-5 for stickers/cardstock

#### 3. Twinkle Lights (Optional but AMAZING)
- String **white fairy lights** (battery-operated) along the top edge of the black paper
- Dollar Tree and Walmart carry battery-operated string lights for $1-5
- Secure with clear tape or command strips
- **Cost:** ~$3-10 for 2-3 strings

#### 4. Planet Door Signs
- Each classroom door gets a **planet label** (printable — see below)
- Planet name + teacher name + subject: "Planet Rodriguez — Mathematics Division"
- Print on colored cardstock, laminate if possible, tape to doors
- **Cost:** ~$2 for cardstock (print at school)

#### 5. Main Entrance Banner
- Large banner across the hallway entrance: **"WELCOME TO STAAR COMMAND — AUTHORIZED CADETS ONLY"**
- Use a roll of butcher paper (white or black) and paint/marker the letters
- Or print individual letters on 8.5x11 paper and tape together
- Add silver/gold accents
- **Cost:** ~$0-3 (use existing supplies)

#### 6. Floor Details (Optional)
- Use painter's tape to create "runway" lines on the floor leading to classrooms
- Or tape down paper "footprints" (boot prints) leading the way
- Label: "Follow the path to your mission station"
- **Cost:** ~$3-5 for painter's tape

### Budget-Friendly Alternatives
- **Skip fairy lights** — stars on black paper still looks great
- **Use newspaper** painted black instead of butcher paper
- **Have students make stars** as an art activity the week before — free labor + they feel ownership
- **Borrow from other teachers** — someone has leftover butcher paper and fairy lights

## 🏠 CLASSROOM SETUP: STAAR Command Center

### The Vision
Students walk into a command center. The door has an official-looking sign. Inside, desks are arranged in "mission formation," walls have space-themed motivational posters, and there's a Mission Progress board.

### Door Sign
- **"STAAR COMMAND CENTER — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY"**
- Print on 11x17 (or tape two 8.5x11 together)
- Black background, white/silver text, add a star or rocket graphic
- Laminate for durability

### Desk Name Tags
- **"Cadet [Student Name]"** in a space-themed design
- Print on cardstock, fold into tent-style name tags
- Include the student's "crew" (table group) name: Alpha Crew, Bravo Crew, etc.

### Wall Decorations

#### Mission Progress Board
- Large poster or section of butcher paper
- Title: "MISSION PROGRESS — LAUNCH SEQUENCE"
- 5 columns (Mon–Fri), each labeled Mission 1–5
- Student names listed; add a star sticker next to their name for each completed mission
- By Friday, students with 5 stars are "Launch Ready"

#### Space Log Strategy Wall
- Dedicate a section of wall/board to "Space Log Entries"
- Each day, add the day's key strategy (written on a star cutout or strip of paper)
- By Friday, you have 5 strategies posted — a visual study guide

#### Motivational Posters
Print and display:
- "The sky is NOT the limit — you're going to STAAR and beyond!"
- "Failure is not an option." — NASA Mission Control (adapted)
- "Every expert was once a beginner cadet."
- "Houston, we DON'T have a problem — because we PREPARED."

### Room Arrangement Tips
- If possible, arrange desks in clusters (4-5 desks = a "crew station")
- Label each cluster: Alpha Crew, Bravo Crew, Charlie Crew, Delta Crew
- Teacher desk = "Mission Commander's Console"
- If you have a reading corner: rename it "The Observatory"

## 🤝 Who Can Help?

- **Student helpers** — Set up stars, cut out shapes, decorate (the Friday before, or a class period)
- **Parent volunteers** — Hallway transformation goes 3x faster with 2-3 adults
- **Grade-level team** — Split the hallway work. Each teacher decorates their own door; share hallway duties
- **Art teacher** — May have butcher paper, supplies, and willing student artists
- **Custodial staff** — Give them a heads up! Ask about any restrictions on taping things to walls
