# 🏝️ Grammar Island Survivor — Teacher Guide
## Escape Room 3 | 5th Grade | 30–45 Minutes

---

## 📋 Overview
Students are stranded on Grammar Island and must solve 5 grammar challenges to earn raft pieces and escape. Covers complex sentences, conjunctions, prepositional phrases, and irregular past tense verbs.

**TEKS:** 5.11D.vii (subordinating conjunctions), 5.11D.ii (past tense of irregular verbs)

---

## 🖥️ How to Assign in Google Classroom
1. Classwork → **+ Create** → **Assignment**
2. Attach Google Slides → **"Make a copy for each student"**
3. Instructions: *"You're stranded on Grammar Island! Solve 5 grammar challenges to build your escape raft. Record your coordinates on the Raft Tracker (Slide 3)."*

| Setup | Activity | Wrap-Up |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 5 min | 30–40 min | 5 min |

This escape room is slightly more challenging (5th grade) — allow full 45 minutes for struggling learners.

---

## 🎯 Differentiation

### Struggling Learners:
- Provide a conjunction reference chart (FANBOYS + subordinating list)
- Allow partner work
- Pre-teach irregular verb forms with a quick sort activity
- Skip Challenge 5 (mixed review) if needed — award the coordinate freely

### Advanced Learners:
- Write a "Survivor Journal" using all 4 grammar skills in a paragraph
- Create Challenge 6 with their own questions
- Identify MULTIPLE prepositional phrases per sentence in Challenge 3
- Time challenge: escape in under 20 minutes

### ELL Students:
- Irregular verbs are especially challenging for ELLs — provide a past tense verb chart
- Pre-teach prepositions with physical demonstrations (put the book ON, UNDER, BESIDE)
- Allow use of bilingual resources

---

## 🏫 Center vs. Whole Class

### Center:
- 1–2 rotation periods
- Pair struggling/advanced students strategically
- Provide printed conjunction/preposition reference at the center

### Whole Class:
- Work through Challenge 1 together as a model
- Release students for independent work on Challenges 2–5
- Debrief Challenge 5 together (mixed review generates great discussion)

---

## 🎮 Extension Activities
1. **Island Journal:** Write a 3-paragraph survival story using at least 5 subordinating conjunctions, 5 prepositional phrases, and 5 irregular past tense verbs
2. **Conjunction Sort:** Cut-and-sort activity separating coordinating from subordinating conjunctions
3. **Irregular Verb Memory Match:** Create cards matching present to past tense
4. **Prepositional Phrase Hunt:** Find 10 prepositional phrases in a chapter book

---

## 📊 Assessment
- Strong formative assessment for 5th grade grammar conventions
- Excellent STAAR prep for grammar/editing questions
- Use Challenge 5 (mixed review) scores to identify which skills need reteaching
