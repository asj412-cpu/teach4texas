**[Date]**

Dear Families,

Let's taco 'bout next week! 🌮 Our classroom is turning into a fiesta-themed STAAR prep experience called **"Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready!"** Your child will spend the week building their "recipe for STAAR success" through fun, food-themed review activities.

**When:** [Monday, Date] – [Friday, Date]

**What your child will experience:**
- 🌮 Daily review sessions themed as "cooking courses" (Appetizers, Main Course, etc.)
- 🌶️ Three difficulty levels: Mild, Medium, and Spicy challenges
- 📇 A "Recipe Card" tracking their progress through the week
- 🏆 A Friday fiesta celebration with "Taco Champion" certificates

**How to support your chef at home:**
1. **Good sleep** — Chefs need rest to cook their best!
2. **Ask about the menu** — "What was today's special?" "Did you try the Spicy level?"
3. **Review together** — Even 10 minutes of flashcard practice helps
4. **Healthy meals** — Good food fuels good thinking
5. **Stay positive** — "You've got all the right ingredients!"

**STAAR test day ([date]):** Good breakfast, arrive on time, and remind them: "You're a Taco Champion!"

Thank you for your support! 🌮
