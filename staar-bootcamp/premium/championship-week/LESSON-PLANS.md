## 🏈 MONDAY — Quarterfinals: Draft Day

**Theme:** "Every championship team starts with a draft. Today we assess our roster and build our teams."

**Focus:** Foundation skills assessment & team building

### Coach's Huddle (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Athletes! Welcome to Championship Week! I'm Coach [Name], and this week we're training for the biggest game of the year — STAAR. Every champion trains before game day. Michael Jordan didn't just show up and win — he practiced harder than anyone. This week, that's us. Today is Draft Day. We're building our teams, assessing our skills, and getting ready to compete. Let's go, athletes!"

**Activities:**
- Hand out **Player Jerseys** (name tags — see STUDENT-MATERIALS.md)
- Assign team names (let groups choose from options or make their own)
- Reveal the **Tournament Bracket** poster
- Quick "skills combine" — 5-question diagnostic to see where students are

### Practice Drill 1: Guided Review (45 min)

**ELA/Reading Focus:**
1. **(10 min) "Warm-Up Laps"** — 5 quick review questions on whiteboards. "Just like stretching before a game — gotta warm up those brains!"
2. **(20 min) "Film Study"** — Teacher models reading a STAAR passage like studying game film. "Watch how I break down this play..." Demonstrate annotation, evidence-finding, question analysis.
3. **(15 min) "Partner Drills"** — Pairs work through a short passage together. One reads, one annotates. Discuss answers. "A great teammate makes everyone better."

### Water Break (5 min)
- "Stretches & Sprints" — students stand, do 30 seconds of exercises (jumping jacks, arm circles, running in place), then sit. Quick physical reset.

### Practice Drill 2: Independent Practice (40 min)

**"Quarterfinal Playbook" Packet**
- STAAR-format questions covering today's focus area
- Frame: "This is your first game. Show Coach what you've got."
- Early finishers: "Bonus Play" challenge sheet

**Differentiation:**
- **Struggling:** "Rookie Edition" — shorter passages, vocabulary pre-taught, guided questions
- **Advanced:** "All-Star Challenge" — complex texts, analytical questions
- **ELL:** Bilingual glossary, simplified question stems

### Post-Game Wrap (10 min)
- Review key plays (most-missed questions)
- **Coach's Chalkboard:** Write the day's key strategy (like diagramming a play)
- Award **Monday MVP** — 1-2 students who showed exceptional effort
- Update bracket: "We won the quarterfinals! Moving on to semifinals!"

## 🏈 TUESDAY — Semifinals: Practice Drills

**Theme:** "Semifinals are when it gets real. Today we drill the fundamentals until they're automatic."

**Focus:** Mathematics (or your primary math-tested area)

### Coach's Huddle (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Athletes, yesterday we won the quarterfinals. Today's the semis — this is where pretenders get eliminated and real athletes shine. What separates good from great? PRACTICE. The fundamentals. Today we drill math until these skills are muscle memory. When you see a problem on STAAR, your brain should react automatically — like a point guard running a fast break. Let's drill!"

### Practice Drill 1 (45 min)

**Math Focus (adapt to grade):**
1. **(10 min) "Sprint Drill"** — Timed computation problems (2 min each set). "Quick feet, quick calculations!"
2. **(20 min) "Play Diagramming"** — Teacher models word problems step by step, drawing them out like diagramming a play on the chalkboard. Show work visually. "Here's the play. Step 1: read the defense (the question). Step 2: identify your options. Step 3: execute."
3. **(15 min) "Two-a-Days"** — Partners take turns being "coach" and "player." Coach reads a problem, player solves while thinking aloud. Coach checks. Switch.

### Water Break (5 min)
- "Timeout!" — Quick trivia game (non-academic sports trivia). Fun mental break.

### Practice Drill 2 (40 min)

**"Semifinal Playbook" Packet**
- 15-20 STAAR-format math problems
- Include mix: computation, word problems, griddable, multi-step
- "This is the semifinal. Leave it all on the field."

**Differentiation:**
- **Struggling:** Formula reference sheet included, worked examples at top, fewer problems
- **Advanced:** "Varsity-level" problems — multi-step, real-world application
- **ELL:** Simplified word problem language, key math vocabulary visuals

### Post-Game Wrap (10 min)
- Review 2-3 key problems
- **Coach's Chalkboard:** Day's strategy on the board
- Award **Tuesday MVP**
- Bracket update: "Semifinals — DONE. Championship round is Thursday."

## 🏈 WEDNESDAY — Halftime Show 🎉

**Theme:** "Every great game has a halftime show. Time to catch our breath, have some fun, and refocus for the second half."

**Focus:** Fun review games, mixed subjects, energy boost

### Coach's Huddle (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "We're at the halfway point, athletes. You've been grinding hard — two days of intense training done. Today is our Halftime Show. That doesn't mean we stop working — it means we train differently. Today is about GAMES, teamwork, and having fun while reviewing. Sometimes the best practice is play. Let's enjoy this!"

### Practice Drill 1: Halftime Games (45 min)

Run 2-3 of the following (see ACTIVITIES.md for full instructions):

1. **(15 min) "STAAR Bowl"** — Jeopardy-style review game. Categories by subject/topic. Teams compete, keep score on the class "scoreboard."

2. **(15 min) "Relay Race"** — Teams line up, relay-style. Each member runs to solve one problem. First team to finish all problems correctly wins.

3. **(15 min) "Trading Card Swap"** — Students create test-strategy trading cards, then trade with classmates to collect a full set (see ACTIVITIES.md).

### Water Break (5 min)
- Play pump-up music. Let students talk/socialize briefly. Real halftime vibes.

### Practice Drill 2: Mixed Review (40 min)

**"Halftime Report" Mixed Practice**
- Shorter packet than other days — 10-12 mixed-subject questions
- Frame: "Quick second-half tune-up. Stay sharp."
- Remaining time: **Study Hall** — students review their weak areas using flashcards, playbooks, or past packets

### Post-Game Wrap (10 min)
- **Motivational Moment:** Share a story of a sports comeback (28-3 Super Bowl, Astros 2017 WS, etc. — Texas teams get bonus points!)
- Award **Wednesday MVP**
- Bracket: "Halftime done. Second half starts tomorrow. Championship round!"
- Hand out midweek treats if desired

## 🏈 THURSDAY — Championship Round: Scrimmage

**Theme:** "This is it. The championship scrimmage. Game-like conditions. Everything you've trained for."

**Focus:** Full STAAR-format practice test (simulated conditions)

### Coach's Huddle (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Athletes. Today is the championship scrimmage. A scrimmage is a practice game under real conditions. I need you to treat this like the real thing. Game faces on. No talking. No distractions. Show me that you can perform when it matters. I've seen you train all week — I KNOW you can do this. Now prove it to yourselves."

### Practice Drill 1: Scrimmage (45 min)

**Championship Scrimmage Test**
- Mini STAAR-format test (20-30 mixed questions)
- Test conditions: silent, independent, timed
- "This is the championship. Play like a champion."

### Water Break (5 min)
- Quiet stretch. Maintain intensity.

### Practice Drill 2: Film Review (40 min)

**Post-Scrimmage Analysis:**
1. **(15 min)** Review answers as a class — "Let's watch the game film and see what we can improve."
2. **(10 min)** Students self-identify weak areas on an assessment sheet
3. **(15 min)** Small group "position coaching" — group students by need. Teacher and peer tutors provide targeted review.

### Post-Game Wrap (10 min)
- **Coach's Chalkboard:** Final strategies
- Award **Thursday MVP**
- Bracket: "Championship round COMPLETE. Tomorrow: PRE-GAME PREP."
- Serious moment: "Tomorrow we celebrate. But right now, I want you to know — I'm proud of every single one of you."

## 🏈 FRIDAY — Pre-Game Prep: Pep Rally 🎉

**Theme:** "Game day is almost here. Today we sharpen our strategies, build our confidence, and celebrate how far we've come."

**Focus:** Test-taking strategies, confidence building, celebration

### Coach's Huddle (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Athletes! This is it — the last day of training camp. You have worked SO hard. You ran drills. You studied film. You survived the championship scrimmage. Today is PRE-GAME PREP. We're going to review our game plan, get our minds right, and finish with a PEP RALLY. Because when you walk into that test, you're walking in as CHAMPIONS."

### Practice Drill 1: Strategy Session (45 min)

1. **(15 min) "Coach's Top 10 Game-Day Plays"** — Review the 10 most important test-taking strategies (see STAAR Wars LESSON-PLANS.md Friday for the list — same strategies, sports language)

2. **(15 min) "Scouting Report"** — Analyze common STAAR "trick plays" (misleading answer choices). Students practice identifying traps.

3. **(15 min) "Captain's Speech"** — Students write/share one thing they've learned and one piece of advice for their teammates. 2-3 volunteers share with the class.

### Water Break (5 min)

### Practice Drill 2: Pep Rally & Celebration (40 min)

1. **(10 min) "Letters to Myself"** — Students write a game-day letter, sealed in envelope for test day
2. **(10 min) "MVP Certificate Ceremony"** — Award **Championship Certificates** to every student. Call each name, handshake, photo.
3. **(10 min) "Team Cheer"** — Class creates a short STAAR cheer together. Practice it. It's silly. It's fun. They'll remember it on test day.
4. **(10 min) Celebration** — Pump-up music, treats, students sign each other's **"Championship Program"** page

### Post-Game Wrap — Final Whistle (10 min)
- All 5 MVPs earned = Champion status
- Final cheer together
- "When you sit down for that STAAR test, remember: you TRAINED for this. Champions don't hope to win — they PREPARE to win. And you've prepared. Now go win."
