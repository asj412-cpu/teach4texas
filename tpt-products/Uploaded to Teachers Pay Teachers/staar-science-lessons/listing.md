## 📦 5 Complete Lesson Plans:

### 🔓 Lesson 1: Ecosystem Escape Room (5th Grade)
**TEKS 5.12A, 5.12B — Organisms & Environments**
- 5-station escape room: Energy Flow, Food Webs, Nonliving Factors, Photosynthesis, Ecosystem Disruption
- Students solve puzzles to collect "codes" and escape!
- Food chains, food webs, and producer/consumer/decomposer relationships
- Critical thinking: predict what happens when ecosystems are disrupted

### 🧪 Lesson 2: Kitchen Chemistry (8th Grade)
**TEKS 8.5E, 8.5F — Chemical Reactions & Conservation of Mass**
- 4 hands-on labs using household items (baking soda, vinegar, yeast, steel wool)
- Balloon Blow-Up, Elephant Toothpaste, Rusty Steel Wool, Conservation of Mass Challenge
- Students identify evidence of chemical reactions
- Balancing equations and conservation of mass — made tangible!

### ☁️ Lesson 3: Weather Forecasters (5th Grade)
**TEKS 5.8A, 5.8B — Weather, Climate & Water Cycle**
- Students become meteorologists analyzing real weather data
- Interactive water cycle model building
- Weather vs. climate sorting activities
- Predict Friday's weather using data trends!

### 🏋️ Lesson 4: Force & Motion Olympics (8th Grade)
**TEKS 8.6A, 8.6B — Force, Motion & Energy**
- 5 Olympic events: Speed Trial, Ramp Race, Collision Course, Friction Challenge, Acceleration Calculator
- Hands-on labs with toy cars, ramps, and marbles
- Calculate speed, velocity, and acceleration from real data
- Unbalanced forces demonstrated through experiments

### 🚀 Lesson 5: Space Mission Planner (5th Grade)
**TEKS 5.9A, 5.9B — Earth & Space**
- Students plan "missions" to planets using fact cards
- Solar system properties, movements, and comparisons
- Day/night cycle demonstration with globe and flashlight
- 60-second "Mission Briefing" presentations
