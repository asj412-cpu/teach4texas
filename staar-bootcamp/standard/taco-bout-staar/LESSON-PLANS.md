# 🌮 Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready — 5-Day Lesson Plans

> **Head Chef's Menu — Serving up success one day at a time.**

## Daily Schedule Template

| Time Block | Duration | Activity |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Daily Specials | 10 min | Themed intro, today's "menu," motivation |
| Cooking Session 1 | 45 min | Guided review |

## 🌮 MONDAY — Appetizers

**Theme:** "Every great meal starts with appetizers. Today we set the table with our ELA foundations."

### Daily Specials (10 min)
> "Welcome to the STAAR Kitchen, chefs! I'm Head Chef [Name], and this week we're cooking up something special — a STAAR-ready feast! Today we start with appetizers. Appetizers are light, essential, and set the tone for the whole meal. Just like reading skills set the tone for the whole STAAR. Let's taco 'bout getting ready! 🌮"

- Hand out **Recipe Cards** (see STUDENT-MATERIALS.md)
- Post the **"Daily Specials" menu board** (list today's learning objectives as menu items)
- Explain difficulty levels: 🌶️ Mild (basic), 🌶️🌶️ Medium (on grade level), 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy (challenge)

### Cooking Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Taste Test" Warm-Up** — 5 quick questions. "Sample these appetizer questions to warm up your palate!"
2. **(20 min) "Recipe Demonstration"** — Teacher models reading strategies like a cooking show. "First, we ADD careful reading. Then we STIR in annotations. We FOLD in evidence from the text. And VOILÀ — a perfect answer!"
3. **(15 min) "Sous Chef Practice"** — Partners work through a passage together. "Every Head Chef needs a Sous Chef!"

### Snack Break (5 min)
- "Taco Dance" — play a fun Latin/fiesta song, students dance for 90 seconds. "Chefs need to move!"

### Cooking Session 2 (40 min)
**"Appetizer Sampler" — ELA Packet**
- STAAR-format reading questions
- Three difficulty levels clearly marked: 🌶️ 🌶️🌶️ 🌶️🌶️🌶️
- Students must complete Mild + Medium; Spicy is optional "Extra Credit Salsa"

**Differentiation:** Built into the 3-level system!
- Struggling students focus on 🌶️ Mild
- On-level students complete 🌶️🌶️ Medium
- Advanced students tackle 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy

### Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)
- Review key questions: "Let's clean up any confusion..."
- **Chef's Tip #1:** Post on board — "Always go back to the text. The recipe is IN the passage!"
- Stamp recipe cards: "Appetizer ingredient: READING ✓"

## 🌮 TUESDAY — Main Course

**Theme:** "The main course is the heart of any meal. Math is the main course of the STAAR."

### Daily Specials (10 min)
> "Chefs! Yesterday's appetizers were *chef's kiss* 👨‍🍳. Today: the MAIN COURSE. Math. This is the hearty, filling, most important part of the meal. You can't have a fiesta without the main course, and you can't ace the STAAR without math skills. Let's cook!"

### Cooking Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Ingredient Check"** — Quick computation warm-up. "Make sure your ingredients (basic skills) are fresh!"
2. **(20 min) "Cooking Demo"** — Teacher models word problems. "Just like following a recipe — read ALL the steps before you start. Don't skip ingredients!"
3. **(15 min) "Kitchen Partners"** — Partners solve problems, checking each other's "recipes" (work shown)

### Snack Break (5 min)
- "This or That: Food Edition" — Would you rather eat tacos or pizza? Stand on left or right side. Quick fun reset.

### Cooking Session 2 (40 min)
**"Main Course Platter" — Math Packet**
- Three difficulty levels: 🌶️ 🌶️🌶️ 🌶️🌶️🌶️
- "This is a BIG serving of math. Pace yourself — don't eat too fast!"

### Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)
- **Chef's Tip #2:** "Show ALL your work. A chef who skips steps gets a bad dish. A student who skips steps gets a wrong answer."
- Stamp recipe cards: "Main Course ingredient: MATH ✓"

## 🌮 WEDNESDAY — Nacho Average Review Day 🧀

**Theme:** "This is NACHO AVERAGE review day! Today we go beyond the basics with science and social studies."

### Daily Specials (10 min)
> "Chefs, this is NOT your average review day. This is NACHO Average Review Day! 🧀 Today we add the special flavors — science and social studies. These are the toppings that make the meal memorable. Plus, it's the middle of the week, so we're going to have some FUN while we review. Let's spice it up!"

### Cooking Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Salsa Sampler"** — Rapid-fire science/SS questions
2. **(20 min) "Food Truck Stations"** — 4 stations around the room, each a different "food truck" serving a different subject:
   - 🌮 Taco Truck (Reading review)
   - 🍕 Pizza Stand (Math review)
   - 🌶️ Salsa Station (Science)
   - 🧀 Nacho Bar (Social Studies)
   Groups rotate every 5 min.
3. **(15 min) "Recipe Swap"** — Students share their best study tips with a partner, then trade tips with a new partner. "Chefs share recipes!"

### Snack Break (5 min)
- Actual snack if possible! Even small crackers or a piece of candy. "Nacho Average snack break!"

### Cooking Session 2 (40 min)
**"Nacho Platter" — Mixed Review (shorter)**
- 10-12 mixed questions
- Leave time for the review game:

**(15 min) "Salsa Showdown" Review Game**
- Teams compete. Teacher asks question. First team to answer correctly "adds salsa" to their nacho plate (earns a point). Team with the most loaded nachos wins.

### Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)
- **Chef's Tip #3:** "When you don't know an answer, eliminate what doesn't taste right. Cross out the options that don't make sense!"
- Stamp recipe cards: "Special Ingredient: KNOWLEDGE ✓"
- Midweek treat! 🎉

## 🌮 THURSDAY — Taste Test

**Theme:** "Before any chef serves their dish, they do a taste test. Today we taste-test our STAAR skills."

### Daily Specials (10 min)
> "Chefs, every great chef does a taste test before serving. You don't send a dish to the table without trying it first. Today is your taste test — a full practice run. We're tasting EVERYTHING — all subjects, all skills. If something tastes off, we'll adjust the recipe before the big fiesta."

### Cooking Session 1 (45 min)
**"Taste Test" — Mini Practice Test**
- 20-30 mixed STAAR-format questions
- Test conditions: silent, independent, timed
- "Taste carefully. Don't rush."

### Snack Break (5 min)
- Quiet stretch.

### Cooking Session 2 (40 min)
1. **(15 min)** Review answers. "How did it taste? What needs more seasoning?"
2. **(10 min)** Self-assessment: students identify their "undercooked" areas
3. **(15 min)** Small group "recipe fix" — targeted review by need

### Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)
- **Chef's Tip #4:** "If a question is too tough, skip it and come back. Don't let one bad dish ruin the whole meal."
- Stamp recipe cards: "Quality Control: PRACTICE ✓"

---

## 🌮 FRIDAY — Fiesta Friday! 🎉

**Theme:** "The fiesta is here! Celebrate how far you've come. You're cooked to perfection!"

### Daily Specials (10 min)
> "CHEFS! It's FIESTA FRIDAY! You've cooked appetizers, the main course, nacho-level reviews, and passed the taste test. Today we celebrate! But first, a few final recipes to seal in the flavor. Then... WE PARTY."

### Cooking Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(15 min) "Chef's Secret Recipes"** — Top 10 test-taking strategies presented as cooking tips:
   - "Read the recipe twice before cooking" = Read the question twice
   - "Don't add ingredients that aren't in the recipe" = Don't add info that's not in the text
   - "Taste as you go" = Check your work as you go
2. **(15 min) "Mystery Ingredient Challenge"** — Fun activity: each table gets a "mystery" question type, must explain the strategy to solve it to the class
3. **(15 min) Review game of teacher's choice

### Snack Break (5 min)

### Cooking Session 2: FIESTA! (40 min)
1. **(10 min) "Recipe for Success Letter"** — Students write their personal "recipe" for test day
2. **(10 min) "Taco Champion" Certificate Ceremony** — Award certificates with flair!
3. **(10 min)** Students sign each other's recipe cards with encouraging messages
4. **(10 min)** Party! Music, treats (tacos if you're amazing, otherwise any snacks), celebration

### Final Kitchen Cleanup
- **Chef's Tip #5:** "You ARE the recipe for success. All the ingredients are inside you."
- All 5 recipe card stamps = "Master Chef" status
- "Let's taco 'bout how READY you are!" 🌮🎉
