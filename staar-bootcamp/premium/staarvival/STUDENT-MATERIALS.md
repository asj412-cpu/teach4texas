## 📓 Explorer Journal (Week-Long Booklet)

**Description:** A 5-page booklet (plus cover) that students write in daily. Their expedition diary.

**Cover:**
- Title: "EXPLORER JOURNAL"
- Subtitle: "The Official Field Notes of Explorer [Name]"
- "Expedition: STAAR-vival [Year]"
- "Team: [Table Group Terrain Name]"
- Illustration space: student draws themselves as an explorer

**Inside pages (one per day):**
- Header: "Day [#] — [Terrain Name] — [Date]"
- Small terrain illustration in corner
- Daily prompt (see LESSON-PLANS.md for specific prompts)
- 12-15 lined writing spaces
- "Survival Tip of the Day:" (blank — teacher writes one, students copy)
- Footer: "Explorer signature: ___________"

**Assembly:** Print double-sided, fold and staple. Or print single pages and have students keep in a folder.

## 🗺️ Survival Badge Card

**Description:** A card with 5 terrain-shaped badge slots. Students earn one badge per day.

**Design:**
- Size: 5" x 7" (print 2 per page)
- Title: "STAAR-VIVAL BADGE TRACKER"
- 5 badge slots arranged as a winding trail:
  1. 🌴 **Jungle** (leaf shape)
  2. 🏜️ **Desert** (cactus shape)
  3. ⛰️ **Mountain** (triangle/peak shape)
  4. 🌊 **Ocean** (wave shape)
  5. 🏔️ **Summit** (flag shape)
- Dotted trail connecting all 5
- "Survive all 5 terrains to become an ULTIMATE SURVIVOR"
- Student name at top

**Badge options:** Sticker in each slot, teacher stamp, or drawn checkmark.

## 📝 Daily Challenge Packets

**Format:**
- **Cover:** "TERRAIN: [Name] — SURVIVAL CHALLENGE"
  - Terrain illustration
  - "Explorer: _____ Team: _____ Date: _____"
  - "Objective:" themed description
  - "Difficulty: 🔥🔥🔥" (fire icons for difficulty level)
- **Content:** STAAR-format questions (teacher provides)
- **Back page:** "Field Notes" — 3 reflection questions:
  1. "Terrain conquered! My strongest skill today:"
  2. "Biggest obstacle I faced:"
  3. "My survival strategy for tomorrow:"

## 🗺️ Journey Map (Student Version)

**Description:** A personal, smaller version of the classroom journey map. Students track their own progress.

**Design:**
- 8.5x11 landscape
- Illustrated path through 5 terrains (Jungle → Desert → Mountains → Ocean → Summit)
- Each terrain has a checkbox: "☐ Survived!"
- Small facts/tips written along the path as "trail markers"
- "START" at bottom left, "SUMMIT" at top right
- Space to color in each terrain as it's completed

Students color in each section as they complete it. By Friday, the whole map is colored = "EXPEDITION COMPLETE."

## 🧭 Compass Rose Activity

**Description:** Wednesday activity — students create a compass rose organizing their knowledge by subject.

**Template:**
- Large compass rose taking up most of the page
- N = Reading/ELA, E = Math, S = Science, W = Social Studies
- Along each direction: 3-4 blank lines for students to write key concepts
- Center circle: "NAVIGATE YOUR LEARNING"
- Decorative border with terrain elements

---

## 📖 Survival Guide (Mini-Book)

**Description:** A one-page fold into an 8-page mini-booklet. Students create their own study guide organized by terrain.

**Pages:**
1. Cover: "Survival Guide — Explorer [Name]"
2. Jungle (Reading): Top strategies
3. Desert (Math): Key formulas/steps
4. Mountain (Science): Must-know facts
5. Ocean (Social Studies): Key events/concepts
6. Summit (Test Strategies): Game-day plan
7. "Emergency Procedures" (what to do when stuck)
8. Back cover: "I SURVIVED STAAR-VIVAL!"

## 📦 Survival Kit (Monday Handout)

**Description:** A ziplock bag with essential "supplies" — doubles as a fun first-day surprise.

**Contents:**
- 2 sharpened pencils ("fire starters")
- 1 eraser ("first aid")
- 1 small snack — granola bar, fruit snack, etc. ("rations")
- 1 small note: "Explorer — you have everything you need to survive this week. Use your tools wisely!"

**Label on bag:** "STAAR-VIVAL KIT — Explorer [Name]"

**Cost:** ~$0.25-0.50 per student if buying in bulk. Optional — pencils and erasers alone work fine.

## ✉️ Letter from the Summit (Friday)

**Template:**
- Header: "FROM THE SUMMIT OF STAAR MOUNTAIN"
- "Dear Future Me,"
- "I survived the jungle of reading by..."
- "I crossed the desert of math by..."
- "I climbed the mountain of science/social studies by..."
- "On STAAR day, I will remember..."
- "I am an Ultimate Survivor because..."
- "See you at the top! — Explorer [Name]"

Students seal in envelopes. Teacher returns on STAAR test day.
