# 🌮 Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready — Decoration Guide

> Simple, colorful, and fun. The fiesta vibe does half the work for you.

---

## 🏠 CLASSROOM: STAAR Kitchen / Fiesta HQ

### Door Decoration: Paper Plate Taco
- Take 2 large paper plates, fold in half, staple together at the bottom
- Stuff with colored tissue paper "ingredients": brown (meat), yellow (cheese), green (lettuce), red (tomato)
- Add a sign above: **"TACO 'BOUT STAAR READY!"**
- Takes 10 minutes. Kids LOVE it.
- **Cost:** $1-2 (paper plates + tissue paper)

### Daily Specials Menu Board
- Dedicate a whiteboard section or poster to the **"Daily Specials"**
- Each morning, write the day's learning objectives as menu items:
  - "Today's Special: Main Idea Tacos with a side of Inference Salsa"
  - "Chef's Recommendation: Show Your Work Burritos"
- Fun and functional — students actually read it!
- **Cost:** $0 (use existing whiteboard) or $1 for poster board

### Fiesta Banner
- String of colorful pennant flags across the front of the room
- Cut triangles from colored construction paper (or buy pre-made)
- Or use a string of small paper plates colored in bright fiesta colors
- **Cost:** $2-3 for construction paper + yarn

### Difficulty Level Signs
- Three signs posted prominently:
  - 🌶️ "MILD — I'm warming up"
  - 🌶️🌶️ "MEDIUM — I'm on fire"
  - 🌶️🌶️🌶️ "SPICY — I'm unstoppable!"
- Students self-select or teacher assigns difficulty levels for assignments
- **Cost:** $0 (print or hand-draw)

### Motivational Posters
- "Lettuce taco 'bout how STAAR-ready you are! 🌮"
- "This class is NACHO average class!"
- "You're one smart cookie! 🍪"
- "Don't be afraid of the STAAR — you've got the right ingredients!"
- Bright, colorful, pun-heavy

### Desk Name Tags
- **"Chef [Student Name]"**
- Small taco or chili pepper graphic
- "Specialty: [Student's best subject]"
- Print on bright cardstock

---

## 🏫 HALLWAY: Simple Banner

- **"TACO 'BOUT STAAR READY! 🌮"**
- Large letters on butcher paper or individually printed
- Add paper plate tacos or chili pepper cutouts around it
- Hang above your classroom door or across hallway section
- **Cost:** $0-3
