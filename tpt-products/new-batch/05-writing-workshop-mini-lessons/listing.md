# ✏️ Writing Workshop Mini-Lessons — 15 Complete Lessons for Grades 3-5

### Everything you need to teach writing across ALL three genres! 📝

**15 ready-to-teach mini-lessons** covering Narrative, Opinion, and Informational writing. Each lesson includes a mentor text excerpt, teacher script, graphic organizer, student checklist, writing prompt, peer feedback form, and scoring rubric. Just open and teach!

---

## 📦 NARRATIVE WRITING (5 Lessons)

### Lesson 1: "Show, Don't Tell" — Sensory Details
- **Mentor text:** Excerpt showing vivid sensory language
- **Activity:** Rewrite a "telling" paragraph with sensory details
- **Graphic organizer:** 5 Senses web for brainstorming
- **Writing prompt:** "Describe a place that makes you feel safe — make the reader FEEL it"

### Lesson 2: Hooking Your Reader — Strong Leads
- **Mentor text:** 4 different opening styles (question, action, dialogue, description)
- **Activity:** Write 4 different leads for the SAME story
- **Student sort:** Match leads to their type
- **Writing prompt:** "Write the opening of a story that starts mid-action"

### Lesson 3: Dialogue That Pops
- **Mentor text:** Dialogue with tags, action beats, and emotion
- **Activity:** Add dialogue to a silent scene
- **Graphic organizer:** Dialogue planning strip
- **Mini-assessment:** Fix the punctuation in 5 dialogue sentences

### Lesson 4: Building Tension — Problem & Rising Action
- **Mentor text:** Excerpt showing escalating conflict
- **Activity:** Plot mountain mapping + write your own rising action
- **Graphic organizer:** Story arc planner
- **Writing prompt:** "Your character is lost in an unfamiliar place. Build the tension."

### Lesson 5: Endings That Satisfy — Resolution & Reflection
- **Mentor text:** 3 ending styles (surprise, full-circle, lesson-learned)
- **Activity:** Write 3 different endings for the same story
- **Peer review:** "Did the ending make you feel something?"
- **Writing prompt:** Complete narrative using lessons 1-5 techniques

---

## 📦 OPINION WRITING (5 Lessons)

### Lesson 6: Stating Your Claim
- **Mentor text:** Strong vs. weak opinion statements
- **Activity:** Sort claims into "strong" and "needs work" + rewrite the weak ones
- **Graphic organizer:** Claim + 3 Reasons planner
- **Writing prompt:** "Should students have homework on weekends?"

### Lesson 7: Evidence That Convinces
- **Mentor text:** Excerpt using facts, examples, and expert quotes
- **Activity:** Match evidence to claims — which support is strongest?
- **Graphic organizer:** Evidence strength meter (weak → strong)
- **Writing prompt:** "Should your school change lunch options?" — support with evidence

### Lesson 8: Counterargument & Rebuttal
- **Mentor text:** "Some people say... however..."
- **Activity:** Think like the OTHER side — write counterarguments, then rebut them
- **Sentence frames:** "While some believe _____, the evidence shows _____"
- **Writing prompt:** "Should kids under 13 have cell phones?" — address the other side

### Lesson 9: Persuasive Transitions
- **Word bank:** Transition words organized by purpose (adding, contrasting, concluding)
- **Activity:** Rewrite a paragraph adding transitions — see how it transforms
- **Matching game:** Pair the transition to its purpose
- **Writing prompt:** Revise your lesson 6 or 7 piece with strong transitions

### Lesson 10: Call to Action — Powerful Conclusions
- **Mentor text:** Conclusions that MOVE the reader
- **Activity:** Write 3 conclusions: summary, call-to-action, and emotional appeal
- **Peer review:** "Would this conclusion make you ACT?"
- **Final prompt:** Complete opinion essay using lessons 6-10

---

## 📦 INFORMATIONAL WRITING (5 Lessons)

### Lesson 11: Researching & Organizing Information
- **Activity:** Mini-research using provided source texts (no internet needed!)
- **Graphic organizer:** KWL chart + note-taking template
- **Skill focus:** Paraphrasing vs. copying (plagiarism mini-lesson)
- **Writing prompt:** Choose a topic from 5 options, take notes from 2 provided sources

### Lesson 12: Topic Sentences & Subtopics
- **Mentor text:** Well-organized informational paragraph
- **Activity:** Sort sentences into main idea vs. details
- **Graphic organizer:** Hamburger paragraph planner (topic → details → conclusion)
- **Writing prompt:** Write an introductory paragraph for your research topic

### Lesson 13: Text Features That Help Readers
- **Activity:** Add headings, bold words, captions, and a glossary to a plain text
- **Creation station:** Design an infographic about your topic
- **Matching:** Text feature → its purpose
- **Writing prompt:** Add 3 text features to your informational piece

### Lesson 14: Domain-Specific Vocabulary
- **Activity:** Replace "regular" words with expert vocabulary
- **Word wall builder:** Create a mini-glossary for your topic
- **Graphic organizer:** Frayer model for 3 key terms
- **Writing prompt:** Revise your piece to sound like an EXPERT

### Lesson 15: Informational Conclusions — Why It Matters
- **Mentor text:** Conclusion that connects to the reader's life
- **Activity:** Write "So What?" conclusions for 3 different topics
- **Peer review:** "Did you learn something AND care about it?"
- **Final prompt:** Complete informational piece using lessons 11-15
