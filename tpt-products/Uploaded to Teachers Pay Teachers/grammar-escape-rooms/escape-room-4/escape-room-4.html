Grades 3-5 (Multi-Level) | Mixed Grammar Review — PREMIUM

TEKS: 3.11D, 4.11D, 5.11D (Editing Conventions)

Design Overview

  • Color Palette: Spooky purple (#6C3483), pumpkin orange (#E67E22), ghost white (#F8F9FA), midnight black (#1B1B2F), eerie green (#00FF88)
  • Font: Creepster (headers), Nunito (body)
  • Characters: Ghost Guide (“Boo-k Worm” — a friendly ghost with glasses and a book), Grammar Ghosts (one per room — must be “cleared” by fixing grammar)
  • Interactive Mechanic: Fix grammar errors to “clear” ghosts. Wrong answer → ghost gives a spooky hint. THREE difficulty tracks (3rd, 4th, 5th grade).
  • Structure: Choose-your-path format. After the intro, students select their grade level track. Each track has 5 rooms.

🟢 3rd GRADE TRACK — Escape Spell: GHOST

Room 1: Capitalization (Letter: G)

# Correct Explanation
1 B) My family went to Dallas, Texas in July. Dallas, Texas = proper nouns (city, state). July = month.
2 C) Every Monday, Mrs. Johnson reads to us. Monday = day. Mrs. Johnson = person’s name + title.
3 C) I think that Spot is the best dog in the world. Spot = pet’s name (proper). “dog” and “world” are common nouns.
4 B) We celebrate Thanksgiving in November. Thanksgiving = holiday. November = month. “Celebrate” is NOT capitalized mid-sentence.

Watch For: Students capitalizing common nouns (Dog, Family, World) or failing to capitalize titles (mrs. instead of Mrs.).

Room 2: End Punctuation (Letter: H)

# Sentence Correct Why
1 What time does the movie start ? It asks a question.
2 Watch out for that car ! Strong emotion/warning.
3 The library closes at five . Simple statement.
4 Can I borrow your pencil ? It asks a question.

Room 3: Spelling (Letter: O)

# Correct Spelling Common Errors
1 because becuz, becuase, becuse
2 friend freind (i before e confusion)
3 said sed, siad
4 their thier; also confusion with there/they’re
5 different diffrent, diferent

Room 4: Parts of Speech (Letter: S)

# Word Answer Explanation
1 brave Adjective Describes the firefighter (what kind?).
2 quickly Adverb Describes how she ran.
3 children Noun Person (people).
4 jumped Verb Action — what he did.

Room 5: Sentences (Letter: T)

# Correct Why Others Are Wrong
1 B) The children were running in the rain. A & C are fragments — no complete predicate.
2 B) I like pizza, and I also like tacos. A is a run-on. C is a fragment.
3 B) She went to bed early because she was tired. A is a dependent clause fragment. C is choppy/unclear.
4 B) The big brown fluffy dog slept on the rug. A & D have no verb.

🟡 4th GRADE TRACK — Escape Spell: SPELL

Room 1: Subject-Verb Agreement (Letter: S)

# Correct Explanation
1 has “Each” is singular.
2 have “Girls” is plural.
3 go “Neither…nor” — verb matches closer subject (“dogs” = plural).
4 wash “Sister and I” = compound subject = plural.

Room 2: Pronoun Usage (Letter: P)

# Correct Explanation
1 She Subject pronoun. (Remove “and I” to test: “She went” not “Her went.”)
2 me Object pronoun after “to.” (Remove “Marcus and”: “gave to me.”)
3 its Possessive for things/animals. NOT “it’s” (= it is).
4 his or her “Somebody” is singular indefinite.

Room 3: Commas (Letter: E)

# Correct Rule
1 B) apples, bananas, and grapes Series comma
2 A) Well, I think… After introductory word
3 A) homework, so I went Before conjunction in compound sentence
4 B) On the first day of school, we… After introductory phrase

Room 4: Homophones (Letter: L)

# Correct Meaning
1 They’re They are (contraction)
2 its Possessive (belonging to it)
3 write To put words on paper
4 through Moving in one side and out another
5 There Indicating location/existence

Room 5: Mixed Editing (Letter: L)

All answers are A — multiple errors per sentence requiring comprehensive editing.

🔴 5th GRADE TRACK — Escape Spell: HAUNT

Room 1: Complex Sentences (Letter: H)

# Correct Rule
1 B) comma after “rings” Dependent clause BEFORE independent = comma.
2 B) no comma Dependent clause AFTER independent = usually no comma.
3 C) comma after “hard” Dependent clause before independent = comma.
4 A) no comma Dependent clause after independent.

Room 2: Irregular Verbs (Letter: A)

# Verb Past Past Participle (with had/has)
1 freeze froze frozen (had frozen)
2 choose chose chosen
3 blow blew blown (had blown)
4 speak spoke spoken
5 begin began begun (had begun)

Key Distinction: Simple past (no helper verb) vs. past participle (with had/has/have).

Room 3: Phrases (Letter: U)

# Answer Explanation
1 onto the kitchen counter Prepositional phrase (onto = preposition)
2 My teacher, Mrs. Garcia, gave… Appositive needs commas on BOTH sides
3 The book, a mystery novel, kept… Same — appositive in commas
4 Between you and me Object of preposition = objective case “me” not “I”

Room 4: Edit Passage (Letter: N)

# Error Correction
1 saturday, antonio Saturday, Antonio (proper nouns)
2 drived drove
3 leaved left
4 their (possessive) there (in that place/existing)
5 She and me She and I (subject pronouns)

Room 5: STAAR-Style (Letter: T)

# Error Correction Skill
1 has been working have been working SVA (students = plural)
2 Fragment after period Combine with comma Sentence structure
3 brung / was brought / were Irregular verb + SVA
