## 📇 Recipe Card (Progress Tracker)

**Description:** A card tracking student progress through the week — like collecting ingredients for a recipe.

**Design:**
- Size: 5" x 7" (2 per page)
- Title: "MY RECIPE FOR STAAR SUCCESS"
- 5 ingredient slots:
  1. 🥬 **Reading** (Monday) — ☐ Collected!
  2. 🔢 **Math** (Tuesday) — ☐ Collected!
  3. 🧀 **Knowledge** (Wednesday) — ☐ Collected!
  4. 📝 **Practice** (Thursday) — ☐ Collected!
  5. ⭐ **Confidence** (Friday) — ☐ Collected!
- "Collect all 5 ingredients to become a TACO CHAMPION! 🌮"
- Student name at top
- Teacher stamps/checks each box daily

## 📝 Daily Packets

**Cover format:**
- "🌮 TODAY'S SPECIAL: [Day/Course Name]"
- "Chef: _____ Date: _____"
- Difficulty indicators:
  - Section A: 🌶️ Mild
  - Section B: 🌶️🌶️ Medium
  - Section C: 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy (bonus)
- STAAR-format questions inside (teacher provides)
- Back page: "Chef's Notes — What I cooked up well today: _____ What needs more seasoning: _____"

---

## 🌶️ Extra Credit Salsa

**Description:** Bonus challenge problems for students who finish early or want extra practice.

**Design:**
- Header: "🌶️🌶️🌶️ EXTRA CREDIT SALSA — Handle with Care!"
- 2-3 challenging problems per sheet
- "Warning: This salsa is SPICY! Only attempt if you've finished your daily special."
- Space for teacher to stamp "SALSA MASTER" if completed correctly

Keep 10-15 sheets in a folder. Students grab one when done with daily work.

---

## 📖 Menu Vocabulary Organizer

**Description:** Wednesday activity — students create a "restaurant menu" organizing concepts by difficulty.

**Template:**
- Folded paper to look like a restaurant menu
- **Appetizers ($):** Easy concepts I've mastered
- **Entrées ($$):** Medium concepts I'm comfortable with
- **Desserts ($$$):** Challenging concepts I'm still learning
- **Daily Specials:** Test-taking strategies
- Students fill in concepts from all subjects studied this week
