# 🏈 STAAR Athletes: Championship Week — Themed Activities

> 12 sports-themed activities for your Championship Week. Mix of drills, games, and team challenges.

---

## 🎮 REVIEW GAMES

### 1. STAAR Bowl (Jeopardy-Style Game)
**Type:** Teams | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Best for:** Multi-subject review

**Setup:**
- Create a 5x5 grid on the board (5 categories, 5 point values: 100-500)
- Categories: Reading Plays, Math Offense, Science Defense, Vocabulary Blitz, Wild Card
- Divide class into 3-4 teams

**How to Play:**
1. Teams take turns selecting a category and point value
2. Read the question; team has 30 seconds to discuss and answer
3. Correct = earn points. Incorrect = other teams can steal
4. "Daily Double" — one hidden square doubles the points
5. Team with most points wins the STAAR Bowl

**Materials:** Grid on board, question cards, scoreboard

---

### 2. Relay Race Review
**Type:** Teams of 5-6 | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Quick computation, grammar, vocabulary

**Setup:**
- Teams line up single-file facing the board
- Each team has a set of questions at the front (face down)

**How to Play:**
1. First player runs up, flips a question, solves it, writes answer
2. Runs back, tags next teammate
3. Next player runs up, checks previous answer (fix if wrong), then solves the next question
4. First team to correctly answer all questions wins
5. Penalty: wrong answer = team must do 5 jumping jacks before continuing

**Materials:** Question sets (one per team), pencils, answer key

---

### 3. March Madness Bracket Challenge
**Type:** Individual/class | **Time:** Ongoing all week | **Best for:** Daily engagement

**Setup:**
- Create a tournament bracket on poster board with 16 "skills" (e.g., Main Idea, Fractions, Vocabulary, etc.)
- Each day, two skills "compete" — students answer questions from both skills
- The skill the class scores higher on "advances"

**How to Play:**
1. Monday: 4 matchups (8 skills compete, 4 advance)
2. Tuesday: 2 matchups (4 skills, 2 advance)
3. Wednesday: Halftime — Fun bracket (favorite snacks, songs, etc.)
4. Thursday: Championship — final 2 skills compete
5. Friday: Crown the class's "Strongest Skill"

**Materials:** Large bracket poster, daily question sets for each matchup

## 👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

### 4. Personal Playbook (Study Guide Creation)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 20-25 min | **Best for:** Any subject synthesis

**Instructions:**
Students create their own "playbook" — a folded booklet with their personal study notes organized as "plays":
- **Offensive Plays** = Strategies for questions they're good at (how to maintain those skills)
- **Defensive Plays** = Strategies for their weak areas (how to handle tough questions)
- **Special Teams** = Test-taking tricks (elimination, time management, etc.)
- **Audibles** = What to do when stuck (skip and come back, re-read, etc.)

**Materials:** Paper (folded into booklet), pencils, colored markers

---

### 5. Strategy Trading Cards
**Type:** Individual (then partner swap) | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Reviewing test-taking strategies

**Instructions:**
Each student creates 3 trading cards (index cards):
- **Front:** Strategy name + illustration (e.g., "The Eliminator" with a picture of crossing out wrong answers)
- **Back:** How to use the strategy, when it works best, example
- After creation, students TRADE cards with classmates to collect different strategies
- Goal: collect at least 8 different strategy cards by end of week

**Strategy ideas:** The Eliminator, The Re-Reader, The Evidence Hunter, The Show-Your-Worker, The Time Manager, The Double Checker, The Process of Elimination Pro, The Context Clue Detective, The Key Word Finder, The Reasonableness Checker

**Materials:** Index cards, markers, colored pencils

---

### 6. MVP Scouting Report (Self-Assessment)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Metacognition, goal setting

**Instructions:**
Students fill out a "scouting report" on themselves:
- **Player Name:** [their name]
- **Position:** [their strongest subject]
- **Strengths (Offensive Stats):** List 3 skills they're confident in
- **Areas to Develop (Defensive Stats):** List 2-3 skills they need to work on
- **Game Plan:** For each weak area, write one specific action they'll take
- **Season Goal:** "On STAAR day, I will score _____ because I will _____"

**Materials:** Scouting Report template (printed), pencils

## 👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

### 7. Coach & Athlete Pairs
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 20-25 min | **Best for:** Math problem solving, reading comprehension

**Instructions:**
- Partner A = Coach (guides, questions, doesn't solve)
- Partner B = Athlete (solves, thinks aloud)
- Coach uses prompts: "What's the play here?", "Show me your work," "Are you sure about that call?"
- Switch roles every 2-3 problems
- Both partners record answers and strategies

**Materials:** Problem set (8-10 problems), recording sheet

---

### 8. Two-Minute Drill (Speed Partner Quiz)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 10-15 min | **Best for:** Fact fluency, vocabulary, quick recall

**Instructions:**
- Partners face each other with a stack of flashcards or question strips between them
- Set timer for 2 minutes
- Partner A quizzes Partner B as fast as possible — keep a tally of correct answers
- Switch roles for another 2 minutes
- Compare scores — both try to beat their own score in round 2
- "In the two-minute drill, every second counts!"

**Materials:** Flashcards or question strips, timer, tally sheet

## 👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

### 9. Scrimmage Stations (Rotation Drill)
**Type:** Groups of 4-5 | **Time:** 30-35 min | **Best for:** Multi-subject review

**Setup:** 5 stations, each themed as a different "position":
- **Quarterback Station** — Reading comprehension (leads the offense = understands the text)
- **Running Back Station** — Math computation (runs the numbers)
- **Wide Receiver Station** — Vocabulary (catches the meaning)
- **Defensive Line Station** — Error analysis (find the mistakes)
- **Kicker Station** — Word problems (precision under pressure)

**How to Play:**
Groups rotate every 6 minutes. Complete 3-4 questions per station. Record on scorecard.

**Materials:** Station question sets, scorecards, timer, station labels

---

### 10. Huddle Up: Team Problem Solving
**Type:** Groups of 4 | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Complex multi-step problems

**Instructions:**
1. Each group gets ONE complex, multi-step problem (challenging!)
2. Team huddles — discuss strategy before anyone writes anything (2 min)
3. Team works together to solve (8 min)
4. One team member presents their solution to the class (2 min each)
5. Class votes: "Did they make the right call?" — discuss correct approach

**Materials:** One complex problem per group (laminated or on large paper), markers, chart paper for presenting

---

### 11. Championship Bracket Debate
**Type:** Groups of 4 (2v2) | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Reading/ELA, Social Studies

**Instructions:**
- Present a STAAR question with 2 plausible answer choices
- In each group, 2 students argue for Answer A, 2 for Answer B
- Each side presents evidence (2 min per side)
- Group votes, then teacher reveals correct answer
- Winning side earns a point for their team on the class scoreboard

**Materials:** Debate questions with passages, timer, scoreboard

---

### 12. Tailgate Party Review (End-of-Day Celebration Game)
**Type:** Whole class | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** End of Wednesday (Halftime Show)

**Setup:**
- "Tailgate" stations around the room with snacks and games
- Station 1: Vocabulary Cornhole (toss beanbags at vocab words, define the one you hit)
- Station 2: Math Ring Toss (toss rings onto numbered pegs, solve the problem on that number)
- Station 3: Trivia Toss (answer question correctly, earn a free throw with a paper ball into a trash can)
- Station 4: Word Problem Horseshoes (solve problem, toss horseshoe — closest to the stake gets a bonus point)

Keep it simple — use what you have. Paper balls and trash cans work great. The point is FUN with learning mixed in.

**Materials:** Paper balls, trash cans, beanbags (or balled-up socks), vocab cards, math problems, small snacks
