# 🌮 Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready — Themed Activities

> 10 food/fiesta-themed activities with 3 review games.

---

## 🎮 REVIEW GAMES

### 1. Salsa Showdown (Team Competition)
**Type:** Teams | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Multi-subject review

**How to Play:**
- 3-4 teams. Teacher asks STAAR questions.
- First team to answer correctly "adds salsa" (earns a point)
- Track on a scoreboard with salsa jars (tally marks = salsa scoops)
- Every 5th question is a "GHOST PEPPER" — worth 3 points but if wrong, lose 2
- Team with the most salsa wins!

**Materials:** Questions, scoreboard with salsa jar drawings

---

### 2. Recipe Relay (Team Relay Race)
**Type:** Teams of 5-6 | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Quick computation, vocabulary

**How to Play:**
- Each team has a "recipe" (set of 6 questions) at the front
- First chef runs up, answers question 1 (an "ingredient"), runs back
- Next chef adds ingredient 2 (answers question 2)
- If an ingredient is wrong (answer incorrect), the "dish is ruined" — next person must fix it
- First team to complete their recipe correctly wins

**Materials:** Question sets, answer key

---

### 3. Food Truck Challenge (Station Game)
**Type:** Groups | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Best for:** Mixed review

**Setup:** 4-5 "food trucks" (stations) around the room:
- 🌮 Taco Truck — Reading questions
- 🍕 Pizza Stand — Math problems
- 🌶️ Hot Sauce Station — Spicy challenge problems (hard)
- 🧀 Nacho Bar — Science/SS questions
- 🍩 Donut Shop — Fun bonus challenges

Groups rotate every 5-6 minutes. Complete the "order" (question set) at each truck. Record on an "Order Ticket" sheet.

**Materials:** Station questions, order ticket recording sheets, timer, truck labels

## 👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

### 4. Recipe for Success (Study Guide)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Synthesis

**Instructions:**
Students write their personal "Recipe for STAAR Success":
- **Ingredients:** List skills/concepts they need (e.g., "1 cup of main idea skills, 2 tbsp of fraction knowledge")
- **Directions:** Step-by-step test-day plan ("Step 1: Read the question first. Step 2: Eliminate 2 wrong answers...")
- **Cooking Time:** How long to spend on each section
- **Chef's Notes:** Personal reminders and tips

Written on a recipe card template.

**Materials:** Recipe card template (printed), pencils, colored markers

---

### 5. Extra Credit Salsa (Bonus Challenge)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** Varies (ongoing) | **Best for:** Early finishers, motivated students

**Description:** A set of extra-spicy 🌶️🌶️🌶️ challenge problems available all week. Students who complete them earn "Extra Credit Salsa" — a special stamp/sticker on their recipe card.

Keep 10-15 challenge problems in a folder labeled "EXTRA CREDIT SALSA — Handle with Care!" Students grab one when they finish early.

**Materials:** Folder with challenge problems, stamps/stickers

---

### 6. Menu Creation (Vocabulary Organizer)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Vocabulary review

**Instructions:**
Students create a "restaurant menu" for their subject:
- **Appetizers:** Easy concepts (define and give example)
- **Entrées:** Medium concepts
- **Desserts:** The satisfying stuff (things they've mastered)
- **Daily Specials:** Concepts they're still learning
- Include "prices" (difficulty ratings: $=easy, $$=medium, $$$=hard)

**Materials:** Paper folded into menu format, pencils/markers

## 👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

### 7. Sous Chef Partners (Guided Partner Practice)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Any subject

**Instructions:**
- Partner A = Head Chef (solves the problem)
- Partner B = Sous Chef (watches, asks "What's your next step?", checks work)
- Switch roles every 2-3 problems
- Both must agree on every answer before moving on

**Materials:** Problem set, pencils

---

### 8. Taste Test Partners (Quick Quiz)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Fact review, vocabulary

**Instructions:**
Partners quiz each other using flashcards or question strips:
- Round 1 (2 min): Partner A quizzes B — "Taste this!" (rapid fire)
- Round 2 (2 min): Switch
- Round 3 (2 min): Both solve problems together — "Let's cook this one up!"
- Track correct answers. Try to beat your own score each round.

**Materials:** Flashcards/question strips, timer

---

## 👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

### 9. Iron Chef Challenge (Group Competition)
**Type:** Groups of 4 | **Time:** 25 min | **Best for:** Complex problems

**Instructions:**
Each group gets one complex, multi-part problem (the "secret ingredient"). They have 15 minutes to create the best "dish" (solution), including:
- Complete solution with all work shown
- Explanation of their strategy
- Presentation to the class (2 min each)

Class votes on the best "dish" (clearest explanation). Winning team = Iron Chefs!

**Materials:** Complex problems, chart paper, markers

---

### 10. Potluck Review (Collaborative Study Guide)
**Type:** Groups of 3-4 | **Time:** 25 min | **Best for:** End-of-week synthesis

**Instructions:**
Each group brings a "dish" to the class potluck — a poster covering one topic/skill:
- 5 key vocabulary terms
- 3 example problems with solutions
- 2 common mistakes to avoid
- 1 "secret ingredient" (unique tip)

Post all posters. Students do a "potluck walk" — visit each poster and take notes on anything they didn't know.

**Materials:** Poster paper/butcher paper, markers, textbooks
