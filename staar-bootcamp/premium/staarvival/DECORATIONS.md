## 🌴 HALLWAY: Wilderness Trail

### The Vision
The hallway becomes a trail through different terrains. Jungle vines hang from above, animal tracks on the floor lead to classrooms, and each door is a different "base camp."

### Step-by-Step

#### 1. Jungle Vines
- Twist **green crepe paper streamers** into vine shapes. Hang from ceiling/top of walls
- Add paper **leaves** cut from green construction paper (various shades)
- Twist brown paper bags into "tree branches" and tape to walls near doorframes
- **Cost:** ~$4-6 for streamers + construction paper

#### 2. Animal Tracks on the Floor
- Cut **animal footprints** from brown/black construction paper (paw prints, bird tracks, boot prints)
- Tape to the floor creating a trail from the hallway entrance to each classroom
- Or use painter's tape to make simple track shapes
- **Cost:** ~$2-3 for paper, $2 for tape

#### 3. Base Camp Door Signs
- Each classroom door = a different base camp
- **"Base Camp [Teacher Name] — [Terrain]: [Subject]"**
- Example: "Base Camp Rodriguez — Desert Terrain: Mathematics"
- Print on brown cardstock (looks like parchment/wood)
- Add terrain-appropriate graphics (cacti, trees, waves, mountains)
- **Cost:** ~$2 for cardstock

#### 4. Main Entrance Sign
- Large banner: **"STAAR-VIVAL: THE EXPEDITION BEGINS HERE"**
- Use brown butcher paper for a "wooden sign" look
- Write with thick black marker
- Add paper leaves and vines around the edges
- **Cost:** ~$0-2

#### 5. Terrain Zone Markers
- Along the hallway, post signs marking different "zones":
  - "⚠️ ENTERING JUNGLE ZONE — Watch for snakes!"
  - "🏜️ DESERT AHEAD — Conserve your water!"
  - "⛰️ MOUNTAIN PASS — Steep climb ahead!"
- Adds immersion as students walk to class
- **Cost:** ~$1 for paper

## 🏕️ CLASSROOM: Base Camp

### Door Sign
- **"BASE CAMP [Room #] — EXPLORERS ONLY"**
- Brown cardstock, hand-drawn compass rose, rugged font style
- Optional: add a paper "tent flap" (construction paper folded) framing the door window

### Desk Setup
- Desks in clusters = "expedition teams"
- Each team has a terrain name: Team Jungle, Team Desert, Team Mountain, Team Ocean
- Desk name tags: **"Explorer [Name] — Team [Terrain]"** on brown/kraft paper
- Place a **Survival Kit** (ziplock bag with 2 pencils, eraser, small treat) at each desk on Monday

### Wall Decorations

#### Journey Map (CENTERPIECE)
- Large poster or butcher paper map showing 5 terrains in order: Jungle → Desert → Mountains → Ocean → Summit
- Draw a winding path through all 5
- Add a moveable marker (sticky note, pin, magnet) showing current position
- Student names listed; add stickers as they complete each terrain
- **This is the most important decoration.** Make it big and visible.

#### Compass Rose
- Large compass rose on the wall (N/S/E/W)
- Each direction = a subject: N=Reading, S=Math, E=Science, W=Social Studies
- Center: "Navigate Your Learning"

#### Motivational Posters
- "The only way out is THROUGH." 🌿
- "Survivors don't quit. They adapt."
- "Every expert explorer was once lost."
- "The summit is closer than you think."
- "Leave no question unanswered. Leave no explorer behind."

#### Supply Station
- Corner of the room labeled "SUPPLY DEPOT"
- Organize pencils, erasers, scratch paper in labeled containers
- Use camping-style labels: "Fire Starters" (pencils), "First Aid" (erasers), "Rations" (snacks if applicable)
