### The task card bundle that covers the hardest STAAR topics! 💪

## 📦 SET 1: Fractions (30 Cards)

### ⭐ Level 1 — Foundation (10 cards)
- Identify fractions from models (circles, rectangles, number lines)
- Compare fractions with same denominator
- Equivalent fractions with visual models
- Unit fractions on a number line

### ⭐⭐ Level 2 — Application (10 cards)
- Compare fractions with unlike denominators
- Add/subtract fractions with like denominators
- Decompose fractions (3/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4)
- Fraction word problems with models

### ⭐⭐⭐ Level 3 — Challenge (10 cards)
- Multi-step fraction word problems
- Fractions in real-world contexts (recipes, measurement)
- Explain your reasoning (constructed response format)
- STAAR 2.0 multi-select questions

**TEKS:** 3.3(A-H), 4.3(A-G), 5.3(A-L)

---

## 📦 SET 2: Geometry (30 Cards)

### ⭐ Level 1 — Foundation (10 cards)
- Identify 2D shapes and their attributes
- Lines of symmetry
- Classify angles (acute, right, obtuse)
- Identify parallel and perpendicular lines

### ⭐⭐ Level 2 — Application (10 cards)
- Measure angles with a protractor
- Calculate perimeter of regular and irregular shapes
- Calculate area using formulas
- Classify triangles by sides and angles

### ⭐⭐⭐ Level 3 — Challenge (10 cards)
- Composite figure area and perimeter
- Coordinate graphing (ordered pairs)
- Volume of rectangular prisms
- Multi-step geometry word problems

**TEKS:** 3.6(A-B), 4.6(A-D), 4.7(C-E), 5.4(H), 5.6(A-B), 5.8(A-C)

---

## 📦 SET 3: Data Analysis (30 Cards)

### ⭐ Level 1 — Foundation (10 cards)
- Read bar graphs and pictographs
- Read frequency tables
- Read dot plots
- Answer "how many more/fewer" questions

### ⭐⭐ Level 2 — Application (10 cards)
- Create bar graphs from data sets
- Interpret stem-and-leaf plots
- Mean, median, mode, and range
- Draw conclusions from data displays

### ⭐⭐⭐ Level 3 — Challenge (10 cards)
- Two-step data interpretation problems
- Compare two data sets
- Justify conclusions using data evidence
- Design your own survey and display results

**TEKS:** 3.8(A-B), 4.9(A-B), 5.9(A-C)
