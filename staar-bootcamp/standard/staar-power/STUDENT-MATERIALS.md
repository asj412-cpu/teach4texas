## 🦸 Hero ID Card

**Design:**
- Size: 3" x 4" (4 per page)
- Header: "⚡ HERO ACADEMY ⚡"
- Fields:
  - **Real Name:** [Student Name]
  - **Hero Name:** [Student creates]
  - **Superpower:** [Best academic skill]
  - **Weakness:** [Skill to improve]
  - **Catchphrase:** [Motivational phrase for test day]
- Box for self-portrait as a hero
- "Hero Academy Class of [Year]"

---

## 🎟️ Power-Up Tickets

**Description:** Small reward tickets earned throughout the week. Can be collected and redeemed for small rewards (teacher's choice: homework pass, first in line, small prize, etc.)

**Design:**
- Size: 2" x 3" (print 10 per page)
- "⚡ POWER-UP TICKET ⚡"
- "Awarded to a hero for: ___________"
- Comic-book burst border

**Award criteria:** Effort on daily packets, participation, helping a teammate, exceptional focus.

**Tip:** Be generous. These cost nothing and motivate everything. Award 1-3 per student per day.

---

## 📝 Daily Villain Battle Packets

**Cover format:**
- "⚡ VILLAIN BATTLE: [Villain Name] ⚡"
- Villain illustration/description
- "Hero: _____ Date: _____"
- "Power Level Required: ★★★☆☆"
- STAAR-format questions inside (teacher provides content)
- Back page reflection: "Villain Status: ☐ Defeated ☐ Needs Rematch"

---

## 🖼️ Villain Wanted Posters

**Template:**
- "WANTED" in large bold text at top
- Box for student to draw the villain
- "Villain Name:" ___________
- "Crimes Against Students:" (what test mistake this represents)
- "How to Defeat:" (strategy to overcome it)
- "Reward for Capture: STAAR Success!"

Students create these on Wednesday. Display on a "Villain Wall."

---

## 🎨 Hero Emblem (Friday Activity)

**Template:**
- Shield/emblem shape outline
- "Design your personal hero emblem. Include symbols that represent your strongest skills."
- Sections: "My Reading Symbol," "My Math Symbol," "My Secret Weapon" (test strategy)
- Students color and decorate
- Display alongside certificates
