## 🛡️ Detective Badge

**Design:**
- Size: 3" x 4" (4 per page), shield/badge shape
- Header: "STAAR DETECTIVE AGENCY"
- **Detective:** [Student Name]
- **Badge #:** [Number]
- **Rank:** Junior Detective (upgraded to "Master Detective" on Friday)
- **Specialty:** [Student's best subject]
- Space for self-portrait or small photo
- "Licensed to Investigate"

---

## 📓 Detective Notebook

**Description:** A stapled booklet for the entire week. 7 pages + cover.

**Cover:**
- "TOP SECRET — DETECTIVE NOTEBOOK"
- "Property of Detective [Name]"
- "Case: #STAAR — Mission to Mastery"
- "Classification: CONFIDENTIAL"
- Space for badge number

**Pages:**
1. **Case Overview** — Students write: "The case," "My team," "My goal for this week"
2-6. **Daily Case Notes** (one per day Mon-Fri) — Date, daily prompt, key findings, strategy learned
7. **Closing Statement** — Final reflection + space for classmate signatures on Friday

---

## 📁 Daily Case Files (Packets)

**Cover format:**
- "📁 CASE FILE #[Number] — [Case Name]"
- "CLASSIFIED — For Detective Eyes Only"
- "Detective: _____ Badge #: _____ Date: _____"
- "Assignment: Examine the following evidence and determine the correct answers."
- STAAR-format questions inside (teacher provides)
- Back page: "Investigation Notes — Evidence that supports my answers: _____ Leads to follow up: _____"

---

## 🔎 Clue Cards

**Description:** 5 pre-made cards (teacher prepares) revealed one per day.

**Design:**
- Size: 5" x 7"
- Header: "🔍 CLUE #[Number]"
- Large text with the clue message
- Footer: "Add to Evidence Board"
- Slightly aged/worn look (crumple slightly, tea-stain edges if feeling crafty)

**The 5 clues:**
1. "The thief strikes when students don't READ carefully."
2. "The thief hides in SKIPPED STEPS and rushed calculations."
3. "The thief feeds on GAPS IN KNOWLEDGE."
4. "The thief is strongest when students PANIC under pressure."
5. "The thief's name is... DOUBT! And YOU have defeated it with PREPARATION!"

---

## 🎯 Suspect Profile Template

**Description:** Wednesday activity — students "profile" their hardest academic topic.

**Design:**
- Title: "SUSPECT PROFILE"
- **Suspect Name:** _____ (topic name)
- **Aliases:** _____ (related terms)
- **Last Seen:** _____ (when did this topic trip you up?)
- **Known Tactics:** _____ (what makes this confusing?)
- **Weakness:** _____ (strategy to defeat it)
- **WANTED FOR:** Causing confusion on tests
- Large box: "Suspect Sketch" (student draws a funny character)
- "REWARD: STAAR success!"

---

## 🏆 Case Closed Certificate

**Design:**
- 8.5x11 landscape
- Header: Magnifying glass + "STAAR DETECTIVE AGENCY"
- Title: "CASE CLOSED CERTIFICATE"
- "This certifies that **Detective [Name]** has successfully solved Case #STAAR by collecting all evidence, investigating all subjects, and demonstrating mastery. This detective has proven that DOUBT is no match for PREPARATION."
- "Chief Detective: [Teacher Name]"
- "Case Closed: [Date]"
- Border: magnifying glasses, footprints, noir-style design

---

## ✉️ Closing Statement (Friday Letter)

**Template:**
- "CLOSING STATEMENT — Case #STAAR"
- "I, Detective [Name], submit the following findings:"
- "The evidence shows I am strong in: _____"
- "My investigation revealed I should review: _____"
- "My strategy for test day: _____"
- "I am confident because: _____"
- "Case status: READY TO CLOSE. ✓"
- Signed and dated

Sealed in envelope, returned on STAAR day.
