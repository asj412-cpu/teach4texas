# 🌿 STAAR-vival: Escape the Test — 5-Day Lesson Plans

> **Trail Guide's Handbook — Navigate the week terrain by terrain.**

---

## Daily Schedule Template

| Time Block | Duration | Activity |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Trail Briefing | 10 min | Theme intro, daily terrain overview, motivation |

## 🌴 MONDAY — Jungle Terrain

**Theme:** "The jungle is dense and full of surprises. Reading is like navigating a jungle — you have to push through the thick text to find what you need."

**Focus:** Reading/ELA

### Trail Briefing (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Explorers! Welcome to STAAR-vival. I'm your Lead Explorer, and this week we're going on the adventure of a lifetime. We'll cross five terrains — jungle, desert, mountains, ocean, and finally the summit. Each terrain will test different survival skills. Today: the JUNGLE. The jungle is dense, confusing, full of hidden dangers — just like a tough reading passage. But a trained explorer knows how to navigate. They look for paths, read the signs, and find their way through. Today, we learn to navigate the jungle of text. Grab your survival kits — let's move out!"

**Hand out:** Explorer Journals, Survival Badge Cards, Survival Kits (pencil pouches)

### Survival Training 1 (45 min)

**Reading/ELA Focus:**
1. **(10 min) "Trail Markers" Warm-Up** — 5 quick questions on whiteboards. "Trail markers help you find your way. Key words in questions are YOUR trail markers."
2. **(20 min) "Machete Through the Text"** — Teacher models close reading/annotation of a dense STAAR passage. "We're cutting through the jungle. Every time I find an important detail, I mark the trail." Demonstrate highlighting, margin notes, underlining key evidence.
3. **(15 min) "Buddy System"** — Partners read a passage together. One reads aloud (slowly, like hiking through brush), the other marks trail markers (key details). Discuss and answer 3-4 questions together.

### Rest Stop (5 min)
- "Animal Sounds" brain break — teacher calls an animal, students make the sound and a movement (monkey = ooh-ooh + arm swings, snake = hisssss + slither). Silly. Effective.

### Survival Training 2 (40 min)

**"Jungle Survival Challenge" Packet**
- STAAR-format reading passages with questions
- Themed: "You found this message carved into a tree. Read it carefully..."
- Early finishers: "Hidden Treasure" bonus challenge

**Differentiation:**
- **Struggling:** "Trail Guide" version — shorter passages, vocabulary pre-taught, guided annotation marks provided
- **Advanced:** "Uncharted Territory" — complex texts with analytical questions
- **ELL:** Visual vocabulary cards, bilingual key terms

### Tribal Council (10 min)
- Quick review game: Teacher reads a question, students vote (thumbs up/down for answer choices A-D). Discuss the correct "verdict."
- Award **Jungle Survival Badges** 🌴
- Update Journey Map: Jungle = CONQUERED

## 🏜️ TUESDAY — Desert Terrain

**Theme:** "The desert is harsh and unforgiving. Resources are scarce. Every drop of water counts — just like every step in a math problem counts."

**Focus:** Mathematics

### Trail Briefing (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Explorers, we survived the jungle! Today we enter the DESERT. In the desert, you can't waste anything. Every resource matters. Math is like desert survival — every step in your work matters. Skip a step, and you're lost. Show ALL your work today. Conserve your energy. Be precise. The desert rewards the careful and punishes the careless. Let's cross this desert!"

### Survival Training 1 (45 min)

**Math Focus:**
1. **(10 min) "Water Rationing" Warm-Up** — 5 computation problems. "Each correct answer = one water bottle for your team. Get them wrong, and you're dehydrated!"
2. **(20 min) "Map & Compass" Guided Practice** — Teacher models multi-step word problems as navigation challenges. "We need to calculate the distance to the next oasis..." Show every step. Emphasize: "In the desert, skipping steps = getting lost."
3. **(15 min) "Resource Sharing"** — Partners work through 6 problems. Each partner solves 3 independently, then they check each other's work. "Your buddy's life depends on your accuracy!"

### Rest Stop (5 min)
- "Mirage or Real?" — Teacher shows 5 quick math statements. Students decide: true (real) or false (mirage). Stand up for real, sit for mirage.

### Survival Training 2 (40 min)

**"Desert Survival Challenge" Packet**
- STAAR-format math problems
- Themed intro: "You need to calculate your way across the desert. One wrong answer and you veer off course."

**Differentiation:**
- **Struggling:** Reference sheet, worked examples, fewer problems
- **Advanced:** "Scorching Challenge" — complex multi-step problems
- **ELL:** Simplified word problem language, math vocabulary visuals

### Tribal Council (10 min)
- "Oasis or Mirage?" — Teacher presents 3 worked-out problems. Some have errors hidden in them. Students vote on whether each is correct (oasis) or has an error (mirage). Find the errors together.
- Award **Desert Survival Badges** 🏜️
- Journey Map update: Desert = CONQUERED

## ⛰️ WEDNESDAY — Mountain Terrain

**Theme:** "Mountains are the ultimate challenge. You can see the summit, but the climb is steep. Science and social studies are our mountains to climb."

**Focus:** Science & Social Studies (or continued ELA/Math review)

### Trail Briefing (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "We've cleared the jungle and crossed the desert. Now we face the MOUNTAINS. The climb is steep, but the view from the top? Incredible. Today we tackle science and social studies — the peaks of knowledge. Every fact you learn, every concept you master, gets you one step closer to the summit. And remember — we climb together. No explorer left behind!"

### Survival Training 1 (45 min)

**Science Review:**
1. **(10 min) "Base Camp Check"** — 5 rapid-fire science questions on whiteboards
2. **(20 min) "Mountain Stations"** — 4-5 stations around the room, each a different "elevation" (difficulty) and science topic. Students rotate in groups (5 min each). Each station has a terrain flavor: "Elevation 1000ft: Matter & Energy," "Elevation 3000ft: Force & Motion," etc.
3. **(15 min) "Trail Journal"** — Students write in Explorer Journals: draw a compass rose with 4 science topics on each point, add key facts along each direction.

### Rest Stop (5 min)
- "Mountain Goat Balance" — Students stand on one foot for 30 seconds. Switch. Silly balance challenge. "Mountain goats never fall!"

### Survival Training 2 (40 min)

**Social Studies + Mixed Review:**
1. **(15 min) "History Hike" Timeline** — Collaborative timeline activity (same as STAAR Wars but themed as a mountain trail — events placed at increasing "elevations")
2. **(25 min) "Mountain Challenge" Packet** — Mixed science/social studies STAAR questions

### Tribal Council (10 min)
- **Full Tribal Council Game:** Teams of 4-5. Teacher reads a question with 4 answer choices. Each team discusses (1 min), then "votes" by holding up A/B/C/D cards. Teams that voted correctly survive. Teams that voted wrong are "on the chopping block" — they must answer a bonus question to stay in the game. Last team standing wins.
- Award **Mountain Survival Badges** ⛰️
- Journey Map: Mountains = CONQUERED
- Midweek treat! 🎉

## 🌊 THURSDAY — Ocean Terrain

**Theme:** "The ocean is vast and deep. Today we take a deep dive — full STAAR review, navigating the currents of a mixed test."

**Focus:** Mixed STAAR-format practice (all subjects)

### Trail Briefing (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Explorers. Jungle, desert, mountains — all conquered. Now we face the OCEAN. The ocean is unpredictable. Currents can pull you off course. Waves can knock you down. But if you stay calm and use your skills, you'll swim right through. Today is our deep dive — a full practice crossing all subjects. Treat it like the real STAAR. Stay focused. Breathe. Navigate the currents."

### Survival Training 1 (45 min)

**"Deep Dive Simulation"**
- Mini STAAR-format practice test (20-30 mixed questions)
- Test-like conditions: silent, independent, timed
- "You're swimming across the ocean. Every correct answer is a stroke closer to shore."

### Rest Stop (5 min)
- Deep breathing exercise: "When you're swimming and you get tired, you float. Close your eyes. Breathe. Float."

### Survival Training 2 (40 min)

**"Surfacing" Analysis & Review**
1. **(15 min)** Review answers together
2. **(10 min)** Students fill out self-assessment: strengths, weak areas, strategy adjustments
3. **(15 min)** Small group "rescue teams" — students grouped by need, targeted review

### Tribal Council (10 min)
- Quick Tribal Council vote on 3 tough questions from the simulation
- Award **Ocean Survival Badges** 🌊
- Journey Map: Ocean = CONQUERED
- "Tomorrow we reach the SUMMIT. One more terrain. You've got this."

## 🏔️ FRIDAY — The Summit

**Theme:** "You've crossed every terrain. Today you reach the summit. Look how far you've come. You're ready."

**Focus:** Test strategies, confidence, celebration

### Trail Briefing (10 min)
**Teacher Script:**
> "Explorers. Look at your journey map. Jungle — conquered. Desert — conquered. Mountains — conquered. Ocean — conquered. Today, you reach the SUMMIT. The summit isn't about learning new things. It's about standing at the top and seeing everything clearly. Today we review our strategies, celebrate our journey, and prepare for the STAAR. You are SURVIVORS. You are ready."

### Survival Training 1 (45 min)

1. **(15 min) "Survival Guide Review"** — Review top 10 test-taking strategies framed as survival rules: "Rule #1: Never eat unknown berries = Never pick an answer you can't prove with evidence."
2. **(15 min) "Campfire Stories"** — Teacher reads test-taking strategies as adventure tales. "Once, an explorer was lost in a reading passage. She couldn't find the main idea. But then she remembered: look at the first and last paragraph. The trail markers were there all along..."
3. **(15 min) "Final Challenge" Fun Review Game** — Quick, light-stakes game. Celebrate right answers loudly.

### Rest Stop (5 min)
- Quiet meditation: "Close your eyes. You're standing on the summit. Feel the wind. You made it."

### Survival Training 2 (40 min)

1. **(10 min) "Letters from the Summit"** — Students write a letter to themselves from the top of the mountain
2. **(10 min) "Survival Certificate" Ceremony** — Award certificates to each explorer. Make it meaningful.
3. **(10 min) "Summit Photo"** — Class photo, students sign each other's Explorer Journals
4. **(10 min) Celebration** — Treats, music, joy. "You survived STAAR-vival!"

### Final Tribal Council
- Award **Summit Survival Badges** 🏔️
- All 5 badges = "Ultimate Survivor" status
- Journey Map: ALL TERRAINS CONQUERED 🎉
- Final class cheer: "We SURVIVED! We're STAAR READY!"
