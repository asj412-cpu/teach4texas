End of Year Memory Book & Reflection Journal — Full Content

Visual Design Notes

  • Color Palette: Bright rainbow — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple (each section a different color)
  • Style: Yearbook meets scrapbook — fun frames, sticker-style graphics, confetti, stars, pencils
  • Layout: Half-page sections with plenty of writing AND drawing space
  • Fonts: Fun, kid-friendly rounded fonts for headers, lined spaces for writing
  • Cover: “My ___Grade Memory Book” with customizable name/grade, surrounded by school supply doodles

Writing Prompts

Prompt 1: My Year in a Story

Write a short story about your school year as if it were a book. What would the title be? What are the “chapters”? How does it end?

Prompt 2: Thank You Letter

Write a thank-you letter to someone who made your year special. It could be a teacher, friend, family member, custodian, or anyone!

Prompt 3: If I Could Relive One Day

If you could relive one day from this school year, which day would you choose and why? Describe it in detail.

Prompt 4: What _____ Grade Taught Me

Write about the most important lesson you learned this year — not from a textbook, but about life.

Prompt 5: Summer Dreams

Write about your plans, hopes, or dreams for summer. What do you want to do, learn, or experience?
