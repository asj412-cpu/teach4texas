## Lesson 1: Math Mystery Detective 🔍

**Grade:** 3rd
**TEKS:** 3.4K, 3.5B
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Mystery case folders (printed), dry erase boards, markers, "Evidence Logs" (printed), manipulatives

### Learning Objective
*I can solve multiplication and division word problems by identifying clues in the text and choosing the correct operation.*

### STAAR Connection
Practices **multi-step problem solving** and **constructed response** — both key STAAR 2.0 formats.

---

### Warm-Up (10 minutes): "Detective Training"

Display these "Quick Cases" one at a time:

1. "There are 4 teams. Each team has 6 players. How many players in all?" (4 × 6 = 24)
2. "24 stickers shared equally among 3 friends. How many each?" (24 ÷ 3 = 8)
3. "A baker made 5 trays of cookies. Each tray has 7 cookies. He gave away 10 cookies. How many are left?" (5 × 7 = 35; 35 - 10 = 25)

**Key:** For #3, ask "What makes this problem HARDER than #1 and #2?" (two steps!)

---

### Main Activity (25 minutes): "The Case of the Missing Math"

Divide class into teams of 3-4. Each team receives a "Mystery Case Folder" with 4 cases.

**Case #1: The Pet Store Puzzle**
> Mr. Garcia's pet store received 8 boxes of dog treats. Each box has 9 bags inside. He put the bags on 6 shelves equally. How many bags are on each shelf?

*Solution: 8 × 9 = 72 bags total; 72 ÷ 6 = 12 bags per shelf*

**Case #2: The Lunch Room Mystery**
> The cafeteria ordered 56 cartons of milk for 7 classes. Each class got the same number. Then 3 cartons from Mrs. Lee's class were returned. How many good cartons does Mrs. Lee's class have?

*Solution: 56 ÷ 7 = 8 cartons per class; 8 - 3 = 5 good cartons*

**Case #3: The Field Trip Fiasco**
> 48 students are going on a field trip. Each van holds 8 students. The school already has 2 vans. How many MORE vans does the school need to rent?

*Solution: 48 ÷ 8 = 6 vans needed; 6 - 2 = 4 more vans to rent*

**Case #4: The Birthday Party Problem** *(Challenge)*
> Mia is buying party favors for 9 friends: 3 stickers, 2 erasers, 1 pencil each. Stickers come in packs of 9. Erasers come in packs of 6. How many packs of each?

*Stickers: 9 × 3 = 27; 27 ÷ 9 = 3 packs. Erasers: 9 × 2 = 18; 18 ÷ 6 = 3 packs.*

---

### Practice (15 minutes): Solo Detective Work

Students independently solve 2 STAAR-format problems.

### Exit Ticket (10 minutes)

3-question formative assessment in STAAR 2.0 format.
