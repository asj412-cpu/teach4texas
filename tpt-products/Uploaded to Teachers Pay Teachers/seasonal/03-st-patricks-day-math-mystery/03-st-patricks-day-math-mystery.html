Visual Design Notes

  • Color Palette: Emerald green, gold/yellow, white, black accents
  • Style: Detective mystery meets Irish whimsy — magnifying glasses, gold coins, shamrocks, rainbow trails
  • Layout: Each station card is themed as a “locked door” with a keyhole graphic
  • Fonts: “Mystery” serif font for titles, clean sans-serif for math problems
  • Backgrounds: Green textured with subtle shamrock watermarks

Last night, the famous Shamrock City Gold Vault was robbed! The leprechaun’s legendary pot of gold — worth exactly 1,847 gold coins — has vanished!

THE DECODER

Students combine all 6 lock codes: - Station 1: RAINB - Station 2: OWRIT - Station 3: ANOTE - Station 4: FROMR - Station 5: ITAHE (unscramble within station) - Station 6: RITA!

Combined message: “RAINBOW RITA NOTE FROM RITA HE— RITA!”

Final reveal: Rearranging the clues: “RAINBOW RITA” wrote a note that says “THE GOLD IS MINE!” → Rainbow Rita is the thief! 🌈

Students fill in their “Case Solved” certificate: “I solved The Case of the Missing Gold! The thief was Rainbow Rita!”

Teacher Setup Guide

Preparation (15 min)

  1. Print station cards (color recommended) and cut
  2. Optional: laminate for reuse
  3. Set up 6 stations around the room OR distribute as packet
  4. Print student answer sheets (1 per student or pair)
  5. Choose difficulty level per student/group

Classroom Options

  • Centers rotation: 6 stations, 8-10 min each
  • Whole class: Project one station at a time, all solve together
  • Partner work: Print packets, pairs work through all 6
  • Digital: Assign Google Slides version

Time: 45-60 minutes total
