Dear Families,

Next week, our classroom is going on an adventure! We're launching **STAAR-vival** — a survival/adventure-themed STAAR prep week where your child will journey through five "terrains" (Jungle, Desert, Mountains, Ocean, and Summit) while reviewing key skills for the upcoming STAAR test. 🌿🏔️

**What is STAAR-vival?**
It's a fun, immersive review week. Each day focuses on a different terrain (subject area), with themed activities, challenges, and team-based learning. Students earn "Survival Badges" for each terrain they conquer, and by Friday, they'll be certified "Ultimate Survivors" — confident and ready for STAAR.

**When:** [Monday, Date] – [Friday, Date]

**What your child will experience:**
- 🗺️ Daily survival challenges reviewing [Reading/Math/Science/Social Studies]
- 🌴 A classroom and hallway transformed into a wilderness expedition
- 📓 An Explorer Journal for daily reflections
- 🏆 Survival badges, team challenges, and a Friday celebration

**How to support your explorer at home:**
1. **Plenty of sleep** — Explorers need rest for the journey! 8-10 hours nightly
2. **Ask about their terrain** — "What terrain did you survive today?" keeps the adventure alive
3. **Encourage review** — Your child may bring home a pocket "Survival Guide" — flip through it together
4. **Stay positive** — "You've been training all year. You're ready for any terrain!"
5. **Healthy meals** — Brain fuel for the expedition

Thank you for supporting your child's adventure! Please reach out with any questions.

Happy trails! 🏔️
