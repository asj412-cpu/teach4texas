# 🌿 STAAR-vival — Themed Activities

> 12 survival-themed activities. Includes the legendary Tribal Council game.

---

## 🎮 REVIEW GAMES

### 1. Tribal Council (End-of-Day Review Game)
**Type:** Whole class teams | **Time:** 10-15 min | **Best for:** Daily review (any subject)

**Setup:**
- Divide class into 3-4 "tribes" (table groups)
- Teacher is the "Council Leader"

**How to Play:**
1. Teacher reads a STAAR-format question aloud
2. Tribes huddle and discuss (60 seconds)
3. Each tribe holds up their answer (A/B/C/D card)
4. Correct tribes "survive." Wrong tribes get a "strike"
5. 3 strikes = tribe is "voted off the island" (they still play but can't win the final prize)
6. Last tribe standing wins daily Tribal Council

**Variations:**
- **Immunity Challenge:** One question where tribes can wager their strikes — get it right, remove a strike. Get it wrong, add one.
- **Hidden Immunity Idol:** One random question is a "gimme" — all tribes that attempt it get a strike removed
- **Merge:** On Friday, dissolve tribes. It's every explorer for themselves.

**Materials:** A/B/C/D cards (one set per tribe), questions, scoreboard for strikes

### 2. Wilderness Escape Room
**Type:** Small groups (3-4) | **Time:** 30-40 min | **Best for:** Mixed subject review

**Setup:**
- 5 "terrain locks" (envelopes), each with 4-5 questions
- Correct answers generate a code
- Final code unlocks the "escape from the wilderness" (prize envelope)

**How to Play:**
1. Groups work through Lock 1 (Jungle) → Lock 2 (Desert) → Lock 3 (Mountain) → Lock 4 (Ocean) → Lock 5 (Summit)
2. Must solve all questions correctly to get each code
3. Bring code to teacher to verify before moving to next lock
4. First group to crack all 5 and "escape" wins
5. All groups that finish earn "Escape Expert" badges

**Materials:** 5 envelopes per group, question sets, code answer key, prize envelope

---

### 3. Predator vs. Prey (Speed Review)
**Type:** Class game | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Vocabulary, facts, computation

**Setup:**
- Two students stand (Predator and Prey)
- Teacher asks a question
- First to answer correctly "survives" and stays standing
- Loser sits, next person stands as the new challenger

**How to Play:**
1. Predator and Prey face each other
2. Teacher asks a question
3. First correct answer = that student is the Predator (stays)
4. Loser becomes Prey and sits, next person in line stands
5. Whoever has the longest "survival streak" wins

**Materials:** Question list, optional bell/buzzer

## 👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

### 4. Explorer Journal (Daily Reflection)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 10-15 min | **Best for:** Writing practice, metacognition

**Instructions:**
Daily journal entries in their Explorer Journal (a booklet or stapled pages):
- **Monday (Jungle):** "Today I navigated through... The thickest part was... I found my way by..."
- **Tuesday (Desert):** "Resources are scarce. The math skill I need to conserve/strengthen is... My plan..."
- **Wednesday (Mountains):** "The steepest climb was... I conquered it by..."
- **Thursday (Ocean):** "The current pulled me toward... I stayed on course by..."
- **Friday (Summit):** "From the summit I can see everything I've learned... My advice to future explorers..."

**Materials:** Explorer Journal booklet (5 pages + cover), pencils

---

### 5. Survival Guide Creation (Study Tool)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 25 min | **Best for:** End-of-topic synthesis

**Instructions:**
Students create a pocket-sized "Survival Guide" — a folded booklet with essential knowledge organized by terrain:
- **Jungle Section (Reading):** Top 3 reading strategies
- **Desert Section (Math):** Key formulas and steps
- **Mountain Section (Science/SS):** Must-know facts
- **Ocean Section (Mixed):** Common mistakes to avoid
- **Summit Section (Strategies):** Test-day game plan

Fold a single sheet of paper into an 8-page mini-book (search "one-page book fold" for instructions).

**Materials:** 1 sheet of paper per student, scissors, pencils/markers

---

### 6. Terrain Mapping (Concept Web)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Organizing knowledge visually

**Instructions:**
Students draw a map with 5 terrain zones. In each zone, they place key concepts for that day's subject:
- Draw terrain features (trees for jungle, cacti for desert, etc.)
- Write concepts as "landmarks" within each terrain
- Draw paths connecting related concepts across terrains
- Add a "You Are Here" marker for their current confidence level

**Materials:** Large paper, colored pencils/markers

## 👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

### 7. Buddy System Navigation (Partner Problem Solving)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Math, multi-step reading questions

**Instructions:**
- Partners are "trail buddies" — they must stay together (agree on each answer before moving forward)
- Work through a set of 8 problems
- For each problem: discuss strategy FIRST (1 min), then solve together
- Both partners must be able to explain the answer — teacher randomly checks
- "In the wilderness, you never leave your buddy behind!"

**Materials:** Problem set, paper, pencils

---

### 8. SOS Signal (Partner Quiz Creation)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Any subject review

**Instructions:**
Each pair creates an "SOS Signal" — a set of 5 questions about their weakest topics (things they need to be "rescued" from). Then they swap with another pair and answer each other's SOS signals. If they can answer another pair's SOS, they've "rescued" them!

**Materials:** Paper, pencils

## 👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

### 9. Base Camp Stations (Rotation)
**Type:** Groups of 4-5 | **Time:** 30-35 min | **Best for:** Multi-topic review

**Setup:** 5 stations, each themed as a terrain:
- 🌴 **Jungle Station** — Reading comprehension questions
- 🏜️ **Desert Station** — Math computation and word problems
- ⛰️ **Mountain Station** — Science review
- 🌊 **Ocean Station** — Social studies / mixed review
- 🏔️ **Summit Station** — Test-taking strategy scenarios ("What would you do if...?")

Groups rotate every 6 minutes. Record answers on an expedition log sheet.

**Materials:** Station question sets, expedition log sheets, timer, terrain labels

---

### 10. Campfire Stories (Read-Aloud Strategy Tales)
**Type:** Whole class or small groups | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Test strategy review (Friday)

**Instructions:**
Teacher (or student volunteers) reads test-taking strategies as short adventure tales:

**Example:** *"Once, deep in the jungle of a reading passage, Explorer Maria was lost. The question asked for the 'main idea,' but every paragraph seemed to say something different. She was about to panic — but then she remembered her training. 'Check the first sentence of each paragraph,' her trail guide had taught her. She went back. She read each opening sentence. And there it was — a common thread running through every paragraph. The main idea emerged from the jungle like a clearing in the trees. Maria marked her answer with confidence and moved on."*

Write 5 of these (one per strategy). Students LOVE this format — it makes strategies memorable.

**Materials:** Pre-written strategy stories (teacher creates or adapts)

---

### 11. Survival Challenge Relay
**Type:** Teams of 5-6 | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Quick review, computation, vocabulary

**Instructions:**
Each team is a "survival crew." Line up relay-style.
1. First explorer runs to the front, grabs a "supply" (question card), answers it
2. Correct = bring the supply back to base camp. Wrong = put it back, next person tries
3. Team must collect all 6 supplies (answer all 6 questions) to "survive"
4. First team to collect all supplies wins

**Materials:** Question cards in envelopes, answer key

---

### 12. Expedition Map (Collaborative Poster)
**Type:** Groups of 3-4 | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Best for:** Synthesis, study guide creation

**Instructions:**
Each group creates a large illustrated "expedition map" for one subject/topic:
- Draw a path from "Start" to "Summit"
- Along the path, add key concepts as landmarks
- Add "Danger Zones" (common mistakes) with warning signs
- Add "Safe Havens" (helpful strategies) with shelter icons
- Add "Treasure" (bonus tips) hidden along the route

Post all maps on the wall — they become collaborative study guides.

**Materials:** Butcher paper or poster board, markers, textbooks/notes
