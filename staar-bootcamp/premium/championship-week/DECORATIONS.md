## 🏟️ HALLWAY TRANSFORMATION: Stadium Tunnel

### The Vision
Students walk through a "tunnel" into the stadium — just like athletes running onto the field. School colors, team banners, and a scoreboard create game-day energy.

### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Stadium Tunnel Entrance
- Hang **streamers** (school colors) from the ceiling/doorframe at the hallway entrance, creating a curtain students push through
- Alternative: cut black butcher paper into strips and hang like a curtain
- Add a banner above: **"CHAMPIONSHIP WEEK — STAAR ATHLETES ENTER HERE"**
- If allowed, play entrance music from a speaker as students walk through on Monday morning
- **Cost:** ~$3-5 for streamers

#### 2. Locker Room Door Signs
- Each classroom door gets a "locker" treatment:
  - Teacher name as "Head Coach [Name]"
  - Subject as position: "Offensive Coordinator — Mathematics"
  - Team logo (school mascot or custom design)
- Print on colored cardstock (school colors), laminate
- Optional: tape a jersey number above each door (large printed number)
- **Cost:** ~$2-3 for cardstock

#### 3. Hallway Scoreboard
- Create a large **scoreboard poster** in the hallway
- Title: "CHAMPIONSHIP SCOREBOARD"
- List each class period / homeroom as a "team"
- Track daily completion rates, participation, or scores
- Update daily — creates friendly competition between classes
- **Cost:** ~$2 for poster board

#### 4. Team Pennants
- Triangle pennants hung on a string across the hallway
- Each pennant = a subject or skill: "READING," "MATH," "SCIENCE," "VOCABULARY," etc.
- Use school colors for the paper
- String them with yarn or twine
- **Cost:** ~$3-5 for cardstock + yarn

#### 5. Floor Details
- Painter's tape "yard lines" on the hallway floor (every few feet, label 10, 20, 30... "GOAL LINE" at classroom doors)
- Or tape down paper "footprints" (cleats!) leading to each room
- **Cost:** ~$3 for painter's tape

## 🏠 CLASSROOM SETUP: Team Locker Room

### The Vision
Students walk into a locker room / training facility. Team pride, motivational quotes, and a tournament bracket dominate the walls.

### Door Sign
- **"TEAM [CLASS PERIOD] LOCKER ROOM — ATHLETES ONLY"**
- Print with school colors, sport graphics
- Add a jersey number (your room number!)

### Desk Name Tags (Jerseys)
- **Jersey-style name tags:** shaped like a jersey or T-shirt
- Front: Student name + jersey number (their student ID # or a random assignment)
- Back: Team name (their table group)
- Print on cardstock in school colors, fold into tent tags

### Wall Decorations

#### Tournament Bracket Poster
- Large bracket (Sweet 16 or Elite 8 format)
- Fill with skill names or subject areas
- Update daily as the class "advances" through the bracket
- THIS IS THE CENTERPIECE — make it big and visible

#### Coach's Chalkboard
- Dedicate whiteboard or poster space to daily strategy
- Title: "COACH'S PLAYBOOK — TODAY'S GAME PLAN"
- Update daily with the key strategy and motivational message

#### Motivational Sports Quotes
Print and display:
- "Hard work beats talent when talent doesn't work hard."
- "Champions are made when nobody is watching." — adapted
- "There's no 'I' in TEAM, but there IS in WIN."
- "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take." — Wayne Gretzky
- "It's not whether you get knocked down. It's whether you get up." — Vince Lombardi

#### Scoreboard (In-Class)
- Track team (table group) points throughout the week
- Award points for: completed work, participation, teamwork, bonus challenges
- Friday winner = "Class Champions"

### Room Arrangement
- Desk clusters = team huddle formation
- Label each cluster with a team name and pennant
- Teacher desk = "Coach's Office"
