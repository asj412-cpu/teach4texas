## General Video Strategy Notes

### Posting Schedule
- **TikTok:** 3-5x per week (algorithm rewards consistency)
- **Instagram Reels:** 3-4x per week (cross-post TikToks, remove watermark)
- **Best times:** 6-8 AM (teachers before school), 4-6 PM (after school), 8-10 PM (evening scroll)

### Content Mix
- 40% value/educational (tips, strategies)
- 30% product-related (previews, demos)
- 20% personality/humor (POV, GRWM, reactions)
- 10% trending/timely (STAAR countdown, seasonal)

### Hashtags for Video
Always include: `#texasteacher` `#teachertok` `#teachersoftiktok`
Rotate: `#tia` `#staarprep` `#ttess` `#teachertips` `#classroomhacks` `#teacherlife` `#tptresources`

### Key Tips
1. **First 3 seconds decide everything.** Hook must create curiosity or tension.
2. **Text on screen** — many watch without sound. Every key point should be readable.
3. **End with a question or CTA** — comments boost the algorithm.
4. **Repurpose:** Each video can become a carousel post (IG), a pin (Pinterest), and a Facebook post.
5. **Batch film:** Film 5-7 videos in one sitting. Edit and schedule throughout the week.
6. **Show your face** — personal connection drives teacher audiences. Faceless accounts grow slower.
7. **Use trending sounds** but make the content evergreen — the sound gets you discovered, the content gets you followed.
