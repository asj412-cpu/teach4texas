## 1. Profile Setup

Go to **Settings > Profile** (pinterest.com/settings/profile).

**Display Name:**
```
Teach4Texas | STAAR Prep & TIA Resources
```

**About (Bio):**
```
Helping Texas teachers crush STAAR, ace TIA, and save time. Free resources, test prep strategies, data tracking tools, and classroom hacks for grades 3-8. Download ready-to-use activities at teach4texas.com
```

**Website:** `https://teach4texas.com`

**Profile Category:** Education

**Username:** `teach4texas` (should already be set)

After saving, click **Claim Website** under Settings > Claimed Accounts and follow the steps to verify teach4texas.com. This unlocks analytics on every pin linking to your site.

## 4. Posting Schedule

Pinterest rewards consistency. Follow this schedule:

| Day       | Pins to Post | Best Time          |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Sunday    | 5-6 pins    | 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM  |
| Monday    | 3-4 pins    | 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM |
| Tuesday   | 3-4 pins    | 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM |
| Wednesday | 3-4 pins    | 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM |
| Thursday  | 3-4 pins    | 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM |
| Friday    | 2-3 pins    | 6:00 AM - 7:00 AM  |
| Saturday  | 3-4 pins    | 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM |

**Weekly total: 22-29 pins**

### Posting Rules

1. **Never post more than 1 pin to the same board in a single hour.** Space them out.
2. **Mix pin types:** your own content (linking to teach4texas.com) and repins of other Texas teacher content. Aim for 60% original, 40% repins.
3. **Sunday evenings** are peak teacher-Pinterest time. Front-load your best pins here.
4. **Early morning Friday** (6-7 AM) catches teachers planning for the next week.
5. **Use Tailwind or Pinterest's built-in scheduler** to queue pins in advance. Batch-create on Sunday, schedule for the whole week.

### Content Rotation

Each week, aim for this mix across all boards:
- 3-4 pins to STAAR Test Prep Strategies
- 2-3 pins to the subject-specific boards (Math, RLA, Science)
- 2-3 pins to TIA or T-TESS boards
- 2-3 pins to Texas Teacher Tips & Hacks
- 2-3 pins to Free Teacher Resources
- 2-3 pins to Student Data & Growth Tracking
- Remaining pins = repins from other creators into relevant boards

### Pin Image Tips

- **Size:** 1000 x 1500 px (2:3 ratio) -- this is non-negotiable
- **Text overlay:** Bold, readable title on every pin image
- **Colors:** Use consistent brand colors from teach4texas.com
- **Templates:** Create 3-4 Canva templates and rotate them
- **File names:** Name image files with keywords before uploading (e.g., `staar-math-review-games-3rd-grade.png`)
