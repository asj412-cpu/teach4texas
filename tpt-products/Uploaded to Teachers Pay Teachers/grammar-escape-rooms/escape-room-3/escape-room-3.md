## 🪵 CHALLENGE 1: The Log Lagoon (Subordinating Conjunctions)

### 📌 SLIDE 4 — Challenge 1 Story
**Visual:** A lagoon with floating logs. A wise old sea turtle blocks the path. Lush greenery surrounds the water.

**Text:**
> ## 🪵 Challenge 1: The Log Lagoon
> 
> Old Shelly the Sea Turtle guards the logs you need!
> 
> 🐢 "I'll give you the logs, but first you must show me you understand **subordinating conjunctions** — the words that connect a dependent clause to an independent clause!"
> 
> 🧠 **Remember:** Subordinating conjunctions include: *because, although, when, while, since, if, before, after, until, unless*
> 
> They create **complex sentences**: "**Although** it was raining, we played outside."

### 📌 SLIDE 5 — Challenge 1 Questions
**Text:**
> ## 🪵 Challenge 1: Subordinating Conjunctions
> 
> **Choose the correct subordinating conjunction to complete each sentence.**
> 
> **1.** ___ the sun went down, the temperature dropped quickly.
> - A) But
> - B) After ✅
> - C) And
> 
> **2.** We can't go swimming ___ the lifeguard arrives.
> - A) until ✅
> - B) and
> - C) so
> 
> **3.** ___ she studied all night, she still felt nervous about the test.
> - A) Because
> - B) Although ✅
> - C) When
> 
> **4.** The dog hides under the bed ___ there is thunder.
> - A) unless
> - B) before
> - C) whenever ✅
> 
> **5.** You should wear a jacket ___ it is cold outside.
> - A) but
> - B) since ✅
> - C) or

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-B, 4-C, 5-B

### 📌 SLIDE 5B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🐢 Shelly's Hint:
> "A subordinating conjunction connects a DEPENDENT clause (can't stand alone) to an INDEPENDENT clause (complete thought). Read the sentence — which word makes BOTH parts connect logically? Try each option and see which makes the most sense!"

## 🧵 CHALLENGE 2: The Rope Bridge (Coordinating Conjunctions & Compound Sentences)

### 📌 SLIDE 7 — Challenge 2 Story
**Visual:** A rickety rope bridge over a ravine. A cheeky parrot sits on a post, holding rope in its claws.

**Text:**
> ## 🧵 Challenge 2: The Rope Bridge
> 
> Polly the Parrot has the rope, but she wants to test you first!
> 
> 🦜 "SQUAWK! Coordinating conjunctions! FANBOYS! Show me you can combine sentences properly, and the rope is yours!"
> 
> 🧠 **FANBOYS:** For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So
> These join two INDEPENDENT clauses with a comma: "I was tired**,** but I kept running."

### 📌 SLIDE 8 — Challenge 2 Questions
**Text:**
> ## 🧵 Challenge 2: Compound Sentences with FANBOYS
> 
> **Combine the sentences using the best coordinating conjunction. Choose the correct answer.**
> 
> **1.** "I wanted pizza. The restaurant was closed."
> - A) I wanted pizza, and the restaurant was closed.
> - B) I wanted pizza, but the restaurant was closed. ✅
> - C) I wanted pizza, so the restaurant was closed.
> 
> **2.** "She practiced every day. She made the team."
> - A) She practiced every day, yet she made the team.
> - B) She practiced every day, but she made the team.
> - C) She practiced every day, so she made the team. ✅
> 
> **3.** "We could go to the movies. We could stay home and play games."
> - A) We could go to the movies, or we could stay home and play games. ✅
> - B) We could go to the movies, so we could stay home and play games.
> - C) We could go to the movies, for we could stay home and play games.
> 
> **4.** "It was raining hard. We still went to the park."
> - A) It was raining hard, so we still went to the park.
> - B) It was raining hard, yet we still went to the park. ✅
> - C) It was raining hard, and we still went to the park.

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-A, 4-B

### 📌 SLIDE 8B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🦜 Polly's Hint:
> "SQUAWK! Think about the RELATIONSHIP between the two sentences! Contrast/surprise = BUT or YET. Cause/effect = SO. Choice = OR. Adding info = AND. Read each option aloud — which one MAKES SENSE?"

> 🦜 "SQUAWK! You're smarter than you look! Take the rope!"

## ⛵ CHALLENGE 3: The Sail Cave (Prepositional Phrases)

### 📌 SLIDE 10 — Challenge 3 Story
**Visual:** A dim cave with a tattered sail hanging from the ceiling. A crab with glowing eyes guards it. Treasure scattered around.

**Text:**
> ## ⛵ Challenge 3: The Sail Cave
> 
> Clawdius the Crab guards the sail deep in a cave!
> 
> 🦀 "I'll trade you this sail for some grammar knowledge! Show me you can identify **prepositional phrases** — groups of words that start with a preposition and end with a noun!"
> 
> 🧠 **Common Prepositions:** in, on, at, by, for, with, about, under, over, through, between, during, before, after
> 
> **Prepositional Phrase = preposition + noun (object)**
> "The cat slept **under the warm blanket**."

### 📌 SLIDE 11 — Challenge 3 Questions
**Text:**
> ## ⛵ Challenge 3: Prepositional Phrases
> 
> **Identify the prepositional phrase in each sentence.**
> 
> **1.** The bird flew over the tall trees.
> - A) The bird flew
> - B) over the tall trees ✅
> - C) flew over
> 
> **2.** She left her backpack under the desk.
> - A) She left her
> - B) backpack under
> - C) under the desk ✅
> 
> **3.** During the storm, the power went out.
> - A) During the storm ✅
> - B) the power went
> - C) went out
> 
> **4.** The secret passage led through the old tunnel.
> - A) The secret passage
> - B) passage led through
> - C) through the old tunnel ✅
> 
> **5.** We ate lunch at the new restaurant by the lake.
> - A) at the new restaurant ✅ (also accept "by the lake")
> - B) We ate lunch
> - C) the new restaurant

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-A, 4-C, 5-A

### 📌 SLIDE 11B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🦀 Clawdius's Hint:
> "A prepositional phrase STARTS with a preposition (in, on, under, over, through, during, at, by...) and ENDS with a noun. It answers WHERE, WHEN, or HOW. Find the preposition first, then grab everything until the next noun!"

> 🦀 "Not bad for a landlubber! Take the sail and this coordinate!"

## 🗺️ CHALLENGE 4: The Map Mountain (Irregular Past Tense Verbs)

### 📌 SLIDE 13 — Challenge 4 Story
**Visual:** A misty mountain peak with a treasure map pinned to a rock at the top. Mountain goat character wearing glasses.

**Text:**
> ## 🗺️ Challenge 4: The Map Mountain
> 
> Professor Goat has the map, but he's a stickler for grammar!
> 
> 🐐 "Irregular verbs! They DON'T follow the normal '-ed' rule for past tense. You must KNOW them! Prove your knowledge and the map is yours!"
> 
> 🧠 **Regular:** walk → walked, play → played (just add -ed)
> **Irregular:** go → went, run → ran, write → wrote (they CHANGE!)

### 📌 SLIDE 14 — Challenge 4 Questions
**Text:**
> ## 🗺️ Challenge 4: Irregular Past Tense
> 
> **Choose the correct PAST TENSE form of the verb.**
> 
> **1.** Yesterday, I ___ (drink) three glasses of water.
> - A) drinked
> - B) drank ✅
> - C) drunk
> 
> **2.** She ___ (bring) her favorite book to school.
> - A) bringed
> - B) brang
> - C) brought ✅
> 
> **3.** The bird ___ (fly) south for the winter.
> - A) flyed
> - B) flew ✅
> - C) flown
> 
> **4.** He ___ (teach) us a new song last week.
> - A) teached
> - B) tought
> - C) taught ✅
> 
> **5.** They ___ (swim) across the lake in record time.
> - A) swam ✅
> - B) swimmed
> - C) swum

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-B, 4-C, 5-A

### 📌 SLIDE 14B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🐐 Professor Goat's Hint:
> "Irregular verbs are tricky because you can't just add '-ed'! If an answer has '-ed' added to an irregular verb (like 'drinked' or 'bringed'), it's WRONG. These verbs change their spelling entirely. When in doubt, say it aloud — does it SOUND right?"
