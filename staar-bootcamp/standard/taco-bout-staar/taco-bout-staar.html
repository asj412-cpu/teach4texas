🌮 MONDAY — Appetizers

Theme: “Every great meal starts with appetizers. Today we set the table with our ELA foundations.”

Daily Specials (10 min)

“Welcome to the STAAR Kitchen, chefs! I’m Head Chef [Name], and this week we’re cooking up something special — a STAAR-ready feast! Today we start with appetizers. Appetizers are light, essential, and set the tone for the whole meal. Just like reading skills set the tone for the whole STAAR. Let’s taco ’bout getting ready! 🌮”

  • Hand out Recipe Cards (see STUDENT-MATERIALS.md)
  • Post the “Daily Specials” menu board (list today’s learning objectives as menu items)
  • Explain difficulty levels: 🌶️ Mild (basic), 🌶️🌶️ Medium (on grade level), 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy (challenge)

Cooking Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Taste Test” Warm-Up — 5 quick questions. “Sample these appetizer questions to warm up your palate!”
  2. (20 min) “Recipe Demonstration” — Teacher models reading strategies like a cooking show. “First, we ADD careful reading. Then we STIR in annotations. We FOLD in evidence from the text. And VOILÀ — a perfect answer!”
  3. (15 min) “Sous Chef Practice” — Partners work through a passage together. “Every Head Chef needs a Sous Chef!”

Snack Break (5 min)

  • “Taco Dance” — play a fun Latin/fiesta song, students dance for 90 seconds. “Chefs need to move!”

Cooking Session 2 (40 min)

“Appetizer Sampler” — ELA Packet - STAAR-format reading questions - Three difficulty levels clearly marked: 🌶️ 🌶️🌶️ 🌶️🌶️🌶️ - Students must complete Mild + Medium; Spicy is optional “Extra Credit Salsa”

Differentiation: Built into the 3-level system! - Struggling students focus on 🌶️ Mild - On-level students complete 🌶️🌶️ Medium - Advanced students tackle 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy

Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)

  • Review key questions: “Let’s clean up any confusion…”
  • Chef’s Tip #1: Post on board — “Always go back to the text. The recipe is IN the passage!”
  • Stamp recipe cards: “Appetizer ingredient: READING ✓”

🌮 TUESDAY — Main Course

Theme: “The main course is the heart of any meal. Math is the main course of the STAAR.”

Daily Specials (10 min)

“Chefs! Yesterday’s appetizers were chef’s kiss 👨‍🍳. Today: the MAIN COURSE. Math. This is the hearty, filling, most important part of the meal. You can’t have a fiesta without the main course, and you can’t ace the STAAR without math skills. Let’s cook!”

Cooking Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Ingredient Check” — Quick computation warm-up. “Make sure your ingredients (basic skills) are fresh!”
  2. (20 min) “Cooking Demo” — Teacher models word problems. “Just like following a recipe — read ALL the steps before you start. Don’t skip ingredients!”
  3. (15 min) “Kitchen Partners” — Partners solve problems, checking each other’s “recipes” (work shown)

Snack Break (5 min)

  • “This or That: Food Edition” — Would you rather eat tacos or pizza? Stand on left or right side. Quick fun reset.

Cooking Session 2 (40 min)

“Main Course Platter” — Math Packet - Three difficulty levels: 🌶️ 🌶️🌶️ 🌶️🌶️🌶️ - “This is a BIG serving of math. Pace yourself — don’t eat too fast!”

Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)

  • Chef’s Tip #2: “Show ALL your work. A chef who skips steps gets a bad dish. A student who skips steps gets a wrong answer.”
  • Stamp recipe cards: “Main Course ingredient: MATH ✓”

🌮 WEDNESDAY — Nacho Average Review Day 🧀

Theme: “This is NACHO AVERAGE review day! Today we go beyond the basics with science and social studies.”

Daily Specials (10 min)

“Chefs, this is NOT your average review day. This is NACHO Average Review Day! 🧀 Today we add the special flavors — science and social studies. These are the toppings that make the meal memorable. Plus, it’s the middle of the week, so we’re going to have some FUN while we review. Let’s spice it up!”

Cooking Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (10 min) “Salsa Sampler” — Rapid-fire science/SS questions
  2. (20 min) “Food Truck Stations” — 4 stations around the room, each a different “food truck” serving a different subject:
    • 🌮 Taco Truck (Reading review)
    • 🍕 Pizza Stand (Math review)
    • 🌶️ Salsa Station (Science)
    • 🧀 Nacho Bar (Social Studies) Groups rotate every 5 min.
  3. (15 min) “Recipe Swap” — Students share their best study tips with a partner, then trade tips with a new partner. “Chefs share recipes!”

Snack Break (5 min)

  • Actual snack if possible! Even small crackers or a piece of candy. “Nacho Average snack break!”

Cooking Session 2 (40 min)

“Nacho Platter” — Mixed Review (shorter) - 10-12 mixed questions - Leave time for the review game:

(15 min) “Salsa Showdown” Review Game - Teams compete. Teacher asks question. First team to answer correctly “adds salsa” to their nacho plate (earns a point). Team with the most loaded nachos wins.

Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)

  • Chef’s Tip #3: “When you don’t know an answer, eliminate what doesn’t taste right. Cross out the options that don’t make sense!”
  • Stamp recipe cards: “Special Ingredient: KNOWLEDGE ✓”
  • Midweek treat! 🎉

🌮 THURSDAY — Taste Test

Theme: “Before any chef serves their dish, they do a taste test. Today we taste-test our STAAR skills.”

Daily Specials (10 min)

“Chefs, every great chef does a taste test before serving. You don’t send a dish to the table without trying it first. Today is your taste test — a full practice run. We’re tasting EVERYTHING — all subjects, all skills. If something tastes off, we’ll adjust the recipe before the big fiesta.”

Cooking Session 1 (45 min)

“Taste Test” — Mini Practice Test - 20-30 mixed STAAR-format questions - Test conditions: silent, independent, timed - “Taste carefully. Don’t rush.”

Snack Break (5 min)

  • Quiet stretch.

Cooking Session 2 (40 min)

  1. (15 min) Review answers. “How did it taste? What needs more seasoning?”
  2. (10 min) Self-assessment: students identify their “undercooked” areas
  3. (15 min) Small group “recipe fix” — targeted review by need

Kitchen Cleanup (10 min)

  • Chef’s Tip #4: “If a question is too tough, skip it and come back. Don’t let one bad dish ruin the whole meal.”
  • Stamp recipe cards: “Quality Control: PRACTICE ✓”

🌮 FRIDAY — Fiesta Friday! 🎉

Theme: “The fiesta is here! Celebrate how far you’ve come. You’re cooked to perfection!”

Daily Specials (10 min)

“CHEFS! It’s FIESTA FRIDAY! You’ve cooked appetizers, the main course, nacho-level reviews, and passed the taste test. Today we celebrate! But first, a few final recipes to seal in the flavor. Then… WE PARTY.”

Cooking Session 1 (45 min)

  1. (15 min) “Chef’s Secret Recipes” — Top 10 test-taking strategies presented as cooking tips:
    • “Read the recipe twice before cooking” = Read the question twice
    • “Don’t add ingredients that aren’t in the recipe” = Don’t add info that’s not in the text
    • “Taste as you go” = Check your work as you go
  2. (15 min) “Mystery Ingredient Challenge” — Fun activity: each table gets a “mystery” question type, must explain the strategy to solve it to the class
  3. **(15 min) Review game of teacher’s choice

Snack Break (5 min)

Cooking Session 2: FIESTA! (40 min)

  1. (10 min) “Recipe for Success Letter” — Students write their personal “recipe” for test day
  2. (10 min) “Taco Champion” Certificate Ceremony — Award certificates with flair!
  3. (10 min) Students sign each other’s recipe cards with encouraging messages
  4. (10 min) Party! Music, treats (tacos if you’re amazing, otherwise any snacks), celebration

Final Kitchen Cleanup

  • Chef’s Tip #5: “You ARE the recipe for success. All the ingredients are inside you.”
  • All 5 recipe card stamps = “Master Chef” status
  • “Let’s taco ’bout how READY you are!” 🌮🎉

🌮 Taco ’Bout STAAR Ready — Themed Activities

10 food/fiesta-themed activities with 3 review games.

🎮 REVIEW GAMES

1. Salsa Showdown (Team Competition)

Type: Teams | Time: 20 min | Best for: Multi-subject review

How to Play: - 3-4 teams. Teacher asks STAAR questions. - First team to answer correctly “adds salsa” (earns a point) - Track on a scoreboard with salsa jars (tally marks = salsa scoops) - Every 5th question is a “GHOST PEPPER” — worth 3 points but if wrong, lose 2 - Team with the most salsa wins!

Materials: Questions, scoreboard with salsa jar drawings

2. Recipe Relay (Team Relay Race)

Type: Teams of 5-6 | Time: 15 min | Best for: Quick computation, vocabulary

How to Play: - Each team has a “recipe” (set of 6 questions) at the front - First chef runs up, answers question 1 (an “ingredient”), runs back - Next chef adds ingredient 2 (answers question 2) - If an ingredient is wrong (answer incorrect), the “dish is ruined” — next person must fix it - First team to complete their recipe correctly wins

Materials: Question sets, answer key

3. Food Truck Challenge (Station Game)

Type: Groups | Time: 25-30 min | Best for: Mixed review

Setup: 4-5 “food trucks” (stations) around the room: - 🌮 Taco Truck — Reading questions - 🍕 Pizza Stand — Math problems - 🌶️ Hot Sauce Station — Spicy challenge problems (hard) - 🧀 Nacho Bar — Science/SS questions - 🍩 Donut Shop — Fun bonus challenges

Groups rotate every 5-6 minutes. Complete the “order” (question set) at each truck. Record on an “Order Ticket” sheet.

Materials: Station questions, order ticket recording sheets, timer, truck labels

👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

4. Recipe for Success (Study Guide)

Type: Individual | Time: 20 min | Best for: Synthesis

Instructions: Students write their personal “Recipe for STAAR Success”: - Ingredients: List skills/concepts they need (e.g., “1 cup of main idea skills, 2 tbsp of fraction knowledge”) - Directions: Step-by-step test-day plan (“Step 1: Read the question first. Step 2: Eliminate 2 wrong answers…”) - Cooking Time: How long to spend on each section - Chef’s Notes: Personal reminders and tips

Written on a recipe card template.

Materials: Recipe card template (printed), pencils, colored markers

5. Extra Credit Salsa (Bonus Challenge)

Type: Individual | Time: Varies (ongoing) | Best for: Early finishers, motivated students

Description: A set of extra-spicy 🌶️🌶️🌶️ challenge problems available all week. Students who complete them earn “Extra Credit Salsa” — a special stamp/sticker on their recipe card.

Keep 10-15 challenge problems in a folder labeled “EXTRA CREDIT SALSA — Handle with Care!” Students grab one when they finish early.

Materials: Folder with challenge problems, stamps/stickers

6. Menu Creation (Vocabulary Organizer)

Type: Individual | Time: 15 min | Best for: Vocabulary review

Instructions: Students create a “restaurant menu” for their subject: - Appetizers: Easy concepts (define and give example) - Entrées: Medium concepts - Desserts: The satisfying stuff (things they’ve mastered) - Daily Specials: Concepts they’re still learning - Include “prices” (difficulty ratings: $=easy,  = medium,$=hard)

Materials: Paper folded into menu format, pencils/markers

👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

7. Sous Chef Partners (Guided Partner Practice)

Type: Partner | Time: 20 min | Best for: Any subject

Instructions: - Partner A = Head Chef (solves the problem) - Partner B = Sous Chef (watches, asks “What’s your next step?”, checks work) - Switch roles every 2-3 problems - Both must agree on every answer before moving on

Materials: Problem set, pencils

8. Taste Test Partners (Quick Quiz)

Type: Partner | Time: 15 min | Best for: Fact review, vocabulary

Instructions: Partners quiz each other using flashcards or question strips: - Round 1 (2 min): Partner A quizzes B — “Taste this!” (rapid fire) - Round 2 (2 min): Switch - Round 3 (2 min): Both solve problems together — “Let’s cook this one up!” - Track correct answers. Try to beat your own score each round.

Materials: Flashcards/question strips, timer

👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

9. Iron Chef Challenge (Group Competition)

Type: Groups of 4 | Time: 25 min | Best for: Complex problems

Instructions: Each group gets one complex, multi-part problem (the “secret ingredient”). They have 15 minutes to create the best “dish” (solution), including: - Complete solution with all work shown - Explanation of their strategy - Presentation to the class (2 min each)

Class votes on the best “dish” (clearest explanation). Winning team = Iron Chefs!

Materials: Complex problems, chart paper, markers

10. Potluck Review (Collaborative Study Guide)

Type: Groups of 3-4 | Time: 25 min | Best for: End-of-week synthesis

Instructions: Each group brings a “dish” to the class potluck — a poster covering one topic/skill: - 5 key vocabulary terms - 3 example problems with solutions - 2 common mistakes to avoid - 1 “secret ingredient” (unique tip)

Post all posters. Students do a “potluck walk” — visit each poster and take notes on anything they didn’t know.

Materials: Poster paper/butcher paper, markers, textbooks

Door Decoration: Paper Plate Taco

  • Take 2 large paper plates, fold in half, staple together at the bottom
  • Stuff with colored tissue paper “ingredients”: brown (meat), yellow (cheese), green (lettuce), red (tomato)
  • Add a sign above: “TACO ’BOUT STAAR READY!”
  • Takes 10 minutes. Kids LOVE it.
  • Cost: $1-2 (paper plates + tissue paper)

Daily Specials Menu Board

  • Dedicate a whiteboard section or poster to the “Daily Specials”
  • Each morning, write the day’s learning objectives as menu items:
    • “Today’s Special: Main Idea Tacos with a side of Inference Salsa”
    • “Chef’s Recommendation: Show Your Work Burritos”
  • Fun and functional — students actually read it!
  • Cost: $0 (use existing whiteboard) or $1 for poster board

Fiesta Banner

  • String of colorful pennant flags across the front of the room
  • Cut triangles from colored construction paper (or buy pre-made)
  • Or use a string of small paper plates colored in bright fiesta colors
  • Cost: $2-3 for construction paper + yarn
