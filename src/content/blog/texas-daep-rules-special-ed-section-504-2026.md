> **General information, not legal advice.** This post summarizes adopted commissioner rules. Discipline, special education, and Section 504 decisions are local and fact-specific. Read the official rule text linked below and ask your district if you have questions.

TEA has adopted amendments to 19 TAC §§103.1201, 103.1203, and 103.1205, the commissioner rules for Disciplinary Alternative Education Programs (DAEPs). TEA lists **November 18, 2026** as the effective date, pending confirmation in the November 13, 2026 Texas Register. The amendments align those rules with House Bill 114 (88th Legislature, 2023) and House Bill 6 (89th Legislature, 2025). This post covers the adopted commissioner rules, not the bills themselves ([TEA adopted rules not yet effective](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/adopted-commissioner-education-rules-not-yet-effective)).

## What’s changing, and what isn’t

The rules were proposed in the June 26, 2026 Texas Register (51 TexReg 4070), with comment through July 27, 2026. §103.1201 was adopted with changes, and §§103.1203 and 103.1205 were adopted without changes ([TEA adopted preamble](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc.pdf)).

This adoption did not create the 1:15 ratio, the 240-minute DAEP day, elementary and secondary separation, the 90-school-day pre- and post-assessment, or the related home-campus reporting, graduation-plan, course-completion, and TEC §37.023 transition rules. Those remain in the rule ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf), [TEA compiled Chapter 103 CC, September 2026 Update](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-tac-currently-effect/ch103cc.pdf)).

## Special education and Section 504 in DAEPs

Adopted §103.1201(f) requires an academic and self-discipline program that leads to graduation and includes each student’s currently enrolled foundation-curriculum instruction, “including special education and related services and Section 504 accommodations” ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)). TEA made that wording change at adoption after public comment, including support from Disability Rights Texas. The compiled rule said only “including special education services” ([TEA adopted preamble](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc.pdf), [TEA compiled Chapter 103 CC, September 2026 Update](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-tac-currently-effect/ch103cc.pdf)).

## Keeping DAEP students separate from ISS

Adopted §103.1201(h)(3) says students in the DAEP “shall be separated from students in a juvenile justice alternative education program or in-school suspension and students who are not assigned to the DAEP” ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)). The compiled rule did not mention in-school suspension ([TEA compiled Chapter 103 CC, September 2026 Update](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-tac-currently-effect/ch103cc.pdf)).

## Staff training

Adopted §103.1201(i) covers education, behavior management, and safety; education and discipline of students with disabilities who receive special education or Section 504 services; social skills and problem-solving (diversity, dating violence, anger management, bullying, and conflict resolution); annual suicide-prevention training and annual training on procedures for reporting abuse, neglect, or exploitation of students; and any other required local, state, or federal training ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)). New compared with the compiled rule: Section 504, bullying, annual suicide-prevention training, and other required training ([TEA compiled Chapter 103 CC, September 2026 Update](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-tac-currently-effect/ch103cc.pdf)). The 24-hour abuse reporting window is a different rule (19 TAC §§103.1401 and 103.1402). We cover that in [our Texas child-abuse reporting guide](/blog/texas-child-abuse-reporting-24-hours-tea-rule-2026).

If a DAEP student engages in behavior that results in expulsion, the adopted rule says that student “shall be afforded due process in accordance with TEC, §37.009(f)” ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)). This post does not describe those hearing steps. Read [TEC §37.009](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=ED&Value=37.009) and ask your district.

## Academic-growth testing for placements of 90 school days or longer

For placements of 90 school days or longer, the district must still give a pre- and post-assessment in reading and mathematics (TEC §37.0082). Released state assessments may be used, and adopted §103.1203(a) also allows a district’s EOC exams. Fall or first-semester placements use the grade just completed; spring or second-semester placements use the current grade. The home campus must get an academic report within 10 school days after the post-assessment. Adopted §103.1203(d) requires appropriate accommodations and modifications for special education or Section 504 as needed. DAEP students must also be assessed under TEC Chapter 39 ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

A high school personal graduation plan “may not be altered when the student is assigned to a DAEP.” The student must be offered a free chance, before the next school year, to complete a foundation-curriculum course they were enrolled in at removal (correspondence, distance learning, or summer school). Transition still follows TEC §37.023, and the campus of accountability remains the locally assigned campus ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

## “Violent conduct” when the DAEP is full

Since the October 29, 2024 adoption of §103.1205, the rule has let a student placed for offenses involving marihuana, e-cigarettes, alcoholic beverages, or abusable volatile chemicals (TEC §37.006(a)(2)(C-1), (C-2), (D), or (E)) be moved to ISS to open a DAEP seat for a student who engaged in violent conduct. The definition of “violent conduct” in §103.1205(b) is unchanged ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf), [TEA compiled Chapter 103 CC, September 2026 Update](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-tac-currently-effect/ch103cc.pdf)).

Here's what's new. Adopted §103.1205(c) lets a campus behavior coordinator or an appropriate administrator decide whether conduct listed in paragraphs (1)-(9) rises to “violent conduct” for DAEP placement. The compiled rule authorized only a campus behavior coordinator and listed paragraphs (1)-(6). TEA describes the change as a reorganization to conform with HB 6’s repeal of TEC §37.007(d). Use the adopted (1)-(9) list in the rule text, not the old (1)-(6) citations ([TEA adopted preamble](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc.pdf), [TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

## What to do now

1. TEA lists November 18, 2026 as the effective date, pending Texas Register confirmation in the November 13, 2026 issue ([TEA adopted rules not yet effective](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/adopted-commissioner-education-rules-not-yet-effective)).
2. If you teach in a DAEP, confirm that special education and Section 504 services are in place and that DAEP students are kept separate from ISS ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).
3. Ask when updated DAEP staff training will be offered, including Section 504, bullying, and annual suicide-prevention training ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).
4. Campus behavior coordinators and appropriate administrators should use the adopted (1)-(9) list when the DAEP is at capacity ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

## Key dates

- **June 26–July 27, 2026:** proposed (51 TexReg 4070) and open for comment ([TEA adopted preamble](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc.pdf)).
- **November 13, 2026:** next Texas Register after the November 6 issue, when the adopted notice can be confirmed.
- **November 18, 2026:** effective date listed by TEA, pending that Register confirmation ([TEA adopted rules not yet effective](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/adopted-commissioner-education-rules-not-yet-effective)).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do the new DAEP commissioner rules take effect?

TEA lists November 18, 2026, pending confirmation in the November 13, 2026 Texas Register ([TEA adopted rules not yet effective](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/adopted-commissioner-education-rules-not-yet-effective)).

### Do DAEPs have to serve students with Section 504 plans?

Yes. Adopted §103.1201(f) says the academic program includes “special education and related services and Section 504 accommodations.” TEA made that wording change at adoption after public comment ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf), [TEA adopted preamble](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc.pdf)).

### What new training do DAEP staff need?

The adopted training list adds Section 504 (alongside special education), bullying as a social-skills topic, annual suicide-prevention training (alongside annual abuse, neglect, or exploitation reporting-procedure training), and any other required local, state, or federal training ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

### Can a district use its EOC exams to measure academic growth in DAEP?

Yes. Adopted §103.1203(a) allows released state reading and math assessments “as well as a district's end-of-course (EOC) exams” for placements of 90 school days or longer ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).

### Who decides “violent conduct” when the DAEP is full?

A campus behavior coordinator or an appropriate administrator, using the adopted §103.1205(c)(1)-(9) list ([TEA adopted rule text](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/coe-adopted/26-10-103cc-rule-adopt-with-changes.pdf)).
