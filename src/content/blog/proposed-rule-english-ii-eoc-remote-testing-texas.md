> **General information, not legal advice.** This post summarizes a *proposed* rule. It has not been adopted, and its final text could change. Check the official sources linked below before making decisions.

The Texas Education Agency has published a **proposed** amendment to 19 TAC §101.3011, the commissioner's rule on how state assessments are implemented and given. It appeared in the [Texas Register on October 16, 2026](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html). It does two things: it phases the English II end-of-course (EOC) assessment out of the rule after the 2026-27 school year, and it lets eligible full-time virtual campuses and programs give state tests remotely ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html); [TEA proposed rules page](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/proposed-commissioner-education-rules)).

None of this is final yet. Here's what the proposal says, when it would kick in, and what you can do right now.

## Where this comes from

The English II change follows a law that has already passed. According to TEA's notice, HB 8 (89th Legislature, 2nd Called Session, 2025) amended TEC §39.023(c) to remove English II from the list of required high school EOC assessments, and HB 8 set up a new statewide assessment program starting in the 2027-28 school year ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). We covered the bigger picture in [STAAR Is Going Away: What the Student Success Tool Means for Your Classroom](/blog/staar-replacement-student-success-tool-texas), so this post sticks to what this rule would do.

The remote-testing change follows SB 569 (89th Legislature, Regular Session, 2025). That bill repealed TEC Chapter 30A (the Texas Virtual School Network), created TEC Chapter 30B as a new framework for virtual and hybrid education, and gave existing virtual courses, programs and campuses a transition period ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

## What the proposed rule would change

### 1. English II EOC: given in 2026-27, then discontinued

TEA's notice says the proposed amendment to §101.3011(b) "would specify that the English II end-of-course assessment for students subject to assessment requirements remains in effect only through the 2026-2027 school year. Beginning in the 2027-2028 school year, the English II end-of-course assessment will be discontinued" ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

In the proposed rule text, English II comes off the general list of EOC alternative assessment instruments (English I, Algebra I, biology and U.S. history stay). A new item keeps the English II alternative assessment "for eligible students through the 2026-2027 school year" ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

### 2. Remote testing for full-time virtual schools

The proposed §101.3011(c) would let these entities give the state assessments required under TEC Chapter 39, Subchapter B remotely, following the procedures in TEA's test administration materials ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)):

- a full-time virtual campus as defined by TEC §30B.001(3);
- a full-time virtual program as defined by TEC §30B.001(4); and
- a full-time virtual campus of a district or open-enrollment charter school that, in 2026-27, operates under TEC Chapter 30A as it existed before SB 569.

TEA says the expected benefit is better access to state tests for virtual students, especially rural, medically fragile and economically disadvantaged students, and a closer fit with how virtual students already learn ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

### 3. No new paperwork for teachers

TEA states that the proposal "would not require a written report or other paperwork to be completed by a principal or classroom teacher" ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). TEA also estimates remote testing will cost the agency $105,200 per year in fiscal years 2027-2031, absorbed by the current state assessment budget, with no fiscal impact on local government ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

## What teachers can do now

1. **English II teachers: keep this year's plan.** Under the proposal, the English II EOC stays in place through 2026-27 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). Your students still need constructed-response practice this spring. Our [STAAR ECR rubric posters and recording sheets (RLA 3-EOC)](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/STAAR-ECR-Rubric-Posters-Recording-Sheets-RLA-3-EOC-17493531) and [SCR checklists (3-EOC)](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/STAAR-SCR-Reading-2-pt-Writing-1-pt-Checklists-3-EOC-17493627) were made for exactly that.
2. **English I teachers: no change in this proposal.** English I stays on the rule's EOC list ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). Want a review-day activity? Try [Archive Vault English I STAAR EOC](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/Archive-Vault-English-I-STAAR-EOC-TEKS-E19C-E19D-E18A-Google-Slides-17468013).
3. **Virtual campus and program staff: watch the test administration materials.** Remote testing would follow procedures in TEA's test administration materials ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).
4. **Watch for adoption.** Adoption could come as early as November 15, 2026 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). TEA keeps each proposal on its proposed-rules page until it is adopted and filed ([TEA](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/proposed-commissioner-education-rules)).

### How to submit a comment

TEA is taking public comments from October 16 through November 16, 2026 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). During the comment period you can submit a comment electronically with the public comment form on [TEA's proposed commissioner rules page](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/proposed-commissioner-education-rules), where the §101.3011 proposal is listed. For questions about the process, TEA lists its Rulemaking Unit at (512) 475-1497 or rules@tea.texas.gov ([TEA](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/proposed-commissioner-education-rules)). TEA specifically asks for information on the rule's cost, benefit or effect, including any data or analysis ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). A request for a public hearing under the Administrative Procedure Act must reach the commissioner within 14 calendar days after the October 16 publication ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the English II EOC already gone?

No. This is a proposed rule. Under it, the English II EOC remains in effect through 2026-27 and is discontinued starting in 2027-28 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

### Which schools could test remotely?

Full-time virtual campuses and full-time virtual programs as defined in TEC §30B.001, plus full-time virtual campuses operating in 2026-27 under former TEC Chapter 30A ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

### Does this add paperwork for classroom teachers?

TEA says the proposal would not require a written report or other paperwork from principals or classroom teachers ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

### When is the comment deadline?

The comment period ends November 16, 2026 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October162026/Proposed%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)). Comments go through the public comment form on [TEA's proposed commissioner rules page](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/commissioner-rules-tac/proposed-commissioner-education-rules).
