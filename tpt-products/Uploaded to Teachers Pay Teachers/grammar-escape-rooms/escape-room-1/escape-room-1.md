# 🚀 Escape Room 1: The Grammar Galaxy
## 3rd Grade | Parts of Speech & Complete Sentences
### TEKS: 3.11D.vii, 3.11D.i

---

## Design Overview
- **Color Palette:** Deep purples (#2D1B69), electric blues (#00D4FF), cosmic black (#0A0A2E), star white, neon pink accents (#FF6EC7)
- **Font:** Orbitron (headers), Nunito (body text)
- **Characters:** Captain Comma (robot guide), Grammar Aliens (friendly creatures on each planet)
- **Interactive Mechanic:** Click correct answer → warp to next planet. Wrong answer → alien gives a grammar hint.

## 🟣 ROOM 5: Planet Sentences (The Purple Nebula)

### 📌 SLIDE 16 — Planet Sentences Story
**Visual:** A swirling purple nebula with floating sentence fragments drifting like asteroids. A wise owl-like alien with glasses. Deep purple/magenta palette.

**Text:**
> ## 🟣 Planet Sentences
> 
> You've reached the final planet — the Purple Nebula! The Sentence Alien adjusts its glasses:
> 
> "In this nebula, sentences are broken apart! Some are **complete sentences** and some are just **fragments** — pieces that don't make sense alone. Sort them out and the last digit is yours!"
> 
> 🧠 **Remember:** A **complete sentence** has a **subject** (who/what) and a **predicate** (what they do), and expresses a **complete thought**. A **fragment** is missing one of these.
> 
> ➡️ *Click to accept the challenge!*

### 📌 SLIDE 17 — Planet Sentences Challenge
**Visual:** Sentence strips floating in space. Students click "Complete Sentence" or "Fragment" for each. Purple/magenta buttons.

**Text:**
> ## 🟣 Planet Sentences Challenge
> 
> **Is it a COMPLETE SENTENCE or a FRAGMENT?**
> 
> **1.** "The cat sat on the mat."
> - A) Complete Sentence ✅
> - B) Fragment
> 
> **2.** "Running through the field."
> - A) Complete Sentence
> - B) Fragment ✅
> 
> **3.** "Because it was raining outside."
> - A) Complete Sentence
> - B) Fragment ✅
> 
> **4.** "My teacher reads to us every day."
> - A) Complete Sentence ✅
> - B) Fragment
> 
> **5.** "The big, scary, green."
> - A) Complete Sentence
> - B) Fragment ✅

**Correct Answers:** 1-A, 2-B, 3-B, 4-A, 5-B

### 📌 SLIDE 17B — Planet Sentences Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🤔 Think Again!
> 
> **Hint from the Sentence Alien:**
> "Every complete sentence needs THREE things:
> 1. A **subject** — WHO or WHAT the sentence is about
> 2. A **predicate (verb)** — what the subject DOES
> 3. A **complete thought** — it makes sense on its own!
> 
> If ANY of these are missing, it's a fragment. Read it aloud — does it feel finished?"
> 
> ⬅️ *Click to try again!*
