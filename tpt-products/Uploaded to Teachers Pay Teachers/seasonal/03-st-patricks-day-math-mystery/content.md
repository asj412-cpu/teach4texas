# St. Patrick's Day Math Mystery: The Case of the Missing Gold — Full Content

## Visual Design Notes
- **Color Palette:** Emerald green, gold/yellow, white, black accents
- **Style:** Detective mystery meets Irish whimsy — magnifying glasses, gold coins, shamrocks, rainbow trails
- **Layout:** Each station card is themed as a "locked door" with a keyhole graphic
- **Fonts:** "Mystery" serif font for titles, clean sans-serif for math problems
- **Backgrounds:** Green textured with subtle shamrock watermarks

## Storyline

### The Setup (Read aloud or display on Slide 1)

🍀 **BREAKING NEWS FROM SHAMROCK CITY!** 🍀

Last night, the famous Shamrock City Gold Vault was robbed! The leprechaun's legendary pot of gold — worth exactly **1,847 gold coins** — has vanished!

Detective Clover has identified **5 suspects**, but she needs YOUR help to crack the case. Each suspect left behind a trail of clues hidden behind **6 locked doors**. To open each lock, you must solve the math challenges inside.

Solve all 6 locks → decode the final message → catch the thief!

**The Suspects:**
1. 🎩 **Finnegan O'Fiddle** — A traveling musician who was seen near the vault at midnight
2. 🌈 **Rainbow Rita** — A rainbow painter who always needs more gold paint
3. 🍄 **Mushroom Murphy** — A chef who claims he was home cooking stew
4. 📚 **Emerald Emma** — A librarian who says she was reading all night
5. 🐑 **Sheepish Shane** — A shepherd whose sheep were found near the vault

## Station 6: THE DATA LOCK 🔐

### Grade 3 Version
Use the bar graph: "Gold Coins Found by Each Suspect"
- Finnegan: 15, Rita: 30, Murphy: 10, Emma: 5, Shane: 20

1. Who found the most coins? → If Rita, letter = R
2. How many more did Rita find than Murphy? → If 20, letter = I
3. How many coins total? → If 80, letter = T
4. What is the difference between the most and least? → If 25, letter = A
5. If Shane found 5 more, how many would he have? → If 25, letter = !

**Lock Code: R-I-T-A-!**

### Grade 4 Version
Use the table: "Suspect Activity Log (Hours)"

| Suspect | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday |
|---------|--------|---------|-----------|
| Finnegan | 3 | 5 | 4 |
| Rita | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Murphy | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Emma | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Shane | 5 | 3 | 1 |

1. Who spent the most TOTAL hours on activities? → If Rita (21), letter = R
2. What is the mean (average) of Murphy's hours? → If 2.33... round to ~2, letter = I (use: What is the range of Shane's hours? If 4, letter = I)
3. How many total hours for ALL suspects on Monday? → If 20, letter = T
4. Who had the same hours every day? → If Emma, letter = A
5. How many more total hours did Rita have than Shane? → If 21-9=12, letter = !

**Lock Code: R-I-T-A-!**

## THE DECODER

Students combine all 6 lock codes:
- Station 1: **RAINB**
- Station 2: **OWRIT**
- Station 3: **ANOTE**
- Station 4: **FROMR**
- Station 5: **ITAHE**  (unscramble within station)
- Station 6: **RITA!**

**Combined message: "RAINBOW RITA NOTE FROM RITA HE— RITA!"**

**Final reveal:** Rearranging the clues: **"RAINBOW RITA" wrote a note that says "THE GOLD IS MINE!" → Rainbow Rita is the thief!** 🌈

Students fill in their "Case Solved" certificate: "I solved The Case of the Missing Gold! The thief was **Rainbow Rita**!"

## Teacher Setup Guide

### Preparation (15 min)
1. Print station cards (color recommended) and cut
2. Optional: laminate for reuse
3. Set up 6 stations around the room OR distribute as packet
4. Print student answer sheets (1 per student or pair)
5. Choose difficulty level per student/group

### Classroom Options
- **Centers rotation:** 6 stations, 8-10 min each
- **Whole class:** Project one station at a time, all solve together
- **Partner work:** Print packets, pairs work through all 6
- **Digital:** Assign Google Slides version

### Time: 45-60 minutes total
