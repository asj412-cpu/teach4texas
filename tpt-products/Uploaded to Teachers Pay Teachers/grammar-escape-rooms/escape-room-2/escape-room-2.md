## 🔴 SCENE 1: The Library (Subject-Verb Agreement)

### 📌 SLIDE 4 — Scene 1 Story
**Visual:** A dark library with overturned books. Crime scene tape across a shelf. Dust particles in light beams.

**Text:**
> ## 🔴 Crime Scene 1: The Library
> 
> The thief was spotted near the library! Books are scattered everywhere, and grammar errors were left behind.
> 
> Detective Rex examines the scene: "These sentences have **subject-verb agreement** errors. The subject and verb must MATCH — singular with singular, plural with plural. Fix them to find your first clue!"
> 
> 🧠 **Remember:** 
> - Singular subject → singular verb: "The dog **runs**."
> - Plural subject → plural verb: "The dogs **run**."

### 📌 SLIDE 5 — Scene 1 Challenge
**Visual:** Evidence notes (yellow sticky notes) with sentences. Gold/black answer buttons.

**Text:**
> ## 🔴 Scene 1 Challenge: Subject-Verb Agreement
> 
> **Choose the correct verb to complete each sentence.**
> 
> **1.** The group of students ___ ready for the test.
> - A) are
> - B) is ✅
> - C) were
> 
> **2.** My brothers ___ soccer every Saturday.
> - A) plays
> - B) play ✅
> - C) playing
> 
> **3.** The cat and the dog ___ in the yard.
> - A) runs
> - B) run ✅
> - C) is running
> 
> **4.** Everyone in the class ___ a pencil.
> - A) need
> - B) needs ✅
> - C) needing

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-B, 3-B, 4-B

### 📌 SLIDE 5B — Scene 1 Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🔍 Detective Rex's Clue:
> "Look at the **subject** — is it singular or plural? Watch out for tricky subjects! 'Group' and 'everyone' are SINGULAR even though they refer to many people. 'Cat AND dog' = TWO things = PLURAL."
> 
> ⬅️ *Try again, Detective!*

## 🟢 SCENE 3: The Museum (Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement)

### 📌 SLIDE 10 — Scene 3 Story
**Visual:** A grand museum with a smashed display case. Red velvet ropes. Paintings on walls askew.

**Text:**
> ## 🟢 Crime Scene 3: The Museum
> 
> The thief broke into the Grammar Museum! The display of pronouns was smashed. 
> 
> "Pronouns must AGREE with the noun they replace — the **antecedent**. If the noun is singular, the pronoun must be singular. If plural, plural! Match them up!"
> 
> 🧠 **Remember:** An **antecedent** is the noun that a pronoun refers to.
> - "**Maria** forgot **her** lunch." (Maria = antecedent, her = pronoun)

### 📌 SLIDE 11 — Scene 3 Challenge
**Text:**
> ## 🟢 Scene 3 Challenge: Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement
> 
> **Choose the correct pronoun.**
> 
> **1.** Each student should bring ___ own supplies.
> - A) their
> - B) his or her ✅
> - C) our
> 
> **2.** The players celebrated ___ victory.
> - A) his
> - B) its
> - C) their ✅
> 
> **3.** Maria and José finished ___ project early.
> - A) her
> - B) his
> - C) their ✅
> 
> **4.** The dog wagged ___ tail happily.
> - A) their
> - B) its ✅
> - C) his
> 
> **5.** Somebody left ___ backpack in the hallway.
> - A) their
> - B) his or her ✅
> - C) our

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-C, 4-B, 5-B

### 📌 SLIDE 11B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🔍 Detective Rex's Clue:
> "Find the antecedent first! Is it singular or plural? Male, female, or neutral? 'Each' and 'somebody' are SINGULAR — use 'his or her.' 'Players' and 'Maria and José' are PLURAL — use 'their.' For animals/things, use 'its.'"

## 🔵 SCENE 4: The Restaurant (Comma Usage)

### 📌 SLIDE 13 — Scene 4 Story
**Visual:** A fancy restaurant with knocked-over wine glasses and scattered menus. Crime tape on the kitchen door.

**Text:**
> ## 🔵 Crime Scene 4: The Restaurant
> 
> Evidence was found at Café Grammar! The thief left notes with missing commas everywhere.
> 
> "Commas are tiny but POWERFUL! They separate items in a list, come after introductory words, and join sentences with conjunctions. Find where the commas belong!"
> 
> 🧠 **Comma Rules:**
> - **Lists:** I like dogs, cats, and fish.
> - **Introductory words:** First, we went to the store.
> - **Compound sentences:** I ran fast, but I still missed the bus.

### 📌 SLIDE 14 — Scene 4 Challenge
**Text:**
> ## 🔵 Scene 4 Challenge: Comma Usage
> 
> **Where does the comma belong? Choose the correctly written sentence.**
> 
> **1.**
> - A) We need milk eggs and bread from the store.
> - B) We need milk, eggs, and bread from the store. ✅
> 
> **2.**
> - A) After the storm, the rainbow appeared. ✅
> - B) After the storm the rainbow appeared.
> 
> **3.**
> - A) I wanted to play outside but it was raining. 
> - B) I wanted to play outside, but it was raining. ✅
> 
> **4.**
> - A) Yes I would like some more please.
> - B) Yes, I would like some more, please. ✅
> 
> **5.**
> - A) My favorite colors are blue, green, and purple. ✅
> - B) My favorite colors are blue green and purple.

**Correct Answers:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-B, 4-B, 5-A

### 📌 SLIDE 14B — Hint
**Text:**
> ## 🔍 Detective Rex's Clue:
> "Three big comma rules: 1) **Lists** of 3+ items need commas between them. 2) **Introductory words/phrases** at the start of a sentence get a comma after them. 3) When combining two complete sentences with AND, BUT, OR, SO — put a comma BEFORE the conjunction!"
