Read the passage. The old lighthouse had stood on the cliff for over a hundred years. Its paint was peeling, and the light no longer worked. Still, every evening, Mr. Hendricks climbed the stairs to the top and looked out at the sea. What can the reader infer about Mr. Hendricks?
A) He is afraid of the dark.
B) He has a deep connection to the lighthouse.
C) He is trying to fix the light.
D) He does not like living near the ocean.

C) Sea turtles face many threats to their survival.
