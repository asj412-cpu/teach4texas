# Teacher Niche Master Plan
*Created: February 18, 2026*

## The Angle
**STAAR scores → student growth → TIA designation ($3K-$32K/year)**
Nobody is connecting these dots for teachers. That's our moat.

### How TIA Actually Works
1. **District must opt in** — not all 1,200+ TX districts participate (665 currently do)
2. **Teachers qualify through:** student performance/growth + admin evaluations (observations)
3. **Content funnel:** Help teachers discover TIA → push district to opt in → qualify once they're in

## Review/Testimonial Pipeline
1. Track repeat buyers (TPT analytics + email engagement)
2. After 2-3 purchases → personalized outreach
3. Ask: "How have these resources helped your students/TIA goals?"
4. Incentive: free template pack or early access
5. Place testimonials on: TPT listings, content site, SaaS landing page
6. Dream testimonial: "Used these STAAR resources → scores went up → qualified for TIA"
