# 📊 Student Growth Tracking Template — Google Sheets

### Finally, a data tracker that does the math FOR you! 🎉

Are you drowning in assessment data? Spending hours creating spreadsheets before every PLC meeting? **This done-for-you Google Sheets template tracks student growth, flags intervention needs, and generates a beautiful dashboard — automatically.**

---

## ✅ What's Included:

📋 **Tab 1: Class Roster**
- Space for up to 35 students
- Subgroup tagging (ELL, SpEd, GT, 504, Dyslexia, At-Risk, Bilingual)
- All other tabs auto-populate from your roster!

📝 **Tab 2: Assessment Scores**
- Pre-Test, Mid-Year, Post-Test columns
- 8 Unit Assessment columns
- 3 STAAR Mock Score columns
- Auto-calculated running averages and trends

📈 **Tab 3: Growth Analysis (AUTO-CALCULATED!)**
- Point growth and percentage growth — calculated for you
- 🔴🟡🟢 Color-coded growth ratings at a glance
- Pre→Mid and Mid→Post growth breakdowns
- ⚠️ Automatic intervention flags for struggling students

🎯 **Tab 4: STAAR Readiness Tracker**
- Track D/A/M/MA levels across 4 assessment windows
- See who's moving up ⬆️ and who's moving down ⬇️
- Action recommendations based on current level

📊 **Tab 5: Dashboard (AUTO-GENERATED!)**
- Class summary statistics
- STAAR readiness pie chart
- Growth trends line chart
- Subgroup comparison bar chart
- Students needing intervention — flagged automatically
