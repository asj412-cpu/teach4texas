# ⭐ STAAR Wars — Quick-Start Overview

## Welcome, Commander!

You've just downloaded the **STAAR Wars Boot Camp** — a complete, space-themed STAAR prep week that transforms your classroom into STAAR Command and your hallway into a space station. Your students are cadets on a 5-day mission to conquer the STAAR.

- **Setting:** Outer space / STAAR Command space station

- **Completing work earns:** Mission Badges

- **Hallway becomes:** A space station corridor

## 📅 Week Overview

| Day | Mission Name | Focus |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| Monday | **Mission 1: Launch Prep** | Reading/ELA foundations — main idea, inference, vocabulary |
| Tuesday | **Mission 2: Orbit Check** | Math fundamentals — computation, number sense, equations |
| Wednesday | **Mission 3: Deep Space Exploration** | Science & Social Studies review (or continued ELA/Math) |
| Thursday | **Mission 4: Re-Entry Simulation** | Mixed practice — full STAAR-format review across subjects |
| Friday | **Mission 5: Countdown to Launch** | Test-taking strategies, confidence building, final review |

## ⏰ How to Run This Week

### 2 Weeks Before
- [ ] Read through LESSON-PLANS.md completely
- [ ] Order/gather decoration supplies (see DECORATIONS.md)
- [ ] Print student materials (see STUDENT-MATERIALS.md)
- [ ] Send parent letter home (see PARENT-LETTER.md)

### Weekend Before
- [ ] Set up hallway decorations (enlist student helpers or team members!)
- [ ] Set up classroom decorations
- [ ] Organize student materials into daily packets
- [ ] Prep daily playlists (optional but fun)

### Each Morning (Boot Camp Week)
1. Play space-themed music as students enter
2. Deliver the **Mission Briefing** (see teacher scripts in LESSON-PLANS.md)
3. Hand out daily mission packet
4. Run activities from ACTIVITIES.md as needed
5. Award **Mission Badges** at end of day
6. Update the **Launch Sequence Countdown** poster

### Friday Afternoon
- Award **Mission Complete** certificates
- Take a group photo in front of your decorations
- Remind students: "You've trained for this. You're STAAR-ready!"

## 🎯 Customization Tips

- **Pick your grade level & subject** — lesson plans are designed as frameworks you fill with your specific TEKS
- **Scale up or down** — use all 5 days or pick your favorite 3
- **Team up** — each teacher on the grade level can be a different "space station" with different subject missions
- **Mix with your own materials** — drop your existing review worksheets into the themed framework

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Commit to the bit.** Wear a NASA shirt. Call yourself Commander. The more you lean in, the more students buy in.
2. **Start decorations early.** Friday before boot camp week is ideal. Recruit parent volunteers or student helpers.
3. **The badges matter.** Students will work harder for a sticker on a badge card than for a grade. Trust the system.
4. **Use the playlist.** Music transforms the energy in the room.
5. **Take photos!** Parents love seeing the themed classroom. Post on your class page/Remind app.
