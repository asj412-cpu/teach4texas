## Testing Tips Cards (10 strategies)

### Card 1: 📖 READ THE WHOLE QUESTION
Read every word in the question — including ALL answer choices — before you pick one.

### Card 2: ✋ COVER AND THINK
Cover the answer choices. Think about what YOU think the answer is first. Then look at the choices.

### Card 3: ❌ ELIMINATE WRONG ANSWERS
Cross out answers you KNOW are wrong. Now you have fewer to choose from!

### Card 4: 📝 SHOW YOUR WORK
Write in the margins, underline key words, draw pictures. Your work helps you think!

### Card 5: ⏭️ SKIP AND COME BACK
Stuck? Skip it and come back later. Your brain keeps working on it in the background!

### Card 6: 🔍 GO BACK TO THE TEXT
The answer is IN the passage. Go back and find the evidence!

### Card 7: ⏰ PACE YOURSELF
Don't rush, but don't spend too long on one question. Keep moving!

### Card 8: 🧠 USE WHAT YOU KNOW
Connect the question to things you've learned in class. Your teacher prepared you!

### Card 9: ✅ CHECK YOUR WORK
If you finish early, go back and check. Did you answer every question? Did you bubble correctly?

### Card 10: 💪 BELIEVE IN YOURSELF
You've worked hard all year. You are smart. You are ready. You can DO this!
