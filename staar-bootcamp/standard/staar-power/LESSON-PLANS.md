# 💥 STAAR Power — 5-Day Lesson Plans

> **Hero Academy Director's Guide**

## Daily Schedule Template

|-----------|----------|----------|

## 💥 MONDAY — Power of Reading

**Theme:** "Every hero needs the power to understand messages, decode clues, and comprehend information. Today you unlock the Power of Reading."

### Hero Assembly (10 min)
> "Heroes! Welcome to Hero Academy. I'm Director [Name], and this week you'll train to unlock 5 incredible powers. These powers will help you defeat the ultimate villain: the STAAR test. But here's the secret — you already HAVE these powers inside you. This week, we activate them. Today's power: READING. The ability to understand ANY text thrown at you. Let's train!"

- Hand out **Hero ID Cards**
- Explain the **Power-Up Ticket** system (earn tickets for effort, use for small rewards)
- Show the "Villain Board" — a poster listing the "villains" (skill areas) students will battle this week

### Training Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Power Scan"** — 5 reading questions on whiteboards. Quick warm-up.
2. **(20 min) "Hero Training: X-Ray Vision"** — Teacher models reading strategies. "X-Ray Vision lets you see THROUGH the text to find hidden meaning. Watch me..." Demonstrate close reading, inference, finding evidence.
3. **(15 min) "Sidekick Practice"** — Partners work through a passage. One reads, one finds evidence. "Every hero needs a sidekick!"

### Power Recharge (5 min)
- "Hero Pose" — students strike their best superhero pose for 30 seconds. Research shows power poses boost confidence! Play epic music.

### Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)
**"Villain: The Confusing Passage" — Reading Packet**
- STAAR-format reading questions
- Frame: "This villain tries to confuse you with tricky texts. Use your Power of Reading to defeat it!"
- Early finishers: "Bonus Villain" challenge

**Differentiation:**
- **Struggling:** "Training Wheels" version — shorter text, guided questions
- **Advanced:** "Elite Hero" — complex passages, analytical response
- **ELL:** Visual vocabulary, bilingual glossary

### Hero Debrief (10 min)
- Review tricky questions: "The villain almost got us there, but here's how to beat it..."
- Award **Power-Up Tickets** (1-2 per student who completed the battle)
- **Hero Tip #1:** Post on wall — "Always go back to the text for evidence!"

## 💥 TUESDAY — Power of Numbers

**Theme:** "Numbers are the language of the universe. Today you unlock the Power of Numbers — the ability to solve any mathematical challenge."

### Hero Assembly (10 min)
> "Heroes, yesterday you unlocked the Power of Reading. Today: the Power of NUMBERS. Think about it — every superhero uses math. Calculating distances, timing their moves, measuring strength. Without numbers, heroes would crash into buildings. Today's training: mathematical precision."

### Training Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Speed Force"** — Timed computation. "How fast can you calculate?"
2. **(20 min) "Hero Training: Problem-Solving Vision"** — Teacher models word problems step-by-step. "Every word problem is a villain in disguise. First, figure out what it's REALLY asking..."
3. **(15 min) "Dynamic Duo"** — Partner math: one solves, one checks. Switch.

### Power Recharge (5 min)
- "Super Speed" — 30 seconds of running in place, 30 seconds of silence. Reset.

### Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)
**"Villain: The Trick Question" — Math Packet**
- STAAR-format math problems
- "This villain hides extra information in word problems to confuse you!"

### Hero Debrief (10 min)
- **Hero Tip #2:** "Always show your work — heroes don't skip steps!"
- Power-Up Tickets awarded

## 💥 WEDNESDAY — Power of Knowledge

**Theme:** "The smartest heroes know science and history. Knowledge is the most versatile superpower."

### Hero Assembly (10 min)
> "What makes a hero truly powerful isn't just strength — it's KNOWLEDGE. Knowing how the world works. Knowing history so you don't repeat mistakes. Today's power: KNOWLEDGE. Science and social studies are your arsenal."

### Training Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(10 min) "Knowledge Scan"** — Rapid-fire science/SS questions
2. **(20 min) "Hero Stations"** — 4 stations around the room, each a different "power source" (science topic or SS era). Groups rotate, 5 min each.
3. **(15 min) "Villain Lineup"** — Match vocabulary terms to definitions in a timed relay. "Identify the villain before it attacks!"

### Power Recharge (5 min)
- "Invisibility" — Everyone closes their eyes for 60 seconds of complete silence. "Heroes need to be able to focus in any situation."

### Training Session 2: Villain Battle (40 min)
**"Villain: The Unknown" — Science/SS Packet**
- Mixed science and social studies questions
- Midweek energy: keep it shorter (10-12 questions), leave time for a review game

**(15 min) BONUS: "Villain Battle Royale" Review Game**
- Quick whole-class game (see ACTIVITIES.md — "Hero vs. Villain Showdown")

### Hero Debrief (10 min)
- **Hero Tip #3:** "When you don't know an answer, eliminate what you DO know is wrong!"
- Power-Up Tickets + midweek treat

## 💥 THURSDAY — Power of Focus

**Theme:** "The greatest power a hero has is FOCUS. The ability to concentrate under pressure."

### Hero Assembly (10 min)
> "Today is the most important training day. Today you train your FOCUS. Any hero can be strong. Any hero can be fast. But the hero who can stay FOCUSED when things get tough? That's the one who wins. Today we simulate the real battle. Test conditions. Show me your Power of Focus."

### Training Session 1: Simulation (45 min)
**"Ultimate Villain: The STAAR Test" — Mini Practice Test**
- 20-30 mixed questions, test-like conditions
- Silent, independent, timed
- "This is the ultimate villain. You've trained for this."

### Power Recharge (5 min)
- Quiet stretch.

### Training Session 2: Analysis (40 min)
1. **(15 min)** Review answers
2. **(10 min)** Self-assessment — students identify their strongest and weakest "powers"
3. **(15 min)** Small group "power boosts" — targeted review by need

### Hero Debrief (10 min)
- **Hero Tip #4:** "Skip the hard questions and come back. A smart hero picks their battles."
- Power-Up Tickets

---

## 💥 FRIDAY — Power of Confidence

**Theme:** "Your final power — and the most important one. CONFIDENCE. Believing in yourself."

### Hero Assembly (10 min)
> "Heroes. You've unlocked Reading, Numbers, Knowledge, and Focus. Today's power is the one that ties them ALL together: CONFIDENCE. Confidence isn't about knowing everything — it's about trusting your training. You've TRAINED. You're READY. Today we celebrate."

### Training Session 1 (45 min)
1. **(15 min)** Review top strategies (Hero's Handbook)
2. **(15 min)** "Villain Trap Spotter" — identify common trick questions
3. **(15 min)** Fun review game (see ACTIVITIES.md)

### Power Recharge (5 min)
- Hero pose + deep breathing. "Channel your power."

### Training Session 2: Celebration (40 min)
1. **(10 min)** "Letter to My Hero Self" — students write a letter to themselves
2. **(10 min)** **Hero Graduation Ceremony** — certificates awarded
3. **(10 min)** Students design their own "Hero Emblem" (draw their personal superhero logo representing their best skill)
4. **(10 min)** Celebration — music, treats, hero poses for photos

### Final Debrief
- **Hero Tip #5:** "The power was inside you all along. You ARE a hero. Now go prove it on STAAR day."
- All 5 Power-Ups = "STAAR Superhero" status 💥
