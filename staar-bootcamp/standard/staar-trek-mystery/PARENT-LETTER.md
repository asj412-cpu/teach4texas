## English Version

**[School Letterhead]**

**[Date]**

Dear Families,

Next week, our classroom becomes a **Detective Agency!** 🔍 We're launching **"STAAR Trek: Mission to Mastery"** — a mystery-themed STAAR prep week where your child will work as a "detective" solving a week-long case while reviewing key skills for the STAAR test.

**When:** [Monday, Date] – [Friday, Date]

**What your child will experience:**
- 🔍 A week-long mystery with daily clues revealed through completing review work
- 🔍 Detective badges, case files, and a detective notebook
- 🔍 Practice with STAAR-format questions framed as "evidence gathering"
- 🔍 A Friday "Case Closed" celebration with certificates

**How to support your detective at home:**
1. **Good sleep** — Sharp detectives need rest! 8-10 hours nightly
2. **Ask about the case** — "Did you find any new clues today?" "What did you investigate?"
3. **Review together** — Your child may bring home "clue cards" (flashcards). Quick review helps!
4. **Stay positive** — "You're cracking the case. You've got this!"
5. **Healthy breakfast** — Brain fuel for the investigation

**STAAR test day ([date]):** Arrive rested and on time. Remind them: "You've investigated. You've gathered evidence. Now close the case!"

Thank you! 🔍

**Chief Detective [Teacher Name]**
