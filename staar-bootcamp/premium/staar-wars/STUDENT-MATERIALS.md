## 🎖️ Cadet ID Badge

**Description:** A credential card each student receives on Monday morning. Makes them feel "official."

**Design:**
- Size: 3" x 4" (print 4 per page on cardstock)
- Header: "STAAR COMMAND" with small star graphics
- Fields:
  - **CADET:** [Student Name]
  - **CREW:** [Table Group — Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta]
  - **RANK:** STAAR Trainee (can be "upgraded" to STAAR Ready on Friday)
  - **CLEARANCE:** Level 5
- Small box for student to draw their face or attach a photo
- Bottom: "This cadet is authorized for all STAAR missions"

**How to use:** Hand out Monday. Students keep on their desk or in a badge holder (lanyard optional). On Friday, stamp or write "STAAR READY" across it as a final upgrade.

## 📝 Daily Mission Packets

**Description:** Each day's review content, themed as a mission briefing.

**Format for each packet:**
- **Cover page:** "MISSION [#]: [Mission Name] — CLASSIFIED"
  - Date, Cadet name line, Crew assignment
  - "Objective:" one-sentence description of the day's focus
  - "Difficulty Level: ★★★☆☆" (adjust per day)
- **Content pages:** STAAR-format questions (teacher provides the actual questions — use released STAAR tests or your own)
  - Reading: passage + multiple choice + open-ended
  - Math: multiple choice + griddable + word problems
  - Science/SS: multiple choice + short answer
- **Back page:** "Mission Debrief" — 3 reflection questions:
  1. "What strategy helped me most today?"
  2. "What type of question was hardest?"
  3. "What will I focus on tomorrow?"

**Teacher note:** You provide the actual STAAR-format content. We provide the themed framework/cover pages. Use released STAAR tests from TEA (tea.texas.gov) or your own review materials.

## 📓 Mission Log (Daily Journal)

**Description:** A simple journal page for daily reflection. Can be printed as individual sheets or compiled into a booklet.

**Design (per page):**
- Header: "SPACE LOG — Day [#] — [Date]"
- Decorative border with small stars
- **Prompt:** (Changes daily — see LESSON-PLANS.md for prompts)
- 12-15 lined writing spaces
- Footer: "Cadet signature: ___________"

**Booklet option:** Print 5 pages (Mon–Fri), staple together with a cover page: "Mission Log — Cadet [Name] — STAAR Command, [Year]"

## 🏅 Mission Badge System

**The system:** Students earn one badge per day for completing their mission packet and participating. By Friday, a full badge card = 5 badges = "Mission Complete."

### Badge Card
- Size: 5" x 7" (print 2 per page)
- Title: "MISSION BADGE TRACKER"
- 5 circular spaces arranged like a constellation (connect with dotted lines)
- Each circle labeled: Mission 1, Mission 2, Mission 3, Mission 4, Mission 5
- Center text: "Complete all 5 missions to earn LAUNCH CLEARANCE"
- Space at top for student name

### How badges work:
- **Option A (cheapest):** Teacher stamps each circle with a star stamp or draws a star
- **Option B:** Teacher places a star sticker in each circle
- **Option C (fanciest):** Print small circular badge stickers that students peel and place themselves

### Badge Criteria:
- Completed the daily packet (doesn't need to be 100% correct — effort counts)
- Participated in the day's activities
- Showed good "cadet behavior" (respectful, on task)

**Tip:** Be generous. The badge system is about MOTIVATION, not gatekeeping. If a student tried, they earn their badge.

## 🚀 Launch Sequence Countdown Poster

**Description:** A large classroom poster that counts down from 5 to 1 (liftoff!). Updated daily.

**Design:**
- Title: "LAUNCH SEQUENCE"
- Large numbers: 5 — 4 — 3 — 2 — 1 — 🚀 LAUNCH!
- Each number has a checkbox or space for a large X/check
- Each number labeled with the day: "5 = Monday," "4 = Tuesday," etc.
- Rocket graphic at the bottom, "launching" upward toward the numbers
- At the top: "STAAR TEST = LIFTOFF"

**How to use:** Each afternoon during debrief, dramatically check off the day. Students count down together. By Friday: "3... 2... 1... LIFTOFF!"

## 🏆 Mission Complete Certificate

**Description:** End-of-week certificate awarded Friday afternoon. Make it a moment — call each student up individually.

**Design:**
- Size: 8.5" x 11" (landscape)
- Header: Large star graphic + "STAAR COMMAND"
- Title: "MISSION COMPLETE CERTIFICATE"
- Body: "This certifies that **Cadet [Name]** has successfully completed all five missions at STAAR Command and is cleared for STAAR launch. This cadet has demonstrated courage, skill, and determination. The galaxy is proud."
- Signature lines: "Mission Commander: ___________" and "Date: ___________"
- Footer: rocket ship graphic, stars, official-looking border
- Optional: gold/silver foil sticker seal

## ⭐ Bonus Transmission (Early Finisher Activity)

**Description:** For students who finish their daily packet early. A challenge extension.

**Design:**
- Header: "INCOMING TRANSMISSION — CLASSIFIED — FOR ADVANCED CADETS ONLY"
- Contains 2-3 harder questions or a more complex passage/problem
- Or: a creative prompt ("Write a log entry from the perspective of a STAAR question that nobody ever gets right. What would it say?")
- Fun, optional, but gives early finishers something engaging

## 📖 Cadet Yearbook Page (Friday Activity)

**Description:** Fun end-of-week activity where students sign each other's pages. Memory keepsake.

**Design:**
- Title: "STAAR Command Yearbook — Class of [Year]"
- **"My Crew:"** lines for 4-5 names
- **"My Commander (Teacher):"** line
- **"My Favorite Mission Was:"** short answer line
- **"On STAAR Day, I Will:"** short answer line
- **"Autographs:"** Large open space for classmates to sign and write short messages
- Small space: "Draw yourself as an astronaut"

---

## 📋 Self-Assessment Sheet (Thursday)

**Description:** After the Re-Entry Simulation, students identify their strengths and areas to review.

**Design:**
- Title: "POST-SIMULATION ANALYSIS"
- Section 1: "Questions I got RIGHT — what strategy worked?"
  - Space for 3-4 entries
- Section 2: "Questions I got WRONG — what went wrong?"
  - Space for 3-4 entries with columns: "Question Topic | What confused me | What I'll do differently"
- Section 3: "My Top 3 Focus Areas for STAAR Day"
  - Numbered 1-3
- Section 4: "My confidence level: ☆☆☆☆☆" (circle 1-5 stars)

---

## 💌 Letter to Myself (Friday)

**Description:** Students write a brief letter to themselves, sealed in an envelope. Teacher hands them back on actual STAAR test day as a confidence boost.

**Provided template:**
- "Dear Future Me,"
- "This week I learned..."
- "The strategy I'll use most on STAAR day is..."
- "I'm proud of myself because..."
- "Remember:..."
- "You've got this! — [Name]"

Students seal it in an envelope. Teacher writes student name on outside, collects, and stores until test day.
