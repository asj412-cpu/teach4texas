## Best Practices

1. **Vertical images only** — 2:3 ratio (1000x1500px). Use Canva templates.
2. **Text overlay on every pin** — Teachers scroll fast. Your image must communicate the value in 2 seconds.
3. **Multiple pin designs per product** — Create 3-5 different pin images for each product. Test which performs best.
4. **Keyword-rich titles and descriptions** — Pinterest is a search engine. Think SEO, not social media.
5. **Link every pin** — To your TPT listing or blog post. Every pin should drive traffic.
6. **Consistent branding** — Use the same 2-3 fonts and color palette (pick teacher-friendly colors: teal, coral, navy).
7. **Repin your own content** — Pin the same product to multiple relevant boards (spread out over days).
8. **Join group boards** — Search for teacher Pinterest group boards and request to join. Multiplies your reach.
9. **Use Pinterest Trends** — Check trends.pinterest.com for seasonal teacher search terms.
10. **Fresh pins > repins** — Pinterest prioritizes new images. Create new pin designs monthly for existing products.
