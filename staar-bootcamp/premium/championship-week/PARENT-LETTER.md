Dear Families,

Next week is **Championship Week** in our classroom! 🏆 We're turning STAAR prep into an exciting, sports-themed training camp where your child is an athlete preparing for the "big game" — the STAAR test.

**What is Championship Week?**
It's a themed, focused review week designed to boost your child's confidence and skills before STAAR. Each day is a "round" in a championship tournament, building toward test day. Students earn MVP awards, work through playbook-style study guides, and participate in team challenges.

**When:** [Monday, Date] – [Friday, Date]

**What your child will experience:**
- 🏈 Daily "practice drills" reviewing key [Reading/Math/Science/Social Studies] skills
- 🏈 A classroom transformed into a team locker room with motivational sports decor
- 🏈 Team-based and individual review activities
- 🏈 A championship scrimmage (practice test) on Thursday
- 🏈 A Friday pep rally and certificate ceremony

**How you can support your athlete at home:**
1. **Good sleep** — Athletes need rest to perform! 8-10 hours each night
2. **Ask about their "game"** — "How was practice today?" "Did you earn MVP?"
3. **Review together** — Your child may bring home flashcards or a "playbook." Even 10 minutes helps
4. **Stay positive** — "You've been training all year. You've got this!"
5. **Healthy fuel** — Good breakfast and snacks keep the brain sharp

**STAAR test day ([date]):**
- Arrive on time and well-rested
- Eat a good breakfast
- Remind them: "Champions prepare, and you've prepared. Go win!"

Thank you for supporting your child's success. If you have questions, please reach out anytime.

Go team! 🏆

**Coach [Teacher Name]**
