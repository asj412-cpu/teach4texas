# 💥 STAAR Power — Decoration Guide

> Standard theme = focused on classroom. Simple, impactful, budget-friendly.

---

## 🏠 CLASSROOM: Hero HQ

### Door Sign
- **"HERO HQ — Authorized Heroes Only"**
- Bold colors (red, blue, yellow), comic-book style burst/explosion graphics
- Print on 11x17 or 2 pages taped together

### Hero Cape Door Hanger
- Cut a large cape shape from red bulletin board paper or fabric
- Tape above/around the door so it looks like the door is wearing a cape
- Simple, eye-catching, takes 10 minutes
- **Cost:** ~$0-2 (use existing paper or $1 fabric from Dollar Tree)

### Wall Decorations

#### Villain Board
- Poster titled "VILLAINS TO DEFEAT THIS WEEK"
- List 5-6 "villains" (skill areas): "The Confusing Passage," "The Trick Question," "The Time Thief," etc.
- Cross each one off (big red X) as the class "defeats" it during the week
- **Cost:** $1 poster board

#### Power-Up Progress Tracker
- Simple chart: student names on the left, 5 columns (Mon–Fri)
- Add stickers/stamps as students earn Power-Ups
- Title: "HERO POWER LEVEL"
- **Cost:** $1 poster board + stickers

#### Motivational Posters (Print 4-5)
- "With great power comes great STAAR scores."
- "Heroes don't give up. They power up."
- "You don't need a cape to be a hero. Just a pencil."
- "Be the hero of your own STAAR story."
- Comic-book burst style backgrounds (POW! ZAP! BOOM!)

### Desk Name Tags
- "Hero [Name] — Power: [Student's best subject]"
- Comic-book style border
- Print on colored cardstock, tent-fold

### Room Arrangement
- Desk clusters = "Hero Teams" (name each team)
- Teacher desk = "Director's Office"

---

## 🏫 HALLWAY: Simple Banner

Standard themes include a **single hallway banner** (no full transformation):

- **"STAAR POWER WEEK — HEROES IN TRAINING"**
- Use butcher paper or tape individual printed letters
- Add comic-book burst shapes around key words
- Hang above classroom door or across hallway section
- **Cost:** ~$0-2
