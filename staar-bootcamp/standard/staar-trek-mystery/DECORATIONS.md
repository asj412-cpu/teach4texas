# 🔍 STAAR Trek: Mission to Mastery — Decoration Guide

> Moody, mysterious, and surprisingly easy to set up.

---

## 🏠 CLASSROOM: Detective Agency HQ

### Door Sign
- **"DETECTIVE AGENCY HQ — Case #STAAR — INVESTIGATORS ONLY"**
- Magnifying glass graphic
- Black and white color scheme for "noir" feel
- Print on 11x17 or 2 pages taped together

### Evidence Board (CENTERPIECE)
- Bulletin board or large poster dedicated to the week's mystery
- Title: "EVIDENCE BOARD — Case #STAAR"
- Start Monday with a "?" in the center
- Add one clue card each day
- Connect clues with yarn or drawn lines
- By Friday: all 5 clues plus the solution revealed
- **This is the most engaging decoration.** Students check it daily.
- **Cost:** $0-2 (use existing bulletin board + yarn)

### Wall Decorations

#### Detective's Rules
- Post each day's rule as you reveal it (5 total by Friday)
- Write on paper styled as "case notes" (slightly crumpled for effect)

#### Most Wanted Wall
- Space for students' "Suspect Profiles" (Wednesday activity)
- Title: "MOST WANTED — Academic Suspects at Large"

#### Motivational Posters
- "The evidence is clear: you're STAAR ready."
- "Great detectives don't guess — they FIND EVIDENCE."
- "The case isn't closed until you CHECK YOUR WORK."
- "Elementary, my dear student — you've GOT this!"

### Desk Name Tags
- **"Detective [Name]"**
- Badge/shield graphic
- "Badge #: [student number]"
- "Specialty: [best subject]"

## 🏫 HALLWAY: Crime Scene

### Hallway Banner
- **"⚠️ STAAR TESTING ZONE — DO NOT CROSS ⚠️"**
- Styled like crime scene tape: yellow background, black text
- Use yellow butcher paper or yellow streamer strips with black marker
- Hang across hallway entrance or above classroom door
- **Cost:** $1-3

### Footprint Trail
- Cut footprint shapes from black construction paper
- Tape to hallway floor leading from entrance to classroom
- Optional: add magnifying glass cutouts scattered among the footprints
- **Cost:** $1-2 for construction paper

### Classroom Door "Crime Scene"
- Yellow streamer strips across the door (like caution tape)
- Text on strips: "STAAR TESTING ZONE" or "CASE IN PROGRESS"
- Magnifying glass cutout on the door
- **Cost:** $1 for yellow streamers
