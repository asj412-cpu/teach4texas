## 📦 5 Complete Lesson Plans:

### 🔍 Lesson 1: Story Detective (3rd Grade)
**TEKS 3.6F, 3.7C — Inference & Text Evidence**
- Students become "detectives" finding clues in mystery passages
- Build inference skills using the formula: Text Clue + Background Knowledge = Inference
- 3 original mystery passages with guided analysis
- STAAR constructed response practice with text evidence

### 📰 Lesson 2: News Reporters (4th Grade)
**TEKS 4.9D.i, 4.6G — Informational Text & Central Idea**
- Students analyze real-world news articles as "reporters"
- Identify central ideas and supporting details
- 3 original age-appropriate articles included (school gardens, audiobooks, student app developers)
- Summary writing using the Central Idea + Details formula

### 🎤 Lesson 3: Poetry Slam (5th Grade)
**TEKS 5.6G, 5.6H — Poetry Analysis & Figurative Language**
- Analyze poems by Langston Hughes, Robert Frost, and Gary Soto
- Identify and explain simile, metaphor, personification, hyperbole
- Students perform poetry and analyze how devices create meaning
- Theme identification with text evidence

### 🗣️ Lesson 4: Debate Club (4th-5th Grade)
**TEKS 4.11B, 5.11B — Argumentative Writing & Constructed Response**
- Students build arguments using Claim + Reasons + Evidence formula
- Live mini-debates with structured speaking time
- Write STAAR-format constructed responses defending a position
- Counterargument and rebuttal practice

### 📚 Lesson 5: Book Club Challenge (3rd-5th Grade)
**TEKS 3.6G, 4.6H, 5.6H — Paired Passages & Synthesis**
- 3 paired passage sets included (animal adaptations, kids making a difference, space exploration)
- Students compare, contrast, and SYNTHESIZE across two texts
- Mirrors the STAAR paired passage format exactly
- Multi-grade adaptable with adjustment guide
