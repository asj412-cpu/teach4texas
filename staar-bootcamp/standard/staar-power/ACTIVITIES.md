# 💥 STAAR Power — Themed Activities

> 10 superhero-themed activities with 3 review games.

---

## 🎮 REVIEW GAMES

### 1. Hero vs. Villain Showdown (Whole Class)
**Type:** Class game | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Any subject

**How to Play:**
- Project a question. Class is the "Hero." The question is the "Villain."
- Students write answers on whiteboards. If the majority gets it right, the Hero wins. If not, the Villain wins.
- Track on the board: Hero | Villain — tally marks for each
- Play 10-15 rounds. "Can the heroes defeat more villains than not?"
- Dramatic narration: "The villain attacks with... a MULTI-STEP WORD PROBLEM! Heroes, defend yourselves!"

**Materials:** Questions, whiteboards, markers, scoreboard

---

### 2. Power-Up Relay (Team Relay)
**Type:** Teams of 5-6 | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Quick computation, vocabulary

**How to Play:**
- Teams line up. First hero runs to the board, solves a question, runs back
- Correct answer = earns a "power-up" (point) for the team
- If wrong, next hero can attempt the same question
- First team to collect 6 power-ups wins
- "Speed AND accuracy — that's what separates heroes from sidekicks!"

**Materials:** Question cards at the front, answer key, scoreboard

---

### 3. Villain Battle Cards (Partner Game)
**Type:** Partners | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Vocabulary, facts, formulas

**How to Play:**
- Create a deck of cards: front = question, back = answer
- Also create "Villain Cards" (5-6 scattered in the deck) — these have trick questions or bonus challenges
- Partners take turns drawing cards and answering
- Correct = keep the card (1 point). Villain cards = must answer correctly for 3 points, wrong = lose 2 points
- Most points after the deck is done wins

**Materials:** Card deck (index cards), answer key on backs

## 👤 INDIVIDUAL ACTIVITIES

### 4. Hero ID Card Creation
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 15 min | **Best for:** Monday warm-up, community building

**Instructions:**
Students complete their Hero ID cards:
- Hero Name (they create a superhero alter-ego)
- Superpower (their strongest academic skill)
- Weakness (the skill they're working on)
- Catchphrase (a motivational phrase they'll use on test day)
- Self-portrait in hero costume

**Materials:** Hero ID template, colored pencils

---

### 5. Hero's Handbook (Personal Study Guide)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 20 min | **Best for:** Synthesis

**Instructions:**
Students create a small booklet (folded paper):
- Page 1: "My Superpowers" (skills I'm confident in + strategies)
- Page 2: "Villains I've Defeated" (skills I've mastered this week)
- Page 3: "Training Still Needed" (areas to review)
- Page 4: "My Game Plan for STAAR Day"

**Materials:** Paper, pencils

---

### 6. Villain Wanted Posters (Concept Review)
**Type:** Individual | **Time:** 15-20 min | **Best for:** Identifying common mistakes

**Instructions:**
Students create "WANTED" posters for their worst test-taking villains:
- Villain name: "The Rushing Robber" (not reading carefully)
- Illustration
- "Crimes:" what this villain makes you do wrong
- "How to Defeat:" specific strategy
- Post on a "Villain Wall" in the classroom

**Materials:** Paper, markers

---

## 👥 PARTNER ACTIVITIES

### 7. Sidekick Swap (Partner Quiz)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 20 min

**Instructions:**
Each student writes 5 questions. Swap with partner. Answer each other's questions. Grade together. Discuss any disagreements using evidence. "A good sidekick challenges you to be better!"

**Materials:** Paper, pencils

---

### 8. Hero Training Montage (Timed Partner Drills)
**Type:** Partner | **Time:** 15 min

**Instructions:**
Play epic music. Partners take turns doing rapid-fire practice:
- 2 min: Partner A quizzes Partner B (flashcards)
- 2 min: Switch
- 2 min: Both solve problems independently, compare
- Repeat for 3 rounds
- "Every hero training montage has a soundtrack!"

**Materials:** Flashcards or question strips, timer, epic music playlist

---

## 👥👥 GROUP ACTIVITIES

### 9. Hero HQ Defense (Station Rotation)
**Type:** Groups of 4-5 | **Time:** 30 min | **Best for:** Mixed review

**Stations:**
- **Shield Station** — Reading defense (comprehension questions)
- **Laser Station** — Math precision (computation)
- **Mind Station** — Science knowledge
- **Time Station** — Timed challenges (speed round)

Groups rotate every 7 minutes. Record answers on a "Mission Log" sheet.

**Materials:** Station question sets, mission log sheets, timer

---

### 10. Avengers Assemble (Group Problem Solving)
**Type:** Groups of 4 | **Time:** 20 min

**Instructions:**
Each group gets one complex, multi-part problem. Each member is assigned a "role":
- **The Brain** — Reads and interprets the problem
- **The Calculator** — Handles all computation
- **The Checker** — Verifies each step
- **The Reporter** — Presents the solution to the class

All four must contribute. "A hero team is only as strong as its weakest link!"

**Materials:** Complex problems (1 per group), chart paper, markers
