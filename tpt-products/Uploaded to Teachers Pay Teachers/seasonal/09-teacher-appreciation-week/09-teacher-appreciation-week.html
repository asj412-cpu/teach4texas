Teacher Appreciation Week Cards & Activities — Full Content

Visual Design Notes

  • Color Palette: Warm coral, soft gold, apple red, cream, sage green
  • Style: Warm, heartfelt — apple motifs, hearts, stars, pencil illustrations
  • Layout: Card templates fold to create 4-panel cards; writing pages have decorative borders
  • Fonts: Warm handwritten-style headers, clean lined writing areas

Card Template 1: The Classic Thank You

Front: [Large apple illustration] “You’re the BEST teacher!” Inside Left: “What I love about you:” [3 lines] Inside Right: “Thank you for teaching me _____________. You make _____________ grade awesome because _____________.” Back: “From your student, _____________” + [Small heart border]

Card Template 2: The Top 10

Front: “TOP 10 Reasons You’re an Amazing Teacher” Inside: Numbered list 1-10 with lines for students to fill in reasons Back: “Thank you, _____________! You’re #1 to us!”

Card Template 3: The Recipe Card

Front: “Recipe for the BEST Teacher” Inside: “Ingredients: - ___ cups of kindness - ___ tablespoons of patience - ___ teaspoon of humor - A pinch of _______________ - A whole lot of _______________

Directions: Mix together with love and serve to _____ graders daily! Makes: The best school year ever!”

Card Template 4: The Superhero Card

Front: “Not All Heroes Wear Capes… Some Carry Gradebooks!” [Superhero silhouette with pencil cape] Inside: “My teacher’s superpower is: _____________. My teacher saves the day when: _____________. If my teacher were a superhero, their name would be: _____________.” Back: “Thank you, Super _____________!”

Card Template 5: The ABCs of My Teacher

Front: “The ABCs of _____________ (teacher’s name)” Inside: Letters A through Z with lines: - A is for _____________ - B is for _____________ - … (students fill in words that describe their teacher) Students can pick 10-15 favorites

Card Template 6: The Interview Card

Front: “Teacher Spotlight: An Interview with _____________” Inside Left (student fills in): - “My teacher’s favorite thing to teach is: _____________” - “I think my teacher’s favorite food is: _____________” - “When my teacher isn’t teaching, I bet they: _____________” - “My teacher’s catchphrase is: _____________” Inside Right: “What I’d say to my teacher if I had one minute:” [lines]

Writing Prompts

Prompt 1: My Favorite Teacher Memory

Write about your favorite memory with your teacher this year. Describe what happened, how you felt, and why it’s your favorite. Use specific details!

Sentence starters: “One of my favorite memories with my teacher is when…” / “I’ll never forget the time…”

Prompt 2: Why Teachers Matter

Write an opinion piece about why teachers are important. Give at least 3 reasons with examples. Think about how your teacher has helped you grow.

Sentence starters: “I believe teachers are important because…” / “Without teachers, the world would…”

Prompt 3: A Day as My Teacher

Imagine you get to be the teacher for one day. Write a narrative about your day. What would you teach? How would you handle the class? What would you do differently? What would you keep the same?

Prompt 4: Thank You Letter (Formal)

Write a formal thank-you letter to your teacher following proper letter format: date, greeting, body (3 paragraphs), closing, signature.

Template:

[Date]

Dear _____________,

[Paragraph 1: Why are you writing? Express gratitude.]

[Paragraph 2: Specific examples of things you appreciate.]

[Paragraph 3: How has this teacher impacted you?]

Sincerely,
[Your name]

Prompt 5: If I Could Give My Teacher Any Gift

If you could give your teacher ANY gift in the world (money is no object!), what would you give them and why? It can be something you buy, something you make, or even something that doesn’t exist yet!

“All About My Teacher” Poster Template

A one-page poster students complete about their teacher:

[Center: Frame for drawing/photo of teacher]

Around the frame: - Name: _____________ - Teaches: _____ grade at _____ school - Known for: _____________ - Favorite saying: _____________ - Best at: _____________ - Makes us laugh when: _____________ - 3 words that describe them: _____________, _____________, _____________ - We love them because: _____________

Collaborative Class Book: “Why We Love Our Teacher”

Each student gets one page to contribute to a class book:

Page Template: - My name: _____________ - My teacher is special because: _____________ [3-4 lines] - My favorite thing my teacher does: _____________ [2 lines] - [Drawing box: “Draw a picture of you and your teacher”]

Teacher compiles all pages into a book (staple or bind) and keeps as a keepsake!

Teacher Awards (Student-Voted)

The class votes on fun awards for their teacher(s):

🏆 Best Read-Aloud Voice — “_____________” 🏆 Funniest Teacher Moment — “_____________” 🏆 Most Patient — “_____________” 🏆 Most Creative Lesson — “_____________” 🏆 Best Classroom Decorator — “_____________” 🏆 Snack MVP (best snack provider) — “_____________” 🏆 Most Likely to Make You Love [subject] — “_____________” 🏆 Best Encourager — “_____________”

Award certificates: Printable certificates for each award with teacher name, award title, and “Awarded by the students of _____ grade”

Acrostic Poem Template

Using the letters of your teacher’s name, write a word or phrase for each letter that describes them.

Example for “MRS. SMITH”: - M — Makes learning fun - R — Really cares about us - S — Smart and kind

[Template with large decorative letters and lines]
