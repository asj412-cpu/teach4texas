# Spring Writing Prompts & Journal — Full Content

## Visual Design Notes
- **Color Palette:** Pastel pink, sky blue, soft green, sunshine yellow, lavender
- **Style:** Watercolor garden aesthetic — flowers, butterflies, raindrops, sunshine
- **Layout:** Each prompt on a decorated page with themed illustrations around the border
- **Fonts:** Playful rounded font for prompts, clean lined paper for writing
- **Each prompt has a unique spring illustration** (tulips, birds, rain, garden, etc.)

## Creative/Narrative Writing Prompts

### Prompt 1: 🌧️ The Raindrop's Journey
**Imagine you are a single raindrop falling from a cloud. Tell the story of your journey from the sky to the ground. What do you see? Where do you land? What happens next?**

*Graphic Organizer: Story map — Beginning (in the cloud), Middle (falling), End (where you land)*

*Sentence starters for ELL: "First, I formed inside a..." / "As I fell, I could see..." / "Finally, I landed on..."*

---

### Prompt 2: 🦋 The Butterfly Who Couldn't Fly
**Write a story about a butterfly who is afraid to fly for the first time. How does the butterfly overcome its fear? Who helps? What does the world look like from above?**

*Graphic Organizer: Character web (butterfly's name, feelings, problem, who helps, solution)*

---

### Prompt 3: 🌱 My Magic Garden
**You discover that everything you plant in your backyard grows overnight into something magical. If you planted a pencil, a book, and a shoe, what would grow? Write a story about your magic garden and what happens when the neighbors find out.**

*Graphic Organizer: 3-column chart — What I planted / What grew / What happened*

---

### Prompt 4: 🐛 A Day in the Life of a Caterpillar
**Write a first-person story from the perspective of a caterpillar on the day it begins to build its chrysalis. What has your life been like? How do you feel about the big change coming?**

*Graphic Organizer: Timeline — Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Evening*

---

### Prompt 5: 🌪️ The Texas Spring Storm
**A big spring thunderstorm rolls into your town. Write a story about an adventure that happens during the storm. Maybe the power goes out, something unexpected blows into your yard, or you discover something amazing after the storm passes.**

*Graphic Organizer: Problem-Solution chart (Setting → Problem → Events → Solution)*

---

### Prompt 6: 🐣 The Unexpected Egg
**While playing outside during spring break, you find a large, colorful egg behind a bush. It's warm and starting to crack. Write a story about what hatches and what you do about it.**

*Graphic Organizer: Story mountain — Introduction, Rising Action, Climax, Falling Action, Resolution*

---

### Prompt 7: 🌻 The Flower That Talked
**One morning, you walk outside and a sunflower in your garden says "Good morning!" Write a story about your day with a talking flower. What does it say? What does it want? Do you tell anyone?**

*Graphic Organizer: Dialogue planning sheet — Character 1 says / Character 2 responds*

---

### Prompt 8: 🏕️ Spring Break Adventure
**Write a story about the BEST spring break you can imagine. It can be realistic or completely fantastical. Where do you go? Who is with you? What's the most exciting thing that happens?**

*Graphic Organizer: 5 senses web — What I see, hear, smell, taste, feel*

---

### Prompt 9: 🐸 The Neighborhood Nature Explorer
**You and your best friend start a "Nature Detective Agency" and spend a spring weekend investigating the plants and animals in your neighborhood. Write about a mystery you solve using nature clues.**

*Graphic Organizer: Detective notes — Clue 1, Clue 2, Clue 3, Conclusion*

---

### Prompt 10: 🎨 The Color That Disappeared
**One spring morning, you wake up and the color green has vanished from the world. Grass, trees, frogs — all gray! Write a story about what happens and how you bring the color back.**

*Graphic Organizer: Cause and Effect chain*

---

## Informational/Opinion Writing Prompts

### Prompt 11: 🌍 Why Spring is the Best Season (Opinion)
**Do you think spring is the best season? Write an opinion piece with at least THREE reasons to support your answer. Use specific examples and details.**

*Graphic Organizer: Opinion tree — Opinion at top, 3 branches for reasons, leaves for details/evidence*

---

### Prompt 12: 🐝 All About Bees (Informational)
**Write an informational paragraph about why bees are important to our world. Include at least 3 facts about what bees do and why we need them.**

*Graphic Organizer: Fact web — Main topic in center, 4 fact bubbles around it, detail lines from each*

*Vocabulary word bank: pollination, nectar, hive, colony, ecosystem, crops*

---

### Prompt 13: 🌮 The Best Spring Break Activity (Opinion)
**What is the best way to spend spring break? Write an opinion piece convincing your reader that YOUR choice is the best. Remember to address the other side!**

*Graphic Organizer: T-chart — My opinion + reasons vs. Other side + why mine is still better*

---

### Prompt 14: 🌸 How Flowers Grow (Informational)
**Write an informational piece explaining the life cycle of a flowering plant. Include the stages from seed to flower and explain what each stage needs.**

*Graphic Organizer: Cycle diagram — Seed → Sprout → Seedling → Adult Plant → Flower → Seed*

*Vocabulary: germination, roots, stem, photosynthesis, nutrients, pollination*

---

### Prompt 15: 🏫 Should Recess Be Longer? (Opinion)
**Write an opinion letter to your principal arguing that recess should be 15 minutes longer during spring. Give at least 3 strong reasons with evidence.**

*Graphic Organizer: Letter format outline — Greeting, Introduction with opinion, Reason 1+detail, Reason 2+detail, Reason 3+detail, Conclusion, Closing*

---

### Prompt 16: 🌊 Texas Weather in Spring (Informational)
**Write an informational piece about the different types of weather Texas experiences in spring. Include information about thunderstorms, tornadoes, and what to do to stay safe.**

*Graphic Organizer: 3-column chart — Weather type / What it's like / Safety tips*

---

### Prompt 17: 🐦 Should We Have Class Pets? (Opinion)
**Your class is voting on whether to get a class pet for the spring semester. Write an opinion piece for OR against having a class pet. Give at least 3 reasons.**

*Graphic Organizer: Argument ladder — Opinion → Reason 1 → Evidence → Reason 2 → Evidence → Reason 3 → Evidence → Conclusion*

---

### Prompt 18: 🦎 Amazing Texas Animals (Informational)
**Choose one Texas animal (armadillo, horned lizard, roadrunner, or bluebonnet butterfly) and write an informational piece about it. Include its habitat, diet, interesting facts, and why it matters to Texas.**

*Graphic Organizer: Animal fact file — Name, Habitat, Diet, 3 interesting facts, Why important*

---

### Prompt 19: 🧃 Should Students Plan the School Lunch Menu? (Opinion)
**Write an opinion piece about whether students should get to vote on the school lunch menu. Consider nutrition, preferences, and fairness.**

*Graphic Organizer: Pros and Cons + My Opinion conclusion*

---

### Prompt 20: 🌳 How to Plant a Garden (Informational/How-To)
**Write a how-to guide explaining the steps to plant a small garden. Include what supplies you need, the steps in order, and tips for success.**

*Graphic Organizer: Numbered steps outline with materials list*

## Self-Editing Checklist

**Before you turn in your writing, check:**

### Content ✍️
- [ ] I answered the prompt completely
- [ ] I have a clear beginning, middle, and end (narrative) OR introduction, body, conclusion (informational/opinion)
- [ ] I used specific details and examples
- [ ] My ideas make sense and are in order

### Organization 📋
- [ ] I indented my paragraphs
- [ ] I used transition words (first, next, then, also, finally, however, in addition)
- [ ] My ideas flow smoothly from one to the next

### Language & Style 🎨
- [ ] I used interesting vocabulary (not just "good," "nice," "fun")
- [ ] I varied my sentence beginnings
- [ ] I wrote in complete sentences

### Conventions ✅
- [ ] I capitalized the first word of each sentence and proper nouns
- [ ] I used correct end punctuation (. ? !)
- [ ] I checked my spelling
- [ ] My handwriting (or typing) is neat and readable
