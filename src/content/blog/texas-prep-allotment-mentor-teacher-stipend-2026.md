> **General information, not legal advice.** This post summarizes state funding guidance. Your district decides whether it takes part and how it runs the program. Read the official TEA guidance linked below and ask your district if you have questions.

On October 26, 2026, Governor Abbott announced $40 million for 411 Texas school systems through the Preparing and Retaining Educators through Partnerships (PREP) Program Allotment, to strengthen teacher recruitment, preparation and mentorship ([Office of the Texas Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40-million-to-strengthen-the-texas-teacher-workforce)). The money was already moving by then. TEA posted Version 2 of the PREP Allotment Guidebook on October 23, 2026, and PREP funds began flowing through the Foundation School Program that same day ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf), [TEA PREP newsletter, Oct 20, 2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXTEA/bulletins/42fae8b)).

PREP is a funding program, not a new classroom rule; the law creating it (TEC §48.157) took effect September 1, 2026, and there is no new teacher compliance deadline. Participation is optional for districts ([Dallas Express](https://dallasexpress.com/education/texas-announces-40-million-to-support-teacher-preparation-and-mentoring/)).

## What the PREP Allotment is

PREP was created by House Bill 2, 89th Texas Legislature, signed in June 2025. HB 2 provided $8.5 billion in new public education funding ([Office of the Texas Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40-million-to-strengthen-the-texas-teacher-workforce)). If you're looking for the HB 2 teacher pay raise, that's a different part of the bill. We cover it in [our HB 2 teacher pay raise guide](/blog/texas-hb2-teacher-pay-raise-2025).

PREP funds three programs: teacher residencies, Grow Your Own, and beginning-teacher mentorship. As of September 11, 8,260 participants were reported, a snapshot that may change ([Dallas Express](https://dallasexpress.com/education/texas-announces-40-million-to-support-teacher-preparation-and-mentoring/), [TEA PREP newsletter, Oct 20, 2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXTEA/bulletins/42fae8b)). HB 2 also repealed the old Mentor Program Allotment (TEC §48.114) effective September 1, 2026 ([Texas Register](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/October302026/Adopted%20Rules/19.EDUCATION.html)).

Commissioner Mike Morath said: "Strong teachers are the foundation of student success, and the PREP Allotment gives Texas school systems the resources to build a stronger educator pipeline" ([Office of the Texas Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40-million-to-strengthen-the-texas-teacher-workforce)).

## What it pays classroom teachers

- **Mentor teachers:** the district receives $3,000 a year for each mentored first- or second-year teacher, up to 40, and must pay the mentor at least $1,000 for each PREP beginning teacher assigned if the mentor is a teacher of record; full-time mentors and other mentors (such as instructional coaches) get a minimum stipend of $1,000 a year ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).
- **Host teachers:** at least $2,000 per resident ([TEA PREP Guidebook](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/educator-initiatives/prep-guidebook_0.pdf)).
- **Residents:** at least $20,000 in pay, with at least $10,000 from the allotment ([TEA PREP Guidebook Addendum](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/educator-initiatives/prep-guidebook-addendum.pdf)).

TEA also reports that the U.S. Department of Education clarified federal funds may be used for the $10,000 Residency Preservice match, reversing TEA's summer 2026 guidance ([TEA PREP newsletter, Oct 20, 2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXTEA/bulletins/42fae8b)).

## Mentor rules: caseloads and training

The number of beginning teachers you can mentor depends on your role ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)):

| Mentor's role | Max beginning teachers |
|---|---|
| Teacher of record, 4+ hours a day | 2 |
| Teacher of record, under 4 hours a day | 4 |
| Full-time mentor | 15 |
| Neither of the above | 8 |

Mentors who support PREP-funded teachers must complete Texas Mentorship Training (TMT). The education service center cost is $500 per participant per pathway, and it can be paid from PREP funds ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).

Got your mentee after school started? If you were assigned before February 1, you still get the full $1,000. You'll complete missed TMT modules asynchronously, with an artifact for each module, all checks, and at least 85% on module assessments ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).

Our [T-TESS observation tips](/blog/ttess-observation-tips-tia) can help, and the [T-TESS Observation Prep Kit](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/T-TESS-Observation-Prep-Kit-Plan-for-Distinguished-PrePost-Templates-15593390) gives you pre/post observation templates to work through together.

## Residencies and Grow Your Own

Residencies pair a resident with a host teacher, using the stipends above. Grow Your Own helps school employees who don't yet hold a bachelor's degree or teaching certificate, and high school students, prepare to teach ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf), [TEA LASO Cycle 2026-2027](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/superintendents/laso-cycle-2026-2027-pdf-published-october-22-2026-1-0.pdf), [Dallas Express](https://dallasexpress.com/education/texas-announces-40-million-to-support-teacher-preparation-and-mentoring/)).

The rules behind these programs are 19 TAC §153.1303(g), §153.1304(f) and §153.1305(d), and TEC §21.904, §21.906 and §21.907 ([TEA PREP Guidebook Addendum](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/educator-initiatives/prep-guidebook-addendum.pdf)).

## What to do now

1. Ask your campus or district if it's one of the 411 PREP school systems. The Governor's release points to the list on TEA's PREP Allotment page ([Office of the Texas Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40-million-to-strengthen-the-texas-teacher-workforce)).
2. Ask whether you qualify as a mentor or host teacher, and how many beginning teachers you'd be assigned under the caps above ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).
3. If you'll mentor, ask when your Texas Mentorship Training is scheduled ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).
4. If your district isn't in yet, share the next deadline with your principal: LASO Cycle 5 PREP applications close **December 1, 2026 at 5pm CT**, with awards estimated for February 2027 ([TEA LASO Cycle 2026-2027](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/superintendents/laso-cycle-2026-2027-pdf-published-october-22-2026-1-0.pdf)).

Working toward a TIA designation as well? Start with our [TIA complete guide](/blog/tia-complete-guide) or the free [TIA Quick Reference](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/Product/TIA-Quick-Reference-Texas-Teacher-Incentive-Allotment-FREE-2026-27-Guide-15593331).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Texas PREP Allotment?

It's state funding created by HB 2 (89th Legislature) for teacher residencies, Grow Your Own and beginning-teacher mentorship. In October 2026, $40 million went to 411 school systems ([Office of the Texas Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40-million-to-strengthen-the-texas-teacher-workforce)).

### How much do mentor teachers get paid under PREP?

At least $1,000 for each PREP beginning teacher assigned if you're a teacher of record; full-time mentors and other mentors (such as instructional coaches) get a minimum stipend of $1,000 a year ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).

### How much do host teachers and residents get?

Host teachers get at least $2,000 per resident. Residents get at least $20,000, with at least $10,000 from the allotment ([TEA PREP Guidebook Addendum](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/educator-initiatives/prep-guidebook-addendum.pdf)).

### Do I need training to be a PREP mentor?

Yes. Mentors supporting PREP-funded teachers must complete Texas Mentorship Training ([TEA PREP Guidebook v2](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/educator-initiatives-and-performance/prep-guidebook-v2.pdf)).

### When is the next PREP application deadline?

District applications for LASO Cycle 5 close December 1, 2026 at 5pm CT ([TEA LASO Cycle 2026-2027](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/superintendents/laso-cycle-2026-2027-pdf-published-october-22-2026-1-0.pdf)).
