## 👕 Jersey Name Tags

**Design:**
- Shaped like a jersey/T-shirt (or rectangular with jersey graphic)
- **Front:** Student name in large text, jersey number below (use room number + seat number, e.g., "204-15")
- **Team:** [Table group team name]
- **Position:** [Student's strongest subject — they choose]
- School colors as background
- Tent-fold for desk display

---

## 📖 Daily Playbook Packets

**Format for each day's packet:**
- **Cover:** "GAME PLAN — [Day/Round Name]" with date, student name line
- **"Scouting Report" header** explaining the day's objective in sports language
- STAAR-format questions (teacher provides content)
- **"Post-Game Notes"** section at the back: 3 reflection lines
  1. "Strongest play today (what I did well):"
  2. "Flag on the play (what tripped me up):"
  3. "Game plan for tomorrow:"

---

## 🃏 Strategy Trading Cards

**Description:** Students create test-taking strategy cards in a trading card format. Create throughout the week, trade to collect the set.

**Card format (index card):**
- **Front:**
  - Strategy name in bold (e.g., "THE ELIMINATOR")
  - Illustration/icon
  - "Rarity" level: Common ⭐, Rare ⭐⭐, Legendary ⭐⭐⭐
- **Back:**
  - "How to use this play:" (2-3 sentences)
  - "Best used when:" (1 sentence)
  - "Example:" (brief example)
  - Created by: [student name]

**10 Strategy Cards to create (assign 3 per student, trade to collect):**
1. The Eliminator (cross out wrong answers)
2. The Evidence Hunter (find proof in the text)
3. The Re-Reader (read the question twice)
4. The Show-Your-Worker (write out all steps)
5. The Time Manager (pace yourself)
6. The Double Checker (review before submitting)
7. The Key Word Finder (circle important words)
8. The Context Clue Detective (figure out unknown words)
9. The Reasonableness Checker (does my answer make sense?)
10. The Skip & Return (skip hard questions, come back later)

## 🏅 MVP Award System

**How it works:** Each day, teacher selects 1-3 students as "Daily MVP" for exceptional effort, improvement, or teamwork.

**MVP Award Card:**
- Size: 5" x 7"
- Header: "⭐ DAILY MVP AWARD ⭐"
- "Awarded to: [Name]"
- "For: [Specific reason — e.g., 'Outstanding effort on the Semifinal Playbook']"
- "Date: [Date]"
- "Signed: Coach [Teacher Name]"
- Sports graphic border

**Display:** Post MVP names on the class scoreboard. Students keep their award cards.

**Alternative (budget):** Simply announce MVPs and add a star next to their name on the scoreboard. Write names on the whiteboard in a "Hall of Fame" section.

## 📊 Scouting Report (Self-Assessment)

**Used on:** Monday (beginning of week) and/or Thursday (after scrimmage)

**Design:**
- Title: "PLAYER SCOUTING REPORT"
- **Player:** [Name]
- **Team:** [Table group]
- **Offensive Stats (Strengths):**
  - Skill 1: _________ Confidence: ★★★★★ (circle)
  - Skill 2: _________ Confidence: ★★★★★
  - Skill 3: _________ Confidence: ★★★★★
- **Defensive Stats (Needs Work):**
  - Skill 1: _________ Plan to improve: _________
  - Skill 2: _________ Plan to improve: _________
- **Season Prediction:** "I will do well on STAAR because: _________"
- **Coach's Notes:** (space for teacher comments)

## 📐 Foam Finger Printable

**Description:** A fun, printable "#1" foam finger outline. Students color it, write their name, and display it on their desk or locker.

**Design:**
- Classic foam finger shape (index finger pointing up)
- Text: "#1 STAAR ATHLETE"
- Space for student to write their name and team
- Print on cardstock, students color with school colors
- Optional: tape to a popsicle stick for "cheering" during review games

---

## 🏆 Championship Certificate

**Description:** End-of-week certificate for every student. Awarded Friday.

**Design:**
- Size: 8.5" x 11" landscape
- Header: Trophy graphic + "CHAMPIONSHIP WEEK"
- Title: "STAAR CHAMPION CERTIFICATE"
- Body: "This certifies that **[Name]** has completed Championship Week training and is officially a STAAR Champion. This athlete demonstrated dedication, teamwork, and the heart of a champion."
- Signature: "Coach [Teacher Name]"
- Date line
- Border: school colors, sports graphics (footballs, trophies, stars)

---

## 📰 Championship Program Page (Friday Keepsake)

**Description:** Like a game-day program. Students sign each other's copies.

**Design:**
- Title: "CHAMPIONSHIP WEEK PROGRAM — [Year]"
- **"Starting Lineup"** — space for 5-6 friend names
- **"My Coach:"** [Teacher name]
- **"Season Highlights:"** "My best moment this week was..."
- **"My Game Day Strategy:"** one sentence
- **"Autographs:"** large open space for classmates to sign
- Small space: "Draw your championship trophy"
