## 📊 TIA Designation Levels & Pay

| | **Recognized** | **Exemplary** | **Master** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annual Stipend** | $3,000 – $9,000 | $6,000 – $18,000 | $12,000 – $32,000 |
| **Rural/Title I Bonus** | Up to $6,000 extra | Up to $12,000 extra | Up to $20,000 extra |
| **Typical Total** | $3K – $15K/yr | $6K – $30K/yr | $12K – $52K/yr |
| **Difficulty** | Achievable for strong teachers | Highly effective teachers | Top-tier performance |
| **Validity** | 5 years (renewable) | 5 years (renewable) | 5 years (renewable) |

> 💡 **Key Insight:** Teachers at high-needs campuses earn significantly more. Rural and Title I schools unlock the highest allotments.

## 🏛️ The Two Pillars of TIA

TIA designations are based on **two weighted components**:

### Pillar 1: Student Growth (min. 35% weight)
- Based on **student performance data** (STAAR, district assessments, or comparable measures)
- Must demonstrate that your students are growing — not just proficient
- Districts choose which assessments count

### Pillar 2: Teacher Observation (min. 35% weight)
- Scored on the **T-TESS rubric** (or approved alternative)
- Requires calibrated, trained appraisers
- Multiple observations per year for reliability
- Focus areas: Planning, Instruction, Learning Environment, Professionalism

<div class="info-box">

**⚖️ Weighting:** Each pillar must be at least 35%. Your district sets the exact split (commonly 50/50 or 45/55). The remaining weight can go to either pillar.

</div>

## 📅 Key Dates in the TIA Cycle

| **When** | **What Happens** |
|---|---|
| **Aug – Sep** | District submits/updates TIA system application to TEA |
| **Sep – May** | Observation windows & student assessment data collection |
| **Jan – Mar** | Mid-year check-in — review your observation scores |
| **May – Jun** | Final observation scores & student growth data compiled |
| **Jun – Aug** | District submits teacher data to TEA for designation |
| **Sep – Nov** | TEA reviews and issues designation decisions |
| **Dec – Jan** | Designated teachers begin receiving allotment payments |

> ⏰ **Don't wait until spring!** Start collecting evidence and requesting observations early in the school year.

## ✅ 5 Quick-Start Action Items

<div class="action-box">

**Starting your TIA journey? Do these five things NOW:**

1. **🔍 Check your district's TIA status** — Use the TEA link above. If approved, find out your district's specific TIA plan and weighting.

2. **📋 Know your numbers** — Get your most recent T-TESS observation scores and any available student growth data. You need a baseline.

3. **📁 Start an evidence folder** — Create a digital folder TODAY. Save lesson plans, student work samples, observation feedback, PD certificates, and anything showing your impact.

4. **🤝 Talk to your appraiser** — Ask which observation rubric domains matter most for TIA in your district. Request a pre-observation conference.

5. **📈 Track student growth** — Identify 2-3 data points you can use to show student progress over time. Pre/post assessments are gold.

</div>
