# 🌮 Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready — Quick-Start Overview

## Welcome, Chef!

You've downloaded **Taco 'Bout STAAR Ready** — a fiesta/food-themed STAAR prep week stuffed with puns, flavor, and serious review. Your students are building "recipes for success," and by Friday, they're fully cooked and STAAR ready.

---

## 📦 What's in This Package

| File | What It Is |
|------|-----------|
| **LESSON-PLANS.md** | 5-day lesson plans loaded with food puns |
| **ACTIVITIES.md** | 10+ fiesta-themed activities and review games |
| **DECORATIONS.md** | Classroom fiesta setup + hallway banner |
| **STUDENT-MATERIALS.md** | Recipe cards, Taco Champion certificates, bonus challenges |
| **PARENT-LETTER.md** | Family communication template |

---

## 🌮 The Theme at a Glance

- **Students are:** Chefs building their "recipe for STAAR success"
- **Teacher is:** Head Chef / Executive Chef
- **Each day:** A different course in the meal
- **Completing work earns:** Ingredient stamps on their "Recipe Card"
- **STAAR test is:** The Big Fiesta
- **Classroom becomes:** STAAR Kitchen / Fiesta HQ
- **Difficulty levels:** 🌶️ Mild, 🌶️🌶️ Medium, 🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy

## 📅 Week Overview

| Day | Course | Focus |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Monday | **Appetizers** | ELA — setting the foundation, light and essential |
| Tuesday | **Main Course** | Math — the hearty, filling center of the meal |
| Wednesday | **Nacho Average Review Day** | Science/SS — special and unexpected |
| Thursday | **Taste Test** | Mixed practice — sampling everything |
| Friday | **Fiesta Friday** | Strategies, celebration, certificates |

## ⏰ Quick Setup
1. Print recipe cards and taco-themed materials
2. Make a paper plate taco for your door (10 min craft)
3. Post the fiesta banner and "Daily Specials" menu board
4. Send parent letter
5. Bring the puns. ALL the puns. 🌮
