## Section 1: TIA Overview

### What Is the Teacher Incentive Allotment?

The **Teacher Incentive Allotment (TIA)** is a Texas state program (House Bill 3, 2019) that allows districts to provide **permanent salary increases** to teachers designated as **Recognized, Exemplary, or Master**.

Unlike bonuses, TIA designations are:
- **Portable** — they follow you to any participating district
- **Permanent** — as long as you maintain the designation (every 5 years)
- **Funded by the state**

### Designation Levels & Potential Salary Increases

| Designation | Approximate Annual Increase |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| **Recognized** | $3,000 – $9,000 |
| **Exemplary** | $6,000 – $18,000 |
| **Master** | $9,000 – $32,000 |

*Actual amounts vary by district. Rural and high-needs campuses receive higher allotments.*

## Section 2: The Two Pillars

### 🏛️ Pillar 1: Teacher Observation (Qualitative)

Based on **T-TESS** observation scores:

| Designation | Typical T-TESS Requirement |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Recognized | Average of "Accomplished" across observed domains |
| Exemplary | Average of "Accomplished" with some "Distinguished" |
| Master | Average of "Distinguished" across observed domains |

### 🏛️ Pillar 2: Student Growth (Quantitative)

| Designation | Typical Growth Requirement |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Recognized | Above-average (55th+ percentile) |
| Exemplary | Well above average (60th–70th+) |
| Master | Top-tier (75th+ percentile) |

> ⚠️ You need strong performance in **BOTH** pillars.

## Section 3: Building Your Portfolio (Sample)

### Step 1: Know Your District's LDS (August)

- [ ] Obtain your district's TEA-approved Local Designation System document
- [ ] Identify criteria for each designation level
- [ ] Note the observation rubric (T-TESS or alternative)
- [ ] Understand how student growth is measured
- [ ] Identify deadlines for data submission
- [ ] Ask your administrator: "What evidence would be most helpful?"

### Step 2: Establish Baseline Data (August–September)

- [ ] Administer pre-assessments for all units/courses
- [ ] Document pre-assessment data in a tracking system
- [ ] Set Student Learning Objectives (SLOs) if required
- [ ] Photograph/scan baseline student work samples
