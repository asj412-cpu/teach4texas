### Post 17 — STAAR Strategy
**Platform:** Facebook / TikTok
**Best Time:** Tuesday, 7 PM

**Post Text:**
Stop telling students to "go back and check your work" on STAAR. 🙅‍♀️

Instead, teach them the "Cover and Solve" method:

1. Read the question
2. Cover the answer choices
3. Solve it or form your answer FIRST
4. THEN look at the choices

Why? Because wrong answer choices on STAAR are designed to look right. They're based on common mistakes.

If students look at choices first, their brain latches onto the "attractive distractor."

If they solve first, they're looking for THEIR answer — not falling for traps.

Game changer for STAAR multiple choice. Try it Monday. 🧠
