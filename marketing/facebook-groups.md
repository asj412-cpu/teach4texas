# Top Texas Teacher Facebook Groups for Marketing
*Created: February 19, 2026*

## How to Find These Groups
Search each name on Facebook. Some are private (request to join). Use a personal profile, not a business page — groups don't allow page posts.

## General Rules for All Groups
- **Don't spam.** Join 2-3 per week max to avoid Facebook flagging you.
- **Lurk first** — spend 1-2 weeks commenting helpfully before sharing links.
- **80/20 rule:** 80% value (answering questions, tips), 20% promotion.
- **Never drop a TPT link without context.** Always frame it as helping solve a problem.

## Tier 1: Large Texas Teacher Groups (High Priority)

### 1. Texas Teachers (various iterations)
- **Search:** "Texas Teachers" on Facebook
- **Est. Members:** 50,000–100,000+
- **Notes:** Multiple groups with this name. Join the largest active one. Very general — good for TIA awareness posts since many members don't know about TIA yet.
- **Strategy:** Share TIA explainer content ("Did you know you could earn $3K–$32K extra?"). Link to blog posts. Answer TIA questions when they come up.
- **Best Posts:** Blog links, infographics, free TIA Quick Reference Card

### 2. Texas Elementary Teachers
- **Search:** "Texas Elementary Teachers"
- **Est. Members:** 20,000–50,000
- **Notes:** Focused on K–5. High engagement on resource sharing days. Many groups have "Freebie Friday" or "Share Saturday" threads.
- **Strategy:** Share STAAR Math and RLA lesson previews. Offer the free TIA Quick Reference Card.
- **Best Posts:** STAAR tips, free resources, Student Growth Tracker preview

### 3. STAAR Test Prep for Teachers
- **Search:** "STAAR test prep teachers" or "STAAR review"
- **Est. Members:** 10,000–30,000
- **Notes:** Peak activity January–April (testing season). Members actively looking for resources.
- **Strategy:** Share STAAR prep tips freely, then mention your lesson bundles. This is your highest-conversion audience.
- **Best Posts:** Quick STAAR tips with "I made a full lesson bundle for this" soft sell

### 4. Texas Teachers Chat / Texas Teachers Lounge
- **Search:** "Texas Teachers Chat" or "Texas Teachers Lounge"
- **Est. Members:** 15,000–40,000
- **Notes:** More casual/vent-friendly. Good for building relationships. Often discuss policy, pay, burnout.
- **Strategy:** When TIA/pay discussions come up organically, drop knowledge. Be the "TIA expert" people tag.
- **Best Posts:** TIA money facts, empathetic responses + helpful resources

### 5. Texas Teacher Talk
- **Search:** "Texas Teacher Talk"
- **Est. Members:** 10,000–25,000
- **Notes:** Mix of discussion and resource sharing.
- **Strategy:** Similar to above — be helpful, build authority, share blog content.

## Tier 2: Subject/Grade-Specific Groups

### 6. Texas Math Teachers / STAAR Math Prep
- **Search:** "Texas math teachers" or "STAAR math"
- **Est. Members:** 5,000–15,000
- **Notes:** Highly targeted for STAAR Math products. Members share strategies and resources.
- **Strategy:** Share math tips from your lesson plans. "Here's how I teach [concept] in a fun way" → link to product.
- **Best Posts:** Math strategy tips, hands-on activity previews

### 7. Texas Reading / RLA Teachers
- **Search:** "Texas reading teachers" or "STAAR RLA" or "Texas ELA teachers"
- **Est. Members:** 5,000–15,000
- **Notes:** Good for STAAR RLA product. Constructed response is a hot topic.
- **Strategy:** Share writing/reading tips. Constructed response strategies are gold here.
- **Best Posts:** RLA tips, writing strategies, constructed response examples

### 8. Texas Science Teachers / STAAR Science
- **Search:** "Texas science teachers" or "STAAR science"
- **Est. Members:** 5,000–10,000
- **Notes:** Smaller but very engaged. 5th and 8th grade science teachers preparing for STAAR.
- **Strategy:** Share hands-on lab ideas from your Science lesson plans.
- **Best Posts:** Lab activity previews, science vocabulary strategies

### 9. 3rd Grade Texas Teachers / 4th Grade Texas Teachers / 5th Grade Texas Teachers
- **Search:** "[grade] grade Texas teachers"
- **Est. Members:** 3,000–10,000 each
- **Notes:** Grade-specific groups are VERY targeted. Members share exactly what they need.
- **Strategy:** Tailor content to grade. Share specific TEKS-aligned activities.
- **Best Posts:** Grade-specific STAAR resources, test prep timelines

## Tier 3: TIA & T-TESS Specific

### 10. Teacher Incentive Allotment (TIA) - Texas
- **Search:** "Teacher Incentive Allotment" or "TIA Texas teachers"
- **Est. Members:** 2,000–8,000
- **Notes:** Your EXACT audience. Smaller but highest intent. Members actively pursuing TIA.
- **Strategy:** Be the authority. Answer every question. Share blog posts generously. Soft-promote TIA Portfolio Guide and T-TESS Prep Kit.
- **Best Posts:** TIA tips, designation level breakdowns, success stories, portfolio guide

### 11. T-TESS Support / T-TESS Teachers
- **Search:** "T-TESS" on Facebook
- **Est. Members:** 3,000–8,000
- **Notes:** Teachers prepping for observations. Anxiety-driven audience — they want help NOW.
- **Strategy:** Share observation tips from your blog post. Promote T-TESS Prep Kit.
- **Best Posts:** Domain-by-domain tips, observation prep checklists, "what appraisers look for"

## Posting Schedule

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Monday | Comment/engage in 3-4 groups (no links) |
| Tuesday | Share a tip or blog post in 1-2 groups |
| Wednesday | Comment/engage in 3-4 groups |
| Thursday | Share a freebie or resource preview in 1-2 groups |
| Friday | "Freebie Friday" posts where allowed — share TIA Quick Reference Card |
| Saturday | TPT seller group promo days |
| Sunday | Plan next week's group activity |

## Key Timing
- **January–April:** STAAR prep season = highest engagement for STAAR products
- **August–September:** Back to school = T-TESS and new teacher content
- **October–November:** Observation season = T-TESS Prep Kit push
- **Year-round:** TIA content (designations announced in spring, but interest is constant)
